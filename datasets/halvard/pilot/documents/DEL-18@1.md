Cleaning gel pouch notice

Stop using cleaning gel pouches from batch 1190 with the Lumen smart thermostat, and return them for a refund.

Halvard Home is recalling these pouches because they may leave residue that affects the thermostat’s controls. Please keep the pouches sealed and away from children and pets. Do not apply the gel to your thermostat or try to wash it off with water. If gel has already been used, contact Halvard Home customer support for advice before attempting further cleaning. To arrange your refund, contact our support team or take the pouches back to the retailer where you bought them. We apologise for the inconvenience.
