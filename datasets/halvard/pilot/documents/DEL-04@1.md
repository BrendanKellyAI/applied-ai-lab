Vale outdoor fan grille notice

Stop using outdoor fan grilles from batch 6404 with the Vale fridge freezer, and return them for a refund.

Please check the batch details on the grille label or its packaging. If you cannot find these details, contact Halvard Home customer support for help identifying your grille.

Do not attempt to alter or repair an affected grille. If you are unsure how to remove it safely, contact our support team before attempting removal.

Keep the grille and any packaging available when arranging your return. Our support team can explain the return process and help with your refund.

We apologise for the inconvenience.
