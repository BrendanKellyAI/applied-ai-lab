# Returns and refunds

## General terms

### Faulty appliances

If your appliance appears to be faulty, please contact Halvard Home so we can help. Tell us what has gone wrong and provide proof of purchase, such as your receipt or order confirmation. If you cannot find this, let us know so we can discuss how to confirm your purchase.

An appliance that develops a fault within 117 days of delivery can be returned for a full refund. This applies where the fault is not caused by accidental damage, misuse or failure to follow the care instructions. We will explain how to arrange the return and what information we need to process your request.

Your legal rights may also entitle you to a repair, replacement or refund outside this return entitlement. The remedy available will depend on the circumstances. Nothing in this policy limits your rights under consumer law, and you do not need a separate guarantee to ask us to resolve a fault.

Please stop using the appliance if you think it may be unsafe. Do not attempt a repair or remove protective covers to investigate the problem. Describe any warning signs when you contact us, including unusual smells, sounds or visible damage. We will advise you on safe handling and suitable return arrangements.

We may ask you to describe how the fault occurs or share photographs if these would help us understand the problem. You should not put yourself at risk to gather evidence. If the fault is intermittent or cannot be shown in a photograph, explain what you have noticed so our team can take this into account.

We will arrange a suitable way to return the faulty appliance at our expense. Please follow the packing and collection instructions we provide. Original packaging is helpful but is not required. If you cannot pack or move the appliance safely, tell us so we can discuss suitable assistance.

Before handing over the appliance, remove personal belongings and follow any guidance we give about preparing it for transport. Where relevant, sign out of connected accounts and remove personal information if you can do so safely. Let us know if the fault prevents this.

Returned faulty appliances are tested within 19 working days of arrival. Our assessment considers the fault you reported and the condition of the appliance. We will tell you the outcome and explain the proposed resolution. If we cannot reproduce the fault, we will discuss our findings with you rather than assume that no problem exists.

Where a refund is due, we will normally return it through the payment method used for the purchase. If this is not possible, we will agree a suitable alternative with you. If you disagree with our assessment or how your return has been handled, please contact our customer support team and ask for a review.

## Tessa dishwasher

### Returning the appliance

If you would like to return your Tessa dishwasher, contact Halvard Home customer support with your order details and the reason for your return. Tell us whether the appliance has been unpacked, installed or used, and whether you are requesting a refund or an exchange.

We will explain the return arrangements and confirm what you need to do before collection. Please do not send the appliance to us without contacting us, as we need to link the return to your order and arrange suitable handling.

For a change of mind return, the following charge applies where permitted by law. A restocking charge of 35% of the price applies once the packaging is opened. We will explain any applicable deduction before you agree to proceed.

Collection of a returned Tessa dishwasher costs £55.00. We will confirm whether this charge applies to your return and how it will be paid. If the appliance is faulty, damaged or not as described, contact us so we can explain your rights and the appropriate arrangements.

A returns label for the Tessa dishwasher is emailed within 1 hour of the request. Check your junk folder if you cannot find the email, and contact customer support if it has not arrived. Follow the instructions in the email when attaching the label.

Before collection, empty the appliance and make sure it is clean and drained. Disconnect it from the electricity and water supplies safely, following the supplied instructions. If you are unsure how to disconnect it, seek help from a suitably qualified professional.

Use the original packaging if you still have it. If the packaging is missing or damaged, tell us before preparing the return so we can advise you. Secure loose parts and include the accessories supplied with the appliance.

Keep the appliance somewhere dry and accessible until it is collected. Tell us about stairs, narrow access or anything else that may affect safe removal. Do not leave it outside or somewhere that could expose it to damage.

An exchange for a different colour must be requested within 141 days. Let customer support know which colour you would prefer. Exchanges depend on availability, and we will confirm any price difference and return charges before you decide whether to proceed.

When the appliance reaches us, we will check its condition and the contents of the return. If anything is missing or its condition differs from what you described, we will contact you to explain how this affects your request.

Approved refunds are normally returned to the original payment method. We will confirm the refund and explain any lawful deductions. Keep your collection confirmation and return correspondence until your refund or exchange has been resolved.

Nothing in this policy limits your statutory rights. Charges for change of mind returns do not replace the remedies available when an appliance is faulty, damaged or not as described.

## Lumen smart thermostat

### Returning the appliance

If you would like to return your Lumen smart thermostat, contact Halvard Home customer support before sending it back. Tell us why you wish to return it and whether you would prefer a refund or an exchange. Have your order confirmation available so that we can find your purchase and explain the arrangements that apply.

An unopened Lumen smart thermostat is refunded in full, but opening the box changes that. A restocking charge of 21% of the price applies once the packaging is opened.

These conditions apply to returns because you have changed your mind, except where your statutory cancellation rights require different treatment. They do not limit your rights if the appliance is faulty, damaged, not as described or otherwise does not meet your legal rights as a consumer.

Please explain whether the appliance has been unpacked, installed or used. This helps us give you suitable advice and arrange its return safely. If you believe it is faulty, describe the problem rather than attempting a repair. We may ask for photographs where these would help us understand its condition.

Do not remove an installed thermostat unless you can do so safely. Where removal involves electrical wiring, seek help from a suitably qualified professional. Make sure your heating system is left safe and follow the appliance instructions when preparing the thermostat for return.

Before packing the appliance, remove your personal settings and disconnect it from your account using the instructions provided with it. Contact customer support if you cannot complete this process. Do not include account passwords or other sensitive information in the parcel.

Pack the thermostat securely, preferably in its original packaging, and include the accessories supplied with it. Tell us if anything is missing or damaged. Please avoid attaching labels or tape directly to the appliance, and use protective outer packaging to help prevent damage during transport.

Collection of a returned Lumen smart thermostat costs £85.00.

We will explain how any applicable collection charge will be paid before confirming the arrangement. If the return concerns a fault or another issue covered by your consumer rights, we will confirm the appropriate return method and any costs for which we are responsible.

An exchange for a Lumen smart thermostat in a different colour must be requested within 125 days.

Please tell customer support which colour you would prefer. Exchanges depend on availability. We will explain the available options before you send the appliance back, including what happens if your preferred colour is unavailable.

When the return arrives, we will check its condition and confirm the refund or exchange with you. We will explain any proposed deduction and the reason for it. Refunds are normally made using the original payment method. Keep your return confirmation and proof of handover until we have confirmed that the return is complete.

## Keel washing machine

### Packaging and condition

Keep the original packaging of the Keel washing machine for 60 days in case it needs to be returned. Store the carton, protective inserts and transport fittings somewhere dry and secure. Keeping these materials helps protect the appliance during collection and reduces the risk of damage in transit.

If the original packaging is missing or damaged, contact Halvard Home before preparing your return. We can explain what protection is suitable and help you agree arrangements for collection. Please do not assume that missing packaging means you cannot return a faulty appliance. Your legal rights are not affected by whether you have kept the packaging.

The Keel washing machine should be empty, drained and disconnected before it is collected. Follow the instructions supplied with the appliance, and ask for help if you are unsure how to disconnect it safely. Do not attempt work that could put you at risk or damage your plumbing or electrical supply.

Remove clothing, detergent and personal belongings from the drum, dispenser and surrounding areas. Wipe away detergent residue and accessible dirt, then leave the appliance dry. Please do not use harsh chemicals or abrasive materials to prepare it for return, as these may damage its surfaces or seals.

A returned Keel washing machine that needs cleaning is charged £45.00. This charge covers cleaning needed because the returned appliance contains avoidable dirt, residue or other soiling. It does not apply where cleaning is needed because of the fault you have reported. We will explain any proposed cleaning charge and how it affects your refund.

Pack any supplied accessories securely so they cannot move around or scratch the appliance. Tell us if an accessory is missing or damaged rather than replacing it with an unsuitable item. Do not place loose objects inside the drum or attach anything directly to a finished surface with strong adhesive tape.

Use the transport fittings as described in the appliance instructions. Protect exposed edges and keep hoses and cables secure without bending or crushing them. If you cannot prepare the appliance safely, tell us when arranging collection so we can discuss suitable assistance.

A returned Keel washing machine is inspected within 59 days of arrival. We check its condition, the accessories returned with it and any fault or damage you have reported. The inspection helps us establish whether its condition matches the information provided when the return was arranged.

Signs of installation or reasonable use do not automatically prevent a return. Any assessment will take account of the reason for returning the appliance and your legal rights. We will not treat damage caused by a reported fault as damage you have caused.

If we find damage or missing items that may affect your refund, we will explain our findings and give you an opportunity to respond. Photographs of the appliance and its packaging can help resolve questions about its condition. Please keep any collection confirmation until your return has been resolved.

Please do not send the appliance back without contacting us, as we need to arrange suitable handling and confirm where it should go.

Tell us whether the appliance has been unpacked, installed or used, and whether you still have its packaging. Please also mention any damage, missing parts or difficulty accessing your property. This helps us plan the return and avoid problems when the collection team arrives.

For a change of mind return, the appliance should be clean, undamaged and complete with its shelves, drawers, fittings and supplied documents. Keep the original packaging if you have it. If you no longer have the packaging, tell us so we can advise you on how to protect the appliance for transport.

We will explain any applicable deductions before you agree to the return.

An exchange for a Vale fridge freezer in a different colour must be requested within 41 days. This period starts when your appliance is delivered. Colour exchanges depend on availability. Contact customer support to discuss your preferred colour and confirm the arrangements before preparing your appliance for collection.

Our voluntary returns conditions do not replace or restrict those rights, and you do not need the original packaging to report a fault.
