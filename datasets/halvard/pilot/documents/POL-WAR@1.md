# Warranty

This policy explains the warranty support available for eligible Halvard Home purchases used in a private household. It is for customers who have bought directly from Halvard Home or through an authorised retailer and want to understand their cover, request help or make a claim.

The warranty covers faults caused by defective materials or workmanship under normal domestic use, subject to the terms set out in this policy. It explains how we assess claims and when we may offer a repair, replacement or alternative remedy.

The policy also describes what is not covered, including accidental damage, misuse, unauthorised alterations and ordinary wear and tear. It sets out your responsibilities for care and maintenance, the information we may need to support a claim and how to contact us.

This warranty is separate from your legal rights as a consumer and does not limit or replace them.

## General terms

### Registering your appliance

Register a new appliance within 25 days of delivery to activate the standard warranty. Registration helps Halvard Home link your appliance to your purchase details and check your cover if you need support. It also helps us contact you about important safety information relating to your appliance.

You can register through the warranty registration page on our website or ask our customer support team for help. Please use the contact details published on our website. You do not need to agree to receive marketing messages to register your appliance or activate your standard warranty.

Have your proof of purchase and delivery details ready when you register. We will also ask for your contact information and the appliance details shown on its rating label. Please copy these details carefully so that we can identify your appliance and record your registration correctly.

Your proof of purchase should clearly show the retailer, the appliance purchased and the purchase date. If the delivery date is different, please keep evidence of delivery as well. An order confirmation, receipt or delivery note may help us check the information you provide.

Keep your proof of purchase for at least 64 months after delivery. Store it somewhere safe and make sure any digital copy remains clear and readable. Registration does not replace your proof of purchase, and we may ask to see it when assessing a warranty claim.

Please check your registration confirmation and tell us if anything is wrong. If you do not receive confirmation, contact customer support so that we can check whether your registration has been received. Sending a registration request does not confirm that the details have been accepted.

If you cannot complete registration because information is missing, the website is unavailable or you need an accessible alternative, please contact us for assistance. Explain the difficulty and provide whatever purchase and delivery evidence you have. We will explain what is needed to complete your registration.

If you have missed the registration deadline, contact customer support to discuss your circumstances. We will review the information you provide and explain whether the standard warranty can be activated. Please do not assume that an incomplete or late registration has activated your cover.

Registering an appliance does not change the scope of the warranty. Any claim remains subject to the applicable warranty terms, including the conditions and exclusions described elsewhere in this policy. Please keep your registration confirmation with your purchase records so that they are easy to find.

We use registration information to administer warranty cover, provide support and communicate relevant safety notices. Our privacy notice explains how we handle personal information and how you can exercise your data protection rights. Please let us know if your contact details change.

The registration requirement applies to Halvard Home’s standard warranty. It does not affect your statutory rights, including any rights you may have against the retailer. You do not have to register with us to exercise those rights.

### Making a claim

If you believe you have a fault covered by your Halvard Home warranty, contact our customer support team by phone or through the contact options on our website. Explain what has happened and tell us that you wish to make a warranty claim.

Please provide your name, contact details and delivery address, together with a description of the item and the fault. Tell us where you bought it and provide proof of purchase. Clear photographs may help us understand the problem, but please do not put yourself at risk to take them.

Proof of purchase can include a receipt, an order confirmation or other evidence showing where and when you bought the item. If you cannot find this, contact us to discuss what alternative evidence we can accept. You do not need to keep the original packaging to make a claim.

Halvard Home replies to a warranty claim within 18 working days of receiving it. Our reply will explain whether we can assess your claim using the information provided or whether we need further details. It will also explain what you should do next.

A claim made by phone is logged within 58 hours. Please tell our adviser if you need us to contact you in a particular way or if you need support with the claims process.

Describe the fault as clearly as you can, including the circumstances in which it happens. Let us know about any damage, alterations or repairs that may be relevant. Giving us accurate information helps us assess your claim fairly and choose an appropriate way to investigate it.

If you suspect that the item is unsafe, stop using it and contact us for advice. Do not attempt a repair or dismantle it to investigate the fault. Follow the safety instructions supplied with it, where doing so is safe.

We may ask to inspect the item or arrange for it to be collected. Please wait for our instructions before sending anything to us. We will explain how to prepare it for transport and what to include. Tell us if you cannot safely move or package it.

We will assess your claim against the warranty terms and explain our decision in plain language. If the fault is covered, we will tell you what remedy is available and how it will be arranged. We will discuss any practical arrangements with you before proceeding.

If we decide that your claim is not covered, we will explain why and identify the relevant warranty terms. You can ask us to review the decision and provide further evidence. If you remain unhappy, you can use our complaints process.

Making a warranty claim does not affect your statutory rights. You may also have rights against the retailer that sold you the item, separately from this warranty.

### What the warranty does not cover

The warranty covers faults caused by defective materials or workmanship. It does not cover damage or faults caused by misuse, neglect, accidental damage or use that does not follow the instructions supplied by Halvard Home. We will consider the cause of the fault when assessing a claim.

Damage caused by incorrect installation is not covered. This includes unsuitable connections, inadequate ventilation or placement where the surrounding conditions do not meet the installation instructions. This exclusion does not apply where Halvard Home is responsible for the installation error.

The warranty does not cover faults caused by an unsuitable electricity supply, damaged household wiring or problems with plumbing or drainage outside the appliance. Damage caused by fire, flooding, lightning, pests or other external events is also excluded unless the underlying cause is a fault covered by the warranty.

You are responsible for routine cleaning and care in line with the supplied instructions. The warranty does not cover damage caused by a failure to carry out that care, including damage from blocked openings, accumulated dirt or unsuitable cleaning substances. Routine maintenance is not a warranty repair.

Normal wear from ordinary use is not covered unless it results from defective materials or workmanship. The warranty does not cover the replacement of consumable items simply because they have been used up. A manufacturing fault affecting such an item will still be assessed under the warranty.

Cosmetic damage must be reported within 15 days of delivery. Scratches, dents, marks and changes in appearance caused by handling, cleaning or use after delivery are not covered. This reporting requirement does not remove your statutory rights if goods arrive damaged or do not meet the standard required by law.

Faults caused by alterations, unsuitable replacement parts or repairs carried out incorrectly are excluded. Having work carried out by an independent repairer does not automatically invalidate the warranty. We may decline a claim where that work caused the fault or damage being claimed for.

The warranty does not cover damage caused by use in a business or other setting where the appliance is used outside its stated purpose. Claims will be assessed against the intended use described in the supplied instructions, rather than rejected solely because of where the appliance is located.

An inspection that finds misuse is charged at £65.00. We will explain the inspection arrangements and the circumstances in which this charge applies before you agree to a visit. If we decline a claim because of misuse, we will explain the findings supporting that decision.

The warranty does not provide compensation for inconvenience, loss of use or other indirect losses. However, these exclusions do not limit any liability that Halvard Home cannot lawfully exclude. Your statutory rights remain unaffected, including any right to seek a remedy from the retailer that sold you the goods.

## Brio kettle

### Cover period

The Brio kettle is covered for 109 months from the date of delivery. This cover applies to faults covered by the warranty and is subject to its terms. The delivery date, rather than the date you register the kettle or start using it, sets the start of the cover.

Keep your order confirmation, delivery record and proof of purchase somewhere safe. These documents help us confirm when your cover began and whether a repair request falls within it. If your records show different delivery dates, contact Halvard Home so that we can check the details with you.

If you cannot find your delivery record, please contact us with the purchase details you have. We will review the available evidence before confirming the start date. Registering your kettle helps us find your information, but registration does not change the delivery date used to calculate your cover.

Replacement parts fitted to the Brio kettle are covered for a further 17 months. This cover starts on the date the replacement part is fitted. It applies to the replacement part itself and does not restart or extend the warranty for the rest of the kettle. Keep the repair record as evidence of the fitting date.

A replacement part may therefore remain covered after the kettle’s original warranty has ended. In that case, cover is limited to the replacement part and remains subject to the warranty terms. A fault elsewhere in the kettle does not become covered simply because a covered replacement part has been fitted.

Repairs to the Brio kettle include labour only for an opening period. Labour for repairs is free for the first 28 months. This labour cover runs from the kettle’s delivery date. After it ends, a covered fault or replacement part may still qualify for support, but labour may be charged. We will explain any proposed charge before you agree to the work.

Arranging a repair does not restart the kettle’s cover. Please report a fault as soon as you notice it and keep the confirmation of your request. We assess whether the fault was reported within the applicable cover, so a delay in arranging an approved repair does not by itself remove that cover.

If the Brio kettle is sold on, the new owner must transfer the warranty within 26 days. This requirement runs from the date of sale. The new owner should contact Halvard Home with evidence of the sale and the original purchase and delivery details. The transfer does not restart the warranty or change its original expiry date.

These cover periods describe the Halvard Home warranty only. They do not limit your statutory rights. If you are unsure whether your kettle, a replacement part or repair labour is still covered, contact us with your delivery and repair records so that we can explain what applies.

### Repairs under warranty

If your Brio kettle develops a fault while it is under warranty, contact Halvard Home before arranging a repair. Describe what has happened and have your proof of purchase ready. We may ask about how the kettle has been used and request photographs to help us assess the fault.

Stop using the kettle if it leaks, gives off a burning smell or shows damage to its plug or cable. Unplug it if it is safe to do so. Do not open the casing or try to repair electrical components yourself.

We will explain whether the fault appears to be covered and how to make the kettle available for inspection. If we need you to send it to us, we will provide packing and collection instructions. Let the kettle cool, empty it and make sure it is dry before packing it.

Once we have inspected the kettle, we will confirm whether the repair is covered by the warranty. Covered repairs include the labour, parts and transport we arrange. If we find damage or a fault that is not covered, we will explain our findings and ask for your agreement before carrying out chargeable work.

A warranty repair to the Brio kettle is completed within 15 weeks of the fault being confirmed. We will tell you when the fault has been confirmed and keep you informed about the progress of the repair. Please let us know if your contact details change while the kettle is with us.

If a repair to the Brio kettle takes longer than 36 weeks, Halvard Home lends a replacement. The replacement remains our property and is provided for use while your kettle is being repaired. We will explain how it will reach you and arrange its return when your repaired kettle is available.

We may use new or refurbished parts where they are suitable for the repair. Refurbished parts are checked before use to make sure they meet our safety and performance requirements. A refurbished part is covered for 52 weeks. We will identify any refurbished part fitted and explain how to report a fault with it.

Before returning your kettle, we will check that the repair has addressed the confirmed fault and that the kettle operates safely. We will also provide a summary of the work carried out. If the same problem continues, contact us so that we can review the repair.

An engineer visit for the Brio kettle after the warranty has ended costs £105.00. We will explain what the visit includes and whether any separate repair charges would apply before you agree to an appointment.

Nothing in this repair process limits your statutory rights. You may also have rights against the retailer that sold you the kettle, regardless of whether a fault is covered by this warranty.
