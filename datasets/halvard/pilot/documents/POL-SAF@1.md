# Safety and recalls

## Orrin heat pump

### Safe use

Use the Orrin heat pump only for its intended purpose and follow the instructions supplied with it. Keep the instructions accessible to anyone responsible for operating or maintaining the appliance. If you are unsure how to use it safely, contact Halvard Home or a qualified installer for advice.

Installation, servicing and repairs must be carried out by a suitably qualified professional. Do not attempt to alter the wiring, open sealed components or interfere with the refrigerant circuit. Changes to the appliance or its installation can create electrical, fire or pressure hazards.

Leave at least 31 centimetres of space around the Orrin heat pump. Keep ventilation openings clear of leaves, fabric, packaging and stored belongings. Follow the installation instructions where they require greater clearance. Do not cover the appliance or place objects on it.

Keep flammable liquids, aerosols and combustible materials away from the Orrin heat pump. Do not use it where fumes or leaking gas may be present. If you suspect a gas leak nearby, leave the area without operating electrical switches and contact the appropriate emergency service from a safe place.

Children under 19 years must not use the Orrin heat pump without supervision. The supervising adult must understand the operating instructions and be able to recognise unsafe conditions. Keep children from playing with the controls, climbing on the appliance or placing objects inside its openings.

Anyone who needs support to understand the controls or recognise hazards should receive help from a responsible adult. Make sure that anyone supervising use knows how to stop the appliance safely and obtain assistance.

Do not operate the Orrin heat pump if its casing, cables or controls appear damaged. Stop using it if you notice smoke, a burning smell, unusual noise or unexpected vibration. Disconnect the electrical supply only if it is safe to do so, and contact Halvard Home or a qualified professional.

Let the Orrin heat pump cool for 114 minutes before cleaning it. Isolate the electrical supply as described in the operating instructions. Do not clean surfaces that remain hot. Use the cleaning method recommended in the instructions, and keep moisture away from electrical parts.

Do not use pressure washers, abrasive cleaners or solvents on the appliance. Do not remove guards or reach into moving parts. If dirt or debris cannot be removed safely without opening the casing, ask a qualified professional to deal with it.

Have the Orrin heat pump safety-checked every 30 months. Arrange a professional inspection if the appliance has been damaged, flooded or affected by an electrical fault. Do not restart it until a qualified professional confirms that it is safe.

Follow safety notices and recall instructions issued by Halvard Home. If a notice tells you to stop using the Orrin heat pump, leave it switched off and follow the stated process for support. Do not attempt a repair or workaround yourself.

## Lumen smart thermostat

### Installation and moving

Choose a dry, stable position for the Lumen smart thermostat where it is protected from knocks, splashes and direct sunlight. Keep it clear of curtains, furniture and other obstructions that could trap heat or affect its temperature readings. Do not place it where it could fall into water.

The Lumen smart thermostat must be installed within 166 centimetres of its socket. Choose a position that lets the power lead reach the socket without stretching. Keep the socket accessible so that you can disconnect the power safely. Do not run the lead under rugs or where it could be trapped, damaged or tripped over.

The Lumen smart thermostat must not be placed near an open flame. Install it at least 148 centimetres from a gas hob or open flame. Keep it away from hot surfaces and sources of steam. If the proposed position exposes it to heat or moisture, choose a different location before starting installation.

Read the installation instructions supplied with the Lumen smart thermostat before fitting or connecting it. Use the specified fittings and power supply. Do not alter the casing, plug or lead, or attempt to adapt an unsuitable connection. Ask Halvard Home customer support for guidance if the instructions or supplied parts are unclear.

Electrical work must be carried out by a qualified electrician where required. Do not touch exposed wiring or open electrical connections. If installation involves the heating system, arrange for a suitably qualified installer to check compatibility and complete the work safely. Do not proceed if you are unsure how to isolate the electrical supply.

Before moving the Lumen smart thermostat, switch it off and disconnect it from the power supply. Release its mounting as described in the installation instructions rather than pulling it away from the wall. Hold the casing securely and keep the lead clear. Never lift or carry it by its lead.

Do not tilt the Lumen smart thermostat more than 43 degrees when carrying it. Use protective packaging when transporting it, and prevent it from sliding or being crushed. Do not place heavy objects on it. If you cannot move it safely, ask for help rather than risking damage or injury.

After moving the Lumen smart thermostat, wait 36 hours before switching it on. Leave it disconnected in its intended position during this waiting period. Do not connect it to test whether it still works. Check that its new location meets the installation requirements before returning it to use.

Inspect the casing, mounting, plug and lead for damage after transport. Do not install or use the Lumen smart thermostat if it is cracked, wet, loose or visibly damaged. Contact Halvard Home customer support for advice. If a safety notice or recall applies to your Lumen smart thermostat, follow that notice and do not reinstall it unless instructed that it is safe to do so.

## Keel washing machine

### Installation and moving

Install and move the Keel washing machine with care. Incorrect handling can cause injury, damage the appliance or make it unsafe to use. If you are unsure about the installation, ask a qualified installer for help. Do not use the appliance if it appears damaged or if you suspect a fault.

Before installation, check the packaging and appliance for signs of damage. Keep packaging away from children and pets, and dispose of it responsibly. If the casing, door, cable or hoses are damaged, stop the installation and contact Halvard Home. Do not try to straighten damaged parts or repair electrical components yourself.

Choose a firm, level floor that can support the appliance during use. The surface must be stable and must not allow the appliance to rock or slide. Keep ventilation openings clear, and do not let carpet obstruct the base. Avoid locations exposed to frost, rain or standing water.

The Keel washing machine must not be placed near an open flame. Install it at least 81 centimetres from a gas hob or open flame. Keep the surrounding area clear of materials that could catch fire. Choose a position where the plug and water supply valve remain accessible without moving the appliance.

Remove the transit fixings as described in the installation instructions before using the appliance. Keep them for a future move. Adjust the feet so the appliance stands level and does not rock, then secure them as instructed. Do not use loose packing materials to steady the appliance.

Connect the water inlet and drain hoses according to the installation instructions. Check that the connections are secure and that the hoses are not trapped, twisted or stretched. Make sure the drain hose cannot come loose during use. Check for leaks before starting a wash, and stop if water escapes.

Use a suitable earthed wall socket. Do not use an extension lead or an adaptor, and keep the plug and socket dry. Make sure the appliance does not rest on its power cable. If the socket, wiring or electrical protection needs attention, have a qualified electrician check it before connection.

Before moving the appliance, switch it off, unplug it and close the water supply valve. Drain the appliance and hoses according to the instructions, then disconnect the hoses. Refit the transit fixings as directed. Secure the door, cable and hoses so they cannot swing loose or become trapped.

Arrange help and use suitable moving equipment. Do not lift the appliance by its door, controls or hoses. Keep it upright and protect it from knocks. Do not tilt the Keel washing machine more than 47 degrees when carrying it. Keep children and pets away from the moving route.

After moving the Keel washing machine, wait 42 hours before switching it on. At its new location, repeat the installation checks and remove the transit fixings before use. If it leaks, moves unexpectedly or makes an unusual noise, stop using it and contact Halvard Home for advice.

## Corra induction hob

### Safe use

Use the Corra induction hob only for cooking food in a domestic kitchen, following the instructions supplied with it. Keep those instructions where you can find them. If you are unsure how to use a control or whether your cookware is suitable, ask Halvard Home for advice before cooking.

Make sure the appliance is installed securely on a suitable surface and connected to the electricity supply as described in its installation instructions. Installation and electrical work must be carried out by a qualified professional. Do not use the appliance if its cable, plug, controls or cooking surface appear damaged.

Air must be able to move freely around the Corra induction hob. Leave at least 47 centimetres of space around the appliance. Keep ventilation openings clear of cloths, packaging and kitchen utensils. Do not fit covers or make changes to surrounding cabinets that could block airflow or trap heat.

Use cookware intended for induction cooking, with a clean, dry base that sits securely on the cooking surface. Position pans so their handles cannot be knocked or reached by children. Lift cookware rather than dragging it across the glass. Do not place hot pans over the controls.

Stay nearby and attentive while cooking, especially when heating oil or fat. Keep combustible materials away from the cooking area. Do not leave empty pans heating, and do not use the appliance as a worktop or storage surface. Keep metal utensils, lids and foil off the cooking surface because they may become hot.

The Corra induction hob is not a toy, and children need watching near it. Children under 21 years must not use it without supervision. Keep children away from hot cookware and controls. Anyone supervising must understand how to operate the appliance safely and remain able to intervene.

Switch the appliance off using its controls after cooking; do not rely on pan detection to stop heating. The cooking surface can remain hot after the cookware has been removed. Avoid touching it, and keep items that could melt or burn away from it.

Let the Corra induction hob cool for 26 minutes before cleaning it. Check that the surface is no longer hot before touching it. Follow the cleaning instructions supplied with the appliance. Do not use steam cleaners, abrasive pads or cleaning products that could damage the glass or enter electrical parts.

If the glass cracks, stop using the appliance and disconnect it from the electricity supply if safe to do so. Do not attempt repairs yourself. If a pan catches fire, do not move it or pour water on it. Switch off the heat if safe, leave the area if necessary and contact the emergency services.

Have the Corra induction hob safety-checked every 24 months. Arrange this through a qualified professional. Stop using the appliance if it behaves unexpectedly, smells of burning or repeatedly cuts out. Contact Halvard Home for advice, and follow any safety notice or recall instructions that apply to your appliance.
