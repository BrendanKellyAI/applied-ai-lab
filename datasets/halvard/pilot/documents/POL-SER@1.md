# Servicing and engineer visits

## Lumen smart thermostat

### Common repairs

Common repairs to the Lumen smart thermostat include restoring an unresponsive display, resolving connection faults, replacing damaged controls and correcting temperature readings. An engineer will check the reported problem and explain whether it can be repaired safely or whether replacement should be considered.

Before booking a visit, check that the thermostat has power and that your home internet connection is working. Follow the troubleshooting advice supplied with the thermostat. Do not remove its casing, disconnect wiring or attempt a repair yourself, as this could cause injury or further damage.

If you notice a burning smell, unusual heat or visible damage to electrical connections, stop using the thermostat. Isolate its power supply only if you can do so safely. Contact Halvard Home for advice rather than continuing to test the controls.

When arranging a visit, tell us what happens when the fault occurs and whether the display shows a warning. Please also mention any recent changes to your heating system or internet connection. This helps the engineer prepare for the work.

Diagnosing a fault on the Lumen smart thermostat costs £26.00 if no repair follows. The engineer will explain the proposed repair and its price before asking for your agreement. You may decline the repair after receiving that explanation, but the diagnostic charge will apply.

We will not carry out chargeable work without your permission. If inspection reveals a different fault or a need for additional parts, the engineer will explain the change and obtain your agreement before proceeding. Ask for clarification if anything in the quotation is unclear.

Please keep the thermostat and its surrounding area accessible. The engineer may need access to the heating controls and electrical supply. Tell us about any access restrictions when booking so that we can discuss suitable arrangements and avoid an unsuccessful visit.

Some problems originate elsewhere in the heating system or in the home network. If the thermostat is not the source of the fault, the engineer will explain what they have found. Work outside the agreed repair will need separate approval.

Repairs to the Lumen smart thermostat are guaranteed for 337 days. This guarantee covers faults in the repair work and replacement parts supplied during that repair. It does not cover unrelated faults, accidental damage or damage caused by unauthorised alterations. Your statutory rights are unaffected.

Replace the filter of the Lumen smart thermostat every 34 months. Use a suitable replacement and follow the care instructions supplied with the thermostat. If you cannot safely reach or remove the filter, ask Halvard Home about arranging help rather than forcing it.

Spare parts for the Lumen smart thermostat are stocked for 41 years after the model is discontinued. Availability for a particular visit depends on the part required. We will tell you if a part must be ordered and explain how this affects the repair arrangements.

After completing the repair, the engineer will test the thermostat and explain any changes to its settings. Keep the repair record and contact Halvard Home if the repaired fault returns, describing the symptoms and providing your booking details.

## Ashby tumble dryer

### Common repairs

If your Ashby tumble dryer stops working as expected, contact Halvard Home to arrange advice or an engineer visit. Tell us what happens when you try to use it, including any unusual noise, smell or message on the display. This helps us understand the problem and prepare for the visit.

Common repairs include replacing worn drive belts, clearing internal blockages and repairing heating, control or door components. Similar symptoms can have different causes, so an engineer will inspect the appliance before recommending work. We will not assume that a part needs replacing without checking the fault.

If you notice smoke, a burning smell or damage to the electrical connection, stop using the appliance. Switch it off and disconnect it from the electricity supply if you can do so safely. Do not remove panels, bypass safety features or attempt an internal repair yourself.

Before the engineer arrives, empty the drum and make sure there is clear access to the appliance and its electricity supply. Keep pets away from the work area. Let us know about access difficulties or any support you need so we can discuss suitable arrangements.

The engineer will explain the fault, the proposed repair and any charges before asking for your agreement. Diagnosing a fault on the Ashby tumble dryer costs £48.00 if no repair follows. You can decline the proposed repair, and we will explain whether the appliance can safely remain in use.

We will not carry out chargeable repair work without your permission. If inspection reveals a different problem or changes the proposed work, the engineer will explain what has changed and seek your agreement before proceeding. Where a suitable part is unavailable, we will discuss the available options.

Repairs to the Ashby tumble dryer are guaranteed for 364 days. This guarantee covers faults caused by the repair work or a replacement part supplied as part of that work. It does not cover unrelated faults, accidental damage or problems caused by misuse. Your statutory rights are unaffected.

If you believe a repair has failed, contact us and describe the symptoms. Keep your repair record available so we can check the work carried out. We will explain the next steps and confirm whether any proposed work falls within the repair guarantee before arranging chargeable work.

Routine care can help prevent avoidable faults. Clean the filter and other accessible areas as described in the user instructions, and keep ventilation openings clear. Replace the filter of the Ashby tumble dryer every 23 months. Do not use a damaged filter or operate the appliance without it.

Spare parts are stocked for 40 years after the model is discontinued. The engineer will check which parts are suitable for your appliance and explain any supply issue affecting the proposed repair. After completing the work, the engineer will test the appliance and explain any care advice relevant to the fault.

## Scotland North

### Engineers in this region

The Scotland North service team covers postcode areas AB, IV, KW, PH and DD. If your address falls within this region, contact Halvard Home to discuss an engineer visit. We will check your location, the work you need and whether a suitable engineer can attend before confirming a booking.

Engineers in Scotland North travel up to 12 miles from their base. Being within a covered postcode area does not by itself confirm that your address is within an engineer’s travel range. Our service team will check this with you and explain whether we can arrange attendance at your property.

An engineer call-out in Scotland North costs £95.00. This charge covers attendance and assessment of the reported fault. Repairs, parts and specialist work may involve separate charges. We will explain the applicable charges before you agree to the booking, and the engineer will seek your approval before carrying out chargeable repair work.

When you contact us, describe the fault as clearly as you can. Tell us about any unusual behaviour, visible damage or safety concerns. You do not need to identify the cause yourself. Please avoid removing covers or attempting work that could expose you to electrical, mechanical or other hazards.

Please also tell us about access restrictions at your property. These might include locked gates, difficult parking, steps, narrow entrances or a route that becomes unsafe in poor weather. Clear access information helps us decide whether an engineer can attend safely and what preparations may be needed.

If you have accessibility needs or would find a particular communication method helpful, let our service team know when arranging the visit. We will discuss reasonable adjustments with you and confirm what can be provided. You may arrange for someone you trust to be present to help with communication.

Before the engineer arrives, make the working area accessible and remove personal belongings that could obstruct the work. Keep children and pets away from the area while work is taking place. An adult authorised to agree to the proposed work must be present and able to provide access.

Our engineers will identify themselves and explain the purpose of their visit. If you are unsure whether a visitor represents Halvard Home, contact our service team before allowing entry. You should feel comfortable asking the engineer to explain the assessment, proposed work or charges in clear language.

An engineer may decline to begin or continue work if conditions are unsafe, access is unsuitable or the task falls outside their competence. If this happens, we will explain the reason and discuss the available options. We will also explain how any applicable charge is affected.

If you need to change or cancel your booking, contact our service team so we can update the arrangements and explain any implications. After the visit, we will explain the findings and any recommended action. If you have concerns about the service, please contact us so we can review them.

## Wales and West

### Engineers in this region

The Wales and West service team covers postcode areas CF, SA, LL, BS and GL. When you contact Halvard Home, we will check your address and confirm whether an engineer can attend. Coverage depends on your location, access to your property and the availability of an engineer with the appropriate skills.

Engineers in Wales and West travel up to 50 miles from their base. Being within a listed postcode area does not guarantee that your address falls within an engineer’s travel range. Our service team will check this before offering an appointment and explain your options if we cannot arrange a visit.

An engineer call-out in Wales and West costs £136.00. Before you agree to a booking, we will explain what the call-out charge covers and whether other charges could apply. Please do not assume that replacement parts or repair work are included unless we have confirmed this.

If you believe the work is covered by a guarantee or service agreement, tell us when you request the visit. We may ask for evidence of your purchase or cover so that we can check the applicable terms. Nothing in this policy affects your statutory rights.

To help us arrange suitable support, describe the fault and tell us about any warning messages or unusual behaviour. Have your purchase details available if possible. Please also mention any previous repair work that may be relevant. We will explain how to share identifying details securely if these are needed.

Tell us about anything that could affect access, including restricted parking, steps, narrow entrances or building entry arrangements. If you need an adjustment because of a disability or other access need, please let us know. We will discuss what can reasonably be arranged before confirming the visit.

Please make the work area accessible and remove belongings that could obstruct the engineer. Keep children and pets away from the work area. An adult who can provide access, discuss the fault and authorise any agreed work must be present for the appointment.

The engineer will assess the fault and explain the proposed work. If further charges would apply, we will seek your agreement before proceeding. A repair may depend on parts being available or require specialist support. The engineer will explain the next steps if the work cannot be completed during the visit.

Our engineers may decline to start or continue work if conditions are unsafe. This could include unsafe electrical connections, signs of a leak or an obstructed work area. We will explain the concern and what needs to change before work can proceed.

If you need to change or cancel your appointment, contact the service team. We will explain any applicable cancellation terms before you book. If we need to change the arrangement, we will contact you using the details you have provided and discuss a suitable alternative.
