Corra outdoor fan grille notice

Stop using outdoor fan grilles from batch 5107 with the Corra induction hob, and return them for a refund.

Halvard Home is asking customers to check the batch marking on their grille packaging or product label. If your grille matches the affected batch, stop using it and keep it separate from your hob accessories. Please contact Halvard Home customer support for guidance on returning the grille and arranging your refund. If the marking is missing or unclear, leave the grille unused and ask customer support for help identifying it.

We apologise for the inconvenience and appreciate your cooperation.
