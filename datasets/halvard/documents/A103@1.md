Corra hob: condensation in a holiday home

On the Corra induction hob, E-5238 means condensation is interfering with the touch controls. This can happen when a cold holiday home is heated quickly, leaving moisture on the glass.

Switch the hob off at its wall isolator. Once the cooking surface is cool, wipe the glass and control area with a clean, dry cloth. Ventilate the room and leave the hob switched off for at least two hours so any remaining moisture can evaporate. Do not use a hairdryer or heater to dry it.

Restore power and try the hob again. If the warning returns, stop using it and contact Halvard Home support. If you are a guest, tell the property owner or manager.
