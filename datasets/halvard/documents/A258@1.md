Pair your Vale fridge freezer with a voice assistant

Connect your Vale fridge freezer to the Halvard Home app before linking it to a voice assistant. Use a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network during setup.

1. Open the Halvard Home app and check that your fridge freezer appears online.
2. Open your voice assistant’s app and find the section for adding smart home devices or services.
3. Search for Halvard Home, select it and sign in with the same account you use in the Halvard Home app.
4. Allow access, then ask the assistant to discover devices.

Give the appliance a simple name, such as “Kitchen fridge”, to make voice requests clearer. Available commands depend on your voice assistant. If pairing fails, check your internet connection and try linking the accounts again.
