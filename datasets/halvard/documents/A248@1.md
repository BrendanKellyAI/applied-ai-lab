Cleaning your heating or cooling filter

Your Lumen smart thermostat controls your heating or cooling system; the filter to clean is in that equipment, not the thermostat.

Before removing the filter, switch the system off at the thermostat, then isolate the equipment’s power supply. Check your equipment’s instructions for the filter’s location and how to remove it safely.

If the filter is reusable, gently vacuum away loose dust. Only rinse it if the equipment’s instructions allow this, and let it dry completely before refitting. Replace disposable filters rather than washing them.

Refit the filter in the direction shown by its airflow arrow, secure the cover and restore power. If airflow remains weak, contact your heating or cooling service provider.
