# Tessa dishwasher not appearing in the app

If the app cannot find your Tessa dishwasher, check that the dishwasher is switched on and your phone is connected to your home Wi-Fi.

Try these steps:

1. Move your phone close to the dishwasher.
2. Turn on Bluetooth and allow any nearby-device or location permissions requested by the app.
3. Avoid using a guest Wi-Fi network, as it can stop devices communicating with each other.
4. Close the app completely, then reopen it and try adding the dishwasher again.
5. If it still does not appear, switch the dishwasher off at the mains for 30 seconds, then switch it back on.

Still having trouble? Contact Halvard Home support and explain which steps you have tried.
