Corra induction hob lid will not close

E-3946 means the Corra induction hob’s lid sensor has detected that the protective lid is not fully closed. A pan handle, food debris or a misaligned hinge can prevent the lid from reaching its closed position.

Switch off the hob and let the cooking surface cool completely before checking it. Remove all cookware, then wipe the lid’s rim and the surrounding edge with a soft, damp cloth. Gently lower the lid without forcing it.

If the lid closes properly, switch the hob off at the wall for one minute, then restore power. If the warning returns, or the lid remains crooked or stuck, contact Halvard Home support. Do not adjust the hinges yourself.

Keep the lid fully open whenever you cook.
