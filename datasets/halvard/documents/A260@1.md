Pair your Tessa dishwasher with a voice assistant

To pair your Tessa dishwasher, first add it to the Halvard Home app and check that it appears online. Keep your phone connected to your home Wi-Fi during setup.

1. Open your voice assistant’s app and find the option to add a device or link a service.
2. Search for Halvard Home, then select it.
3. Sign in with the same Halvard Home account you use for your dishwasher.
4. Allow the voice assistant to discover your appliances, then select your Tessa dishwasher.

Give the dishwasher a short, distinctive name, such as “Kitchen dishwasher”, so your assistant can recognise it.

If pairing fails, check that the dishwasher is online, then unlink and reconnect the Halvard Home service.
