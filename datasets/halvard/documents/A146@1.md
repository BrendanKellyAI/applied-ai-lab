Mira robot vacuum: using the child lock

The child lock stops little hands from starting a clean or changing settings using the buttons on your Mira robot vacuum.

To switch the child lock on, press and hold the power button for **3 seconds** while the vacuum is switched on and stationary. A sound confirms that the lock is active.

To switch it off, press and hold the power button again for 3 seconds. Wait for the confirmation sound before using the controls.

The child lock only locks the buttons on the vacuum. Scheduled cleaning and controls in the Halvard Home app remain available, so the vacuum may still start automatically.

For safety, keep children away from the vacuum while it is moving.
