Slow draining on your Keel washing machine

If your Keel washing machine takes longer than usual to finish a cycle or leaves clothes unusually wet, a blocked drain filter may be restricting water flow.

KX-3962 is the drain pump filter for this product. A build-up of lint, coins or hair in this filter can cause slow draining and delay the final spin.

To clean it, switch the machine off and unplug it. Allow any hot water to cool. Place towels and a shallow tray beneath the lower front access flap, then loosen the filter slowly to release trapped water.

Remove debris, rinse the filter and refit it securely. Run a drain cycle. If draining remains slow or you notice a leak, stop using the machine and contact Halvard Home support.
