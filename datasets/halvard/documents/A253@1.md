Moving your Fenwick cooker hood

Before moving your Fenwick cooker hood, switch it off, allow it to cool and isolate it from the electricity supply. If it is hardwired, ask a qualified electrician to disconnect it.

Remove the grease filters and wrap them separately to prevent damage. Wear protective gloves when handling exposed metal edges.

Use two people to support the hood while removing its wall or cabinet fixings. Disconnect any ducting carefully, and keep screws and brackets together in a labelled bag.

Pack the hood in its original packaging if available, or use a sturdy box with padding on every side. Do not lift it by its controls or decorative panels.

Secure the packed unit during transport so it cannot slide or tip. Have it safely reinstalled before use.
