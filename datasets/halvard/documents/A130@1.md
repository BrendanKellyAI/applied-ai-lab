Fenwick cooker hood display stays blank

If your Fenwick cooker hood’s display stays blank, first check that its wall switch or fused spur is switched on. Try the hood’s light and fan controls to see whether the whole appliance has lost power or only the display is affected.

Switch the hood off at its electrical supply for 60 seconds, then switch it back on and try the controls again. Keep your hands dry when operating electrical switches.

If the fan and light work but the display remains blank, contact Halvard Home support for advice. Tell them which controls still respond.

If nothing works, check whether other kitchen appliances have power. Do not remove covers or touch internal wiring; arrange a qualified inspection if the problem continues.
