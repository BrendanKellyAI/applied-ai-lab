Pair your Keel with a voice assistant

To pair your Keel washing machine with a voice assistant, first add it to the Halvard Home app and check that it appears online.

1. Open your voice assistant’s app on your phone.
2. Find the section for adding devices, skills or services.
3. Search for Halvard Home and select it.
4. Sign in with the same Halvard Home account you use for your washing machine.
5. Allow account linking, then ask the assistant to discover devices.

Give the machine a simple name, such as “Washing machine”, so the assistant can recognise it easily.

If pairing fails, check your internet connection and confirm that the machine is still online in the Halvard Home app before trying again.
