Brio kettle lint filter notice

Stop using lint filters from batch 9847 with the Brio kettle, and return them for a refund.

Halvard Home is asking customers to check the batch details on their filter packaging. If your filters match the batch shown above, remove them only when the kettle is unplugged and completely cool. Keep the filters and packaging together, and contact Halvard Home customer support for return instructions. Please do not fit these filters to another appliance or pass them on to someone else. If the batch details are missing or unclear, stop using the filters and ask our support team for advice. We apologise for the inconvenience.
