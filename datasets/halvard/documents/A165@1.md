Keel washing machine not found in the app

If the Halvard Home app cannot find your Keel washing machine, start by checking that the machine is switched on and your phone is nearby.

Connect your phone to a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network before adding the machine. A 5 GHz connection will not work for setup. Switch on Bluetooth and allow the app to access nearby devices when prompted.

Close and reopen the app, then try adding the washing machine again. Follow the pairing instructions shown in the app.

If it still does not appear, restart your phone and router, then retry once Wi-Fi is available.

Still having trouble? Contact Halvard Home support with your phone model and a description of where setup stops.
