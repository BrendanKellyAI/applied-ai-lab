Ordering a spare accessory for your Orrin heat pump

To request a spare accessory, contact Halvard Home customer support with the accessory name, your heat pump’s model details and your delivery postcode.

Before getting in touch, take one clear photo of the heat pump’s rating label. If you still have the original accessory, photograph that too. These images can help identify the correct replacement.

Ask support to confirm compatibility, availability, the total price including delivery, and the expected delivery time before you place your order. Do not choose an accessory by appearance alone, as similar items may not fit your model.

When your accessory arrives, check it against your order confirmation. Follow the supplied fitting instructions, and ask support for advice if fitting requires opening the heat pump’s casing.
