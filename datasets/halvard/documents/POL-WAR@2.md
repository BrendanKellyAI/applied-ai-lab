# Warranty

This policy explains the warranty support available for eligible Halvard Home purchases used in a private household. It is for customers who have bought directly from Halvard Home or through an authorised retailer and want to understand their cover, request help or make a claim.

The warranty covers faults caused by defective materials or workmanship under normal domestic use, subject to the terms set out in this policy. It explains how we assess claims and when we may offer a repair, replacement or alternative remedy.

The policy also describes what is not covered, including accidental damage, misuse, unauthorised alterations and ordinary wear and tear. It sets out your responsibilities for care and maintenance, the information we may need to support a claim and how to contact us.

This warranty is separate from your legal rights as a consumer and does not limit or replace them.

## General terms

### Registering your appliance

Register a new appliance within 43 days of delivery to activate the standard warranty. Registration links your appliance to your contact details and helps us check your warranty cover if you need support. It also allows us to contact you about important safety information relating to your appliance.

You can register through the Halvard Home website or ask our customer support team for help. Use the registration page linked from our website rather than an unfamiliar link in a message. If you cannot use the online form, contact us to discuss an accessible alternative.

To register, provide your contact details, delivery address and the appliance details shown on its rating label. We also ask where you bought the appliance and when it was delivered. Please check that the information matches your purchase and delivery documents before submitting the form.

Keep your proof of purchase for at least 62 months after delivery. Your receipt, invoice or order confirmation should identify the seller and the appliance purchased. Keep any delivery confirmation with these documents, particularly if the delivery date differs from the purchase date.

Make sure any documents you upload are clear and readable. Please do not send original paper documents unless we specifically ask for them. If your proof of purchase is missing or unclear, contact the seller for a replacement or ask us what supporting evidence we can accept.

We will send confirmation using the contact details you provide. Check the confirmation carefully and tell us if anything is incorrect. If it does not arrive, check your junk email folder or contact customer support so we can check whether your registration was received.

If the appliance was a gift, you can register it using your own contact details. Ask the purchaser for proof of purchase and delivery information. You do not need to share payment card details or account passwords with us to complete registration.

Registration is free. You do not have to agree to receive marketing messages to activate the standard warranty. Any choice about promotional communications is separate from registration, and declining those communications will not affect your warranty cover.

We use the information you provide to manage registration, support warranty claims and communicate relevant safety information. Our privacy notice explains how we handle personal information, who we may share it with and how you can exercise your data protection rights.

Please tell us if your contact details change so that our records remain accurate. If you have missed the registration deadline or cannot supply the requested information, contact us to discuss your circumstances. We will explain what this means for your standard warranty.

Registration does not replace the warranty’s other terms or establish that a particular fault is covered. We assess claims against the applicable warranty conditions. Your statutory rights are separate from this warranty and are not affected by whether you register your appliance.

### Making a claim

To make a warranty claim, contact Halvard Home through the support details shown on our website or in your warranty information. Tell us what has gone wrong and how the fault affects normal use. Please stop using the item if you believe it may be unsafe.

Have your proof of purchase available when you contact us. This might be a receipt, an order confirmation or another document that shows where and when you bought the item. If you cannot find it, tell us so we can discuss what other evidence may be suitable.

Please provide your name, contact details and the address where the item is kept. Describe the fault clearly, including any warning messages and the circumstances in which the problem occurs. Photographs or a recording may help us understand the issue, but do not put yourself at risk to obtain them.

Halvard Home replies to a warranty claim within 22 working days of receiving it. Our reply will explain whether we need further information, an inspection or another step before we can decide your claim. An acknowledgement does not mean that the claim has been accepted.

A claim made by phone is logged within 67 hours. Please keep any correspondence about your claim and tell us if your contact details change. If you contact us again, mention that you have already raised a claim so we can find the relevant record.

We assess claims against the warranty terms that apply to your purchase. We may ask about installation, use, care or previous repairs where this is relevant to the reported fault. We will explain why we need information if the reason is not clear.

Do not arrange a repair on our behalf or send the item to us unless we ask you to do so. We will explain any inspection, collection or return arrangements and tell you whether a proposed service falls outside the warranty before asking you to agree to it.

If we ask you to return the item, follow our packing instructions and remove personal belongings. You do not need to keep the original packaging to make a claim. Tell us if you need help preparing the item for collection or making it accessible for inspection.

If your claim is accepted, we will explain the remedy available under your warranty and how it will be arranged. If we decline your claim, we will give you the reason in clear language and explain how to ask us to review the decision.

You can provide further evidence or tell us why you disagree with our assessment. We will consider the information you send and explain our conclusion. Making a warranty claim does not affect your statutory rights, including any rights you may have against the retailer that sold you the item.

### What the warranty does not cover

The warranty covers faults caused by defective materials or workmanship. It does not cover damage or faults caused by misuse, neglect, accidental damage or failure to follow the instructions supplied with your appliance. Please read the care and safety guidance before using, cleaning or moving it.

We do not cover faults caused by incorrect installation, unsuitable positioning or connections that do not meet the requirements in the installation instructions. This includes damage caused by an unsuitable electrical supply, plumbing problems or inadequate ventilation. If installation work causes the fault, you should contact the person or business responsible for that work.

Normal wear and tear is not covered. This includes gradual deterioration from ordinary use and the replacement of consumable parts where their condition reflects expected use rather than a manufacturing fault. Routine cleaning, servicing and maintenance are your responsibility and are not included in the warranty.

Damage caused by a build-up of dirt, grease, limescale or other deposits is not covered where it results from a failure to follow the care instructions. We also exclude faults caused by unsuitable cleaning products, foreign objects, pests or substances that the appliance is not designed to handle.

The warranty does not cover scratches, dents, discolouration or other changes in appearance caused by handling, cleaning or use. Cosmetic damage must be reported within 7 days of delivery. Please keep any relevant packaging and photographs to help us assess delivery damage. This reporting requirement does not affect your statutory rights.

We do not cover damage caused by repairs, alterations or replacement parts where the work or parts are unsuitable or have not been approved by us. This exclusion applies to faults resulting from that work; it does not automatically exclude an unrelated manufacturing fault from cover.

Use outside the domestic setting described in the instructions is not covered. This includes commercial use or use in conditions for which the appliance was not designed. Damage caused by flooding, fire, lightning or other external events is also excluded where it is not due to a defect covered by the warranty.

A visit that finds no fault covered by the warranty is not a warranty repair. If the problem concerns installation, maintenance or operation rather than a covered defect, a charge may apply. We will explain any applicable charge before you agree to the visit. An inspection that finds misuse is charged at £106.00.

The warranty does not cover costs you choose to incur without our agreement, such as arranging your own repair or hiring a replacement. Please contact us before authorising work that you expect us to pay for, so we can assess the fault and explain the available support.

These exclusions apply to this warranty only. They do not remove or restrict your statutory rights, including your rights against the retailer if the appliance is faulty, not as described or not fit for purpose. If you are unsure whether a fault is covered, please contact us for guidance.

If you need a warranty repair in Midlands, contact Halvard Home through the support details shown in your warranty documents. Describe the fault and tell us how it affects normal use. We will ask for proof of purchase and information from the appliance label so that we can check your warranty and arrange suitable support.

We assess claims against the terms of your warranty. Acceptance means that we have agreed to arrange support under those terms, based on the information available. The engineer may need to inspect the appliance to confirm the cause of the fault and whether the repair is covered. We will explain any concerns about cover before asking you to approve chargeable work.

In Midlands, a warranty engineer visit is offered within 76 hours of a claim being accepted. We will contact you to offer an appointment and confirm the address, access arrangements and details of the reported fault. This commitment concerns the offer of a visit; it does not mean that the repair will be completed during that visit.

Parts for warranty repairs in Midlands arrive within 58 working days. If your repair needs a replacement part, we will explain what is required and how the repair will proceed. An engineer may need to return to fit the part and check that the appliance operates safely. We will keep you informed about the arrangements for completing the work.

Please make sure the engineer can reach the appliance safely. Clear the surrounding area and tell us about access restrictions, parking arrangements or anything at the property that could affect the visit. If you have accessibility needs or require communication support, let us know when arranging the appointment so that we can discuss suitable adjustments with you.

Do not continue using an appliance if you believe it is unsafe. Follow the safety advice in its instructions and contact us for guidance. Do not remove protective covers or attempt internal repairs yourself. If it is safe to do so, leave the appliance in its installed position so that the engineer can assess the fault and the installation.

Where the fault and the required work are covered by your warranty, we will not charge you for the warranty repair. If the engineer identifies an issue outside that cover, we will explain the findings and any proposed charge. You can decide whether to authorise work that falls outside the warranty.

If you cannot attend the agreed appointment, contact us to rearrange it. Please also tell us if the fault changes or the appliance has been moved. Once the repair is complete, the engineer will explain the work carried out and any relevant care advice. If you remain concerned about the repair, contact Halvard Home so that we can review the matter with you.

## Wales and West

### Warranty repairs in this region

If your Halvard Home appliance develops a fault in Wales and West, contact our customer support team using the contact details in your warranty documents. Explain what has happened and whether the appliance appears safe to use. We will guide you through the warranty claim process.

Please have your proof of purchase and the appliance identification details available. We may ask for photographs or a description of the fault to help us assess your claim. If you cannot find the requested information, tell us so that we can explain what other evidence may be suitable.

We will check whether the fault is covered by your warranty and confirm whether your claim has been accepted. Acceptance means that we have agreed to arrange a repair under the warranty, based on the information available. We will explain if an inspection is needed to establish the cause of the fault.

In Wales and West, a warranty engineer visit is offered within 46 hours of a claim being accepted.

This commitment concerns the offer of a visit, rather than completion of the repair. We will contact you to discuss access and agree an appointment. Please tell us about accessibility needs, parking restrictions or anything else that could affect the engineer’s ability to reach and inspect the appliance safely.

Parts for warranty repairs in Wales and West arrive within 57 working days.

A repair may depend on replacement parts being available. We will explain whether parts are needed and how this affects the repair arrangements. The engineer may need to return to fit them. We will keep you informed about the arrangements and confirm when the appliance is ready to use.

Before the visit, make sure the engineer can reach the appliance without obstruction. Keep children and pets away from the work area. Do not move a heavy appliance or disconnect fixed services yourself. If access requires specialist assistance, discuss this with us when arranging the appointment.

If the appliance appears unsafe, stop using it. Disconnect it only if you can do so safely, and follow the safety instructions supplied with it. Do not attempt a repair yourself while waiting for advice, as this could cause injury or further damage.

The engineer will assess the fault and explain the proposed work. If the inspection identifies damage or work outside the warranty cover, we will explain the reason and discuss the available options. We will seek your agreement before carrying out work that you would need to pay for.

After the repair, the engineer will explain what has been done and provide relevant advice about safe use. Keep the repair record with your warranty documents. If the fault remains or you are unhappy with the service, contact customer support so that we can review the matter.

Your warranty operates alongside your statutory rights. Nothing in these repair arrangements limits the rights you have under consumer law, including rights you may have against the retailer.
