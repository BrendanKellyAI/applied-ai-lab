# Warranty

This policy explains the warranty support available for eligible Halvard Home purchases used in a private household. It is for customers who have bought directly from Halvard Home or through an authorised retailer and want to understand their cover, request help or make a claim.

The warranty covers faults caused by defective materials or workmanship under normal domestic use, subject to the terms set out in this policy. It explains how we assess claims and when we may offer a repair, replacement or alternative remedy.

The policy also describes what is not covered, including accidental damage, misuse, unauthorised alterations and ordinary wear and tear. It sets out your responsibilities for care and maintenance, the information we may need to support a claim and how to contact us.

This warranty is separate from your legal rights as a consumer and does not limit or replace them.

## General terms

### Registering your appliance

Register a new appliance within 43 days of delivery to activate the standard warranty. Registration links your appliance to your purchase details and helps us check your cover when you need support. You can register through the Halvard Home website or ask our customer care team for help. Registration is free, and you do not need to agree to receive marketing messages to complete it.

Before you begin, have your purchase and delivery details ready, along with the identifying information shown on the appliance label. We use this information to match your registration to the correct appliance. Copy the details carefully, as an incorrect entry may make it harder for us to confirm your cover. If the label is missing, damaged or difficult to reach safely, contact us rather than moving or dismantling the appliance.

Give us the name and contact details you want us to use for warranty matters. These details should belong to the person responsible for the registration, or to someone authorised to act for them. If you are registering on behalf of someone else, make sure you have their permission to share their information. Please do not send personal information that we have not asked for.

Keep your proof of purchase for at least 62 months after delivery. Your receipt, invoice or order confirmation should identify the seller and show what you bought and when you bought it. If delivery took place separately from the purchase, keep the delivery confirmation as well. A registration confirmation does not replace these records, and we may ask to see them when assessing a warranty claim.

If you received the appliance as a gift, ask the person who bought it to provide suitable purchase and delivery evidence. You do not need to share details about the occasion or your relationship with the buyer. Where obtaining the paperwork is difficult, contact customer care to discuss what evidence may be acceptable. We cannot promise to confirm warranty eligibility without enough information to verify the purchase.

After you submit your registration, we will send confirmation using the contact details you provided. Check that the appliance and delivery information in that confirmation are correct. If anything is wrong, tell us so that we can update the record. Keep the confirmation somewhere accessible with your purchase documents. If it does not arrive, check your unwanted email folder or contact customer care for assistance.

Submitting a registration does not mean that every fault or request for service will be covered. Warranty claims remain subject to the policy terms, including the conditions relating to installation, use and care. Registration does not extend the scope of cover or remove any exclusions. We will assess a claim using the information available about the appliance, the reported problem and the circumstances in which it arose.

If you have missed the registration deadline, please contact us rather than assuming that support is unavailable. We will explain whether registration can be accepted and what options apply. Do not change the delivery details to make the appliance appear eligible. If you are unsure of the delivery date, tell us and provide the evidence you have so that we can review it.

If the website is unavailable or you cannot use the online form, our customer care team can help you register through an alternative route. Let us know if you need an accessible format or support with the process. You do not need to explain a disability or health condition to request practical help. We will tell you what information is needed to complete the registration.

Please tell us if your contact details change or if you notice an error in your registration record. If ownership of the appliance changes, contact us for guidance about the warranty terms that apply. Updating a registration record does not itself confirm that cover can be transferred. We may need supporting information before making changes that affect who can use the warranty.

We handle registration information in line with our privacy policy. It may be used to administer your warranty, respond to support requests and contact you about relevant safety matters. Your marketing preferences are managed separately and do not determine warranty eligibility. Nothing in this registration requirement limits your statutory rights, including any rights you have against the seller if the appliance is faulty or does not match its description.

### Making a claim

To make a warranty claim, contact Halvard Home customer support using the contact details published on our website or supplied with your purchase. Tell us that you wish to claim under the warranty and explain what has gone wrong. You do not need to use legal language or diagnose the fault yourself.

Please give us your name, contact details and the address where the item is kept. Describe the fault, how it affects normal use and any circumstances that may help us understand it. If you have already contacted us about the same issue, mention this so we can connect the records.

We will ask for evidence of purchase and information that helps us identify the item. A receipt or order confirmation is usually suitable. If you cannot find these documents, tell us what evidence you can provide. We will explain whether it is enough to assess your claim and discuss suitable alternatives where available.

You can ask someone to make the claim on your behalf. We may need your permission before discussing your personal details with them. If you need help communicating with us, or require information in an accessible format, let us know. We will discuss how we can support you through the claim process.

A claim made by phone is logged within 67 hours. During the call, our customer support team will record the information you provide and explain what happens next. Please tell us if you think we have misunderstood your description of the fault or recorded your contact details incorrectly.

Halvard Home replies to a warranty claim within 22 working days of receiving it. Our reply will explain whether we can assess the claim from the information provided or need further evidence. A reply does not necessarily mean that the claim has been accepted or that an inspection has been completed.

We may ask you to describe what you can see or hear, provide photographs, or follow checks that are safe and suitable for you. Do not attempt a check that you are uncomfortable carrying out. Tell us about any access needs or practical difficulties so we can consider another way to assess the fault.

If the item appears unsafe, stop using it and keep others away from it. Disconnect it from the power supply only if you can do so safely. Do not open protective covers, attempt internal repairs or continue testing a fault that could cause injury or damage. Contact customer support for advice.

Please do not arrange repairs or send the item to us unless we have agreed the arrangements with you. Work carried out without our agreement may affect our ability to assess the original fault. It does not automatically remove your legal rights. We will consider the circumstances and explain any effect on your warranty claim.

If an inspection or return is needed, we will explain how it will be arranged and what you need to prepare. Follow the packing and handling instructions we provide, and tell us if you cannot safely move or pack the item. We will clarify responsibility for transport costs before you agree to the arrangements.

Keep your proof of purchase and copies of messages relating to the claim. Do not send original documents unless we specifically ask for them and explain how they will be returned. If photographs or documents contain personal information unrelated to the claim, you may cover or remove that information before sending them.

We assess claims against the warranty terms that apply to your purchase and the evidence available. Where the fault is covered, we will explain the remedy offered and how it will be carried out. If the proposed arrangements are not practical for you, tell us so we can discuss your concerns.

If we believe the fault is not covered, we will give you the reason in plain language and identify the relevant warranty term. We will explain any chargeable option separately. We will not treat your warranty claim as permission to carry out paid work, and we will seek your agreement before proceeding.

If you disagree with our decision, ask customer support to review it and provide any evidence you would like us to consider. You may also use our complaints process. This warranty is separate from your statutory rights and does not limit them, including your rights against the retailer that sold you the item.

### What the warranty does not cover

The warranty covers faults caused by defective materials or workmanship. It does not cover damage, faults or costs caused by circumstances outside that cover. When we assess a claim, we look at the condition of the appliance, the information you provide and any relevant inspection findings. An exclusion applies only where it is relevant to the fault or claim.

The warranty does not cover misuse, neglect or use contrary to the instructions supplied with the appliance. This includes forcing controls, ignoring safety warnings or using the appliance for a purpose for which it was not designed. Damage caused by unsuitable substances or materials is also excluded.

Faults caused by incorrect installation are not covered. This includes unsuitable connections, inadequate ventilation or placing the appliance where its operating requirements cannot be met. If an installation problem has caused the fault, that problem must be addressed before a repair can be expected to resolve it.

The warranty does not cover faults arising from an unsuitable or unreliable supply of electricity, water or other services needed for operation. Damage caused by power surges, leaks from external plumbing or problems with the building’s services is excluded where those conditions caused the damage.

Routine care and maintenance are your responsibility. The warranty does not cover cleaning, clearing obstructions or correcting problems caused by a failure to follow the care instructions. This includes damage linked to a build-up of dirt, grease, limescale or other deposits where appropriate maintenance would have prevented the problem.

Normal wear from ordinary use is not covered. Neither is the replacement of consumable items simply because they have been used up or worn out. If an item fails because of a defect in its materials or workmanship, rather than ordinary use, we will assess it under the warranty.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage. This includes damage caused by dropping, impact, excessive force or objects entering areas where they do not belong. Damage caused by pets, pests or exposure to unsuitable environmental conditions is also excluded unless it results from a fault covered by the warranty.

Changes to appearance that do not affect operation are not generally covered unless they result from defective materials or workmanship. Scratches, dents, stains and surface damage caused by handling, cleaning or use fall outside the warranty. Using abrasive cleaning materials or unsuitable chemicals may damage finishes and is not covered.

Cosmetic damage must be reported within 7 days of delivery. Please contact us with a description of the damage and, where possible, clear photographs. This reporting requirement helps us investigate delivery-related concerns and does not remove your statutory rights if the appliance arrived damaged or was otherwise not as described.

Damage caused by alterations or repairs that introduce a fault is excluded. The same applies where unsuitable replacement parts or accessories cause damage or prevent correct operation. Having work carried out by an independent repairer does not, by itself, mean that an unrelated defect is excluded from warranty cover.

The warranty does not cover use in a commercial, industrial or shared service setting where the appliance was supplied for domestic use. This exclusion applies where that use caused or contributed to the fault, including use that places demands on the appliance beyond its intended operating conditions.

An inspection that finds misuse is charged at £106.00. We will explain the inspection arrangements and any applicable charge before you agree to a visit. If you wish to proceed with work outside the warranty, we will explain the proposed work and its cost before seeking your approval.

The warranty does not cover a visit solely to explain normal operation, adjust settings or resolve an issue that is not a fault. Please check the operating instructions and troubleshooting advice before requesting a repair. If you remain unsure, contact us so we can help assess the problem.

Costs you choose to incur without our agreement, such as arranging a separate inspection or repair, are not automatically reimbursed under this warranty. We will consider the circumstances and any legal obligations that apply. Please contact us before committing to work if you intend to seek reimbursement through the warranty.

These exclusions describe the limits of this warranty, not the limits of your legal rights. Nothing here excludes or restricts rights or remedies available under applicable consumer law, including rights against the retailer. If a warranty exclusion applies, you may still be entitled to assistance or a remedy under that law.

## Brio kettle

### Cover period

The Brio kettle is covered for 47 months from the date of delivery. This is the date the kettle reaches you, rather than the date you place your order or make payment. Your delivery confirmation will normally show the date we use to establish your cover.

Keep your proof of purchase and delivery confirmation somewhere safe. We may ask to see them when you make a claim. If your records are missing or show conflicting information, contact Halvard Home so we can help check the delivery details and confirm when your cover began.

The cover period applies to faults caused by defective materials or workmanship, provided the Brio kettle has been used and cared for as instructed. Being within the cover period does not mean that damage from misuse, accidental damage or normal wear is covered by this warranty.

Tell us about a fault as soon as you notice it. Please explain what has happened and provide the information we need to identify your Brio kettle. If it appears unsafe, stop using it and unplug it if you can safely do so. Do not attempt a repair yourself.

We assess whether a claim falls within the cover period using the date you report the fault to us. If you report a covered fault before your warranty expires, the claim will not become invalid simply because our assessment or the agreed repair takes place after the cover has ended.

Labour for repairs is free for the first 10 months. This applies to work authorised by Halvard Home to put right a fault covered by the warranty. It does not apply to work that you arrange independently without our agreement, or to work on damage outside the warranty.

After the free labour cover ends, a labour charge may apply even if the fault otherwise qualifies for warranty support. We will explain any proposed charge before asking you to approve the repair. We will not treat your request for help as permission to carry out chargeable work.

Replacement parts fitted to the Brio kettle are covered for a further 138 days. This cover begins on the date the parts are fitted during a repair authorised by Halvard Home. Keep the repair record, as it helps us confirm the fitting date if a replacement part develops a fault.

The replacement part cover applies to defects in the fitted part, not to unrelated faults elsewhere in the kettle. It does not shorten any cover still available under the original warranty. Where the original warranty provides longer protection for the same fault, we will assess your claim under that cover.

A repair does not restart the warranty for the Brio kettle itself. Unless we confirm otherwise in writing, its cover continues from the original delivery date. The separate protection for fitted replacement parts should not be read as an extension of the warranty for the entire kettle.

If we replace the Brio kettle under the warranty rather than repair it, the replacement carries the cover remaining from the original delivery date. We will provide written confirmation of the applicable end date so that you can keep it with your claim records and proof of purchase.

If the Brio kettle is sold on, the new owner must transfer the warranty within 42 days. The transfer deadline runs from the date of sale to the new owner. The seller should pass on the proof of purchase, delivery details and any repair records needed to support the transfer.

The new owner should contact Halvard Home with those records and evidence of the sale. A transfer does not restart or extend the cover. The warranty remains linked to the original delivery date, and its conditions continue to apply after the change of ownership.

If the transfer deadline is missed, the new owner cannot rely on this warranty unless Halvard Home agrees otherwise in writing. Contact us if there is a problem obtaining the records or completing the transfer. We will explain what information is needed and whether we can accept the request.

This warranty is separate from your legal rights as a consumer. Its expiry, its labour terms and its transfer requirements do not remove rights you may have under applicable law. You may also have rights against the retailer that sold you the Brio kettle, regardless of whether this warranty applies.
