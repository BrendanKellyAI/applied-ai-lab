Cleaning your Keel washing machine filter

Clean the filter if your Keel washing machine drains slowly or leaves water in the drum.

Switch off the machine and unplug it. If you have used a hot wash, let the water cool completely before starting.

Open the filter access flap at the bottom front of the machine. Place towels and a shallow tray underneath to catch water. Turn the filter anticlockwise slowly, allowing water to drain before removing it fully.

Remove fluff, coins and other debris, then rinse the filter under running water. Wipe the filter opening with a damp cloth.

Refit the filter by turning it clockwise until secure, without forcing it. Close the flap, reconnect the power and run a short rinse to check for leaks.
