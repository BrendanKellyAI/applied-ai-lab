Vale fridge freezer: first installation

If the freezer door will not close fully during first installation, a transit spacer may still be fitted beneath the door.

KX-9696 is the removable transit spacer supplied with your Vale fridge freezer to hold the freezer door steady during delivery. It is packaging, not a working appliance component, and must be removed before use.

With the appliance unplugged, open the freezer door and gently pull the spacer out by hand. Do not use sharp tools or force it. Close the door and check that the seal touches the cabinet all the way round.

If the spacer will not release, leave the appliance unplugged and contact Halvard Home support. Follow the installation guide before switching on.
