Moving your Tessa dishwasher

Before moving your Tessa dishwasher, switch it off, unplug it and turn off the water supply. Disconnect the inlet and drain hoses, catching any remaining water in a shallow tray.

KX-8618 is the rear transport clip that holds the drain hose against the dishwasher during a move. Secure the hose in this clip before lifting. If the clip is cracked, contact Halvard Home for a replacement before transporting the unit.

Keep the dishwasher upright and use two people to move it. Never lift it by the door handle.

After positioning it, release the drain hose from the clip and reconnect it without bends or trapped sections. Failure to drain after moving may be caused by a hose crushed beneath the cabinet. Check its route before running a programme.
