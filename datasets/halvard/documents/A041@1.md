Vale fridge freezer display stays blank

If your Vale fridge freezer is still cooling but its display stays blank, the display control board may have failed. KX-8011 is the display control board for this product; a fault in its internal power circuit can stop the screen lighting up without stopping cooling.

Check that the plug is secure and the wall socket is switched on. Switch off and unplug the appliance for five minutes, then reconnect it with dry hands. Allow two minutes for the display to restart.

If the screen remains blank, contact Halvard Home support to arrange an inspection and, if needed, a board replacement. Do not remove any covers or attempt electrical repairs yourself.
