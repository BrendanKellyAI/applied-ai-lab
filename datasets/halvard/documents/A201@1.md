Ordering a spare accessory

To order a spare accessory for your Lumen smart thermostat, contact Halvard Home customer support with the name or a clear description of the item you need.

Before you get in touch, check the model name on your thermostat and take a photo of the accessory, if you still have it. This helps the support team identify a suitable replacement.

Ask the team to confirm compatibility, price, delivery charges and availability before you place your order. Include your delivery country so they can check the options for your location.

If you are unsure what the accessory is called, explain what it does and where it fits. Do not send payment card details by email.
