Descaling your Vale fridge freezer

A slow or uneven stream from the water dispenser can be caused by limescale blocking the nozzle. KX-3452 is the removable water dispenser nozzle on the Vale fridge freezer.

To descale it:

1. Switch off the fridge freezer at the socket and place a towel beneath the dispenser.
2. Turn the nozzle anticlockwise and gently pull it free.
3. Soak the nozzle for 15 minutes in equal amounts of white vinegar and warm water.
4. Rinse thoroughly with clean water, then refit it by turning clockwise until secure.
5. Restore power and dispense one litre of water into a jug. Discard this water.

Do not pour descaler into the fridge or its cooling system. If the flow remains weak, contact Halvard Home support.
