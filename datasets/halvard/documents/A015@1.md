Descaling your Ashby tumble dryer

Limescale can block the water collection tank’s filter, causing your Ashby tumble dryer to stop and show a full-tank warning even when the tank is empty.

KX-3673 is the removable filter inside the water collection tank. If it has a white, chalky coating, descale it as follows:

1. Switch off the dryer, unplug it and leave it to cool for 30 minutes.
2. Remove and empty the water collection tank, then lift out the filter.
3. Soak the filter in a descaler approved for tumble dryer filters, following the descaler’s dilution and soaking instructions.
4. Rinse thoroughly, then refit the filter and tank.

Do not pour descaler into the dryer. If the warning returns after cleaning, contact Halvard Home support.
