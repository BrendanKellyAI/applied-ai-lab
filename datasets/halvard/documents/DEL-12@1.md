Pan protector safety notice

Stop using pan protectors from batch 9544 with the Keel washing machine, and return them for a refund.

Halvard Home is recalling these pan protectors because they may interfere with the safe operation of your appliance. Please check the batch details on the packaging or product label. If the packaging is missing or the label is unclear, stop using the protectors and contact Halvard Home customer support for advice.

Keep the affected protectors away from the machine while you arrange their return. Our support team can explain the refund process and help if you have any concerns. We apologise for the inconvenience.
