Installing your Ashby tumble dryer

Before installing your Halvard Home Ashby tumble dryer, read the supplied installation instructions and check that your chosen space meets the clearance requirements.

Remove all packaging from inside and around the drum. Place the dryer on a firm, level floor, then adjust its feet until it sits steadily without rocking. Keep ventilation openings clear of carpets and other obstructions.

Follow the supplied instructions for any vent hose or water drainage connection required by your model. Plug the dryer directly into a suitable earthed wall socket; do not use an extension lead.

Wipe the empty drum with a soft, damp cloth before first use. If the cable is damaged or you are unsure about the electrical supply, do not connect the dryer. Seek qualified help.
