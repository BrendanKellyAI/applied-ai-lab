Burning smell from your Fenwick cooker hood

On the Fenwick cooker hood, E-8525 means the fan motor has overheated, which can cause a burning smell. Stop cooking, turn the hood off and isolate its electricity supply at the wall switch, if safe to do so.

Do not restart the hood to check whether the smell has cleared. Keep it switched off until a qualified service engineer has inspected it.

Once the hood is completely cool, check the removable grease filters for heavy build-up. Grease can restrict airflow and contribute to overheating. Clean the filters according to the user guide, but do not remove electrical covers or attempt to repair the motor.

If you see smoke or flames, leave the area and call the fire service.
