Resetting your Corra induction hob

A factory reset restores your Corra induction hob’s original settings and clears saved preferences. Make a note of any settings you want to use again afterwards.

Before starting, remove all cookware and allow the cooking zones to cool. The control panel must be clean and dry.

1. Switch the hob off using the power button, but leave its electrical supply connected.
2. Press and hold the timer button and the minus button together for five seconds.
3. Release both buttons when you hear a beep.
4. Press the power button once to confirm the reset.

Switch the hob on again and set your preferences.

If the controls do not respond or the reset does not complete, contact Halvard Home support. Do not remove the hob or open its casing.
