Using your Ashby dryer in a holiday home

Keep a short set of drying instructions near your Ashby tumble dryer so guests can use it safely and confidently.

Clean the lint filter after every load. A blocked filter restricts airflow and can make drying take longer. Before starting, check clothing care labels and remove anything that is not suitable for tumble drying.

Avoid overloading the drum, and keep the area around the dryer clear so air can circulate. Ask guests to stay in the property while it is running and let the programme finish, including its cooling stage.

Between bookings, check that the drum is empty and the filter is clean. If the holiday home will be empty for an extended period, switch off and unplug the dryer.
