Ice tray safety notice

Halvard Home is recalling ice trays from the affected batch because they may become unsafe when used with the Corra induction hob.

Stop using ice trays from batch 9817 with the Corra induction hob, and return them for a refund.

Please check the batch details on your ice tray or its packaging. If the details match, keep the tray away from the hob and contact Halvard Home customer support for help arranging your return. If you cannot find the batch details, please contact us before using the tray with your hob.

We are sorry for the inconvenience and appreciate your help in keeping your home safe.
