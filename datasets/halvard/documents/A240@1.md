Power cut during use

If your Vale fridge freezer loses power, keep both doors closed to help food stay cold. Note when the power cut started.

A full freezer can usually keep food frozen for around 48 hours with the door shut; a half-full freezer can manage around 24 hours. These are estimates, so check food carefully afterwards.

When power returns, check that the appliance has power and starts cooling. Avoid adding fresh food until the fridge is at 5°C or below and the freezer is at −18°C.

Discard perishable chilled food if it has been above 8°C for more than four hours. Never taste food to judge whether it is safe. If you are unsure how warm it became, discard it.
