Replacing the Brio kettle’s filter

On the Halvard Home Brio kettle, E-2393 means the removable limescale filter is not engaging its safety switch, usually because the filter’s retaining clip is damaged. The kettle will not heat until the filter is securely fitted.

Unplug the kettle, empty it carefully and leave it to cool for 15 minutes. Open the lid and check the filter behind the spout. If the clip is cracked or missing, replace the complete filter rather than trying to glue it.

Order a replacement filter for the Brio kettle from Halvard Home support. Slide the new filter into its guides and press gently until it clicks into place.

If the warning remains, stop using the kettle and contact support.
