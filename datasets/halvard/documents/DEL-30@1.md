Important advice for Tessa owners

Halvard Home is asking customers to check the batch details on their descaling tablet packaging. Stop using descaling tablets from batch 3558 with the Tessa dishwasher, and return them for a refund.

The affected tablets may not be suitable for your dishwasher. Keep the packaging and take the tablets back to the retailer where you bought them. Please do not put them in your household waste or pass them on to someone else.

If you have used these tablets and are concerned about your dishwasher, contact Halvard Home customer support for guidance. We apologise for the inconvenience and appreciate your help.
