Descaling your Orrin heat pump

Limescale can build up inside the domestic hot water heat exchanger, restricting water flow and making the cylinder take longer to heat. This is more likely in hard water areas.

KX-9673 is the descaling service kit for the Orrin heat pump’s hot water heat exchanger. If hot water recovery has become noticeably slower, ask a qualified heating engineer to check for scale and use this kit if descaling is needed.

Do not pour kettle descaler into the cylinder or heating circuit. Keep the casing closed and leave chemical cleaning to the engineer.

After servicing, allow one complete hot water heating cycle before checking performance. If heating remains slow, arrange a further inspection rather than repeating descaling.
