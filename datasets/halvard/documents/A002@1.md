Keel washing machine: child lock will not release

On the Halvard Home Keel washing machine, E-4194 means the child lock has failed to release after you switched it off. The control buttons remain locked because the machine has not confirmed that the lock is disabled.

To reset the child lock:

1. Press and hold the Child Lock button for three seconds.
2. If the lock symbol stays lit, switch the machine off at the socket and unplug it for two minutes.
3. Reconnect the power, switch the machine on, then hold Child Lock for three seconds again.

The child lock disables the controls; it does not indicate whether the door is safe to open. Never force the door. If the controls remain locked, contact Halvard Home support.
