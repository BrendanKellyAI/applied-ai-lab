Vale fridge freezer: unusual vibration

On the Vale fridge freezer, E-7084 means the vibration sensor has detected excessive movement while the compressor is running. An uneven position or contact with nearby furniture can trigger this fault.

To check the appliance:

- Switch it off at the socket and unplug it.
- Check that bottles, shelves and drawers are secure.
- Make sure the cabinet is not touching a wall or cupboard.
- Check that the fridge freezer stands level. Adjust the front levelling feet if needed, without tipping the appliance.
- Leave it unplugged for five minutes, then reconnect it.

If the vibration or warning returns, contact Halvard Home support. An internal compressor mounting may need attention. Do not remove the rear cover or attempt to tighten internal components.
