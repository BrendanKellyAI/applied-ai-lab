Lumen smart thermostat warranty

Your Halvard Home Lumen smart thermostat has a two-year warranty from the date of purchase. It covers manufacturing faults during normal household use.

KX-3170 is the part number identifying this thermostat. Quote it when contacting Halvard Home support so we can check the correct product’s warranty.

For example, if the display repeatedly goes blank while the thermostat still has power, an internal manufacturing fault may be responsible. Contact support with your proof of purchase, purchase date and a brief description of when the problem occurs.

Do not open the casing or attempt an internal repair. Support will assess the fault and explain the next steps. Accidental damage and normal wear are not covered.
