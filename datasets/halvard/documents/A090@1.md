Mira robot vacuum not switching on

For the Halvard Home Mira robot vacuum, E-9454 means the battery protection system has blocked startup because the battery charge is too low.

To restore power:

1. Plug the charging dock into a working wall socket and switch the socket on.
2. Unplug the dock before wiping its charging contacts and those underneath the vacuum with a dry cloth.
3. Reconnect the dock, then place the vacuum on it with the charging contacts aligned.
4. Leave it charging for three hours, then press and hold the vacuum’s power button for three seconds.

If the vacuum still will not switch on, contact Halvard Home support. Do not open the battery compartment or attempt to bypass the protection system.
