Fenwick cooker hood: using the child lock

The child lock prevents accidental changes to your Fenwick cooker hood’s controls. It does not act as a physical barrier, so keep children away from the hob and hot cookware.

To switch the child lock on, press and hold the padlock button for three seconds. A lock symbol appears on the display to confirm that the controls are locked.

To unlock the controls, press and hold the padlock button again for three seconds. Wait for the lock symbol to disappear before selecting a fan speed or changing the light.

If the controls do not respond, make sure your fingers and the control panel are clean and dry, then try unlocking again.
