Brio kettle: fault message on the display

If your Brio kettle shows a fault message, stop using it and switch it off at the socket. Unplug it and leave it to cool for 15 minutes before checking it.

Check that the water level is between the minimum and maximum marks. Make sure the kettle sits flat on its base and that the base and electrical connections are dry. Never immerse the base in water.

Once these checks are complete, reconnect the kettle and try switching it on once. If the message returns, unplug it and contact Halvard Home support. Tell the adviser exactly what appears on the display.

Do not use the kettle if it leaks, smells burnt or has a damaged cable.
