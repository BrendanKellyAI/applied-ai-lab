Corra induction hob: first installation

Before using your Corra induction hob, ask a qualified installer to check that it sits level in the worktop and that its electrical connection is safe.

KX-7011 is the sealing strip supplied for fitting beneath the hob’s glass edge. It must lie flat all the way round before the hob is lowered into place.

During first installation, a folded sealing strip can leave one corner raised, causing the hob to rock when touched. If you notice this, leave the power switched off and ask your installer to lift the hob and refit the strip correctly.

Do not press the raised corner down or add sealant to fill the gap. Only use the hob once the installer has corrected the fit.
