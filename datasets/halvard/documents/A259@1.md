Updating your Orrin heat pump firmware

Firmware updates can improve how your Orrin heat pump works. Use only firmware supplied by Halvard Home for your exact model.

Before you start, note your model name and current firmware version, if it is shown on the controller. Check the update instructions supplied with the firmware, as the installation method may vary.

Keep the heat pump connected to its power supply throughout the update. Do not switch it off at the mains or reset the controller while installation is in progress.

Once the update finishes, follow any restart instructions and check that your usual heating settings are still correct.

If you cannot find the correct update or installation appears to stop, contact Halvard Home support before trying again.
