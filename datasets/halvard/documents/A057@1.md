Mira stops charging after a power cut

If your Halvard Home Mira robot vacuum was cleaning when the power went off, it may return to its dock but fail to charge once power is restored.

KX-8033 is the dock’s power protection module. A sudden loss of mains power can leave this module in its protective shutdown state, preventing the dock from supplying charge.

Switch off the vacuum and unplug the dock from the wall socket for 60 seconds. Then plug the dock back in, switch on the vacuum and place it on the charging contacts.

If charging does not start, contact Halvard Home support. Do not open the dock or attempt to replace the module yourself.
