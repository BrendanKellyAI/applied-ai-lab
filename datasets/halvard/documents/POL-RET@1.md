# Returns and refunds

This policy explains how returns and refunds work at Halvard Home. It is for customers who buy directly from us for personal or household use, and for people helping them with a return. If you bought through a retailer or marketplace, please contact the seller about its returns process.

Here you can find guidance on requesting a return, providing proof of purchase, preparing goods for collection or delivery, and receiving a refund. The policy also explains how we handle goods that arrive damaged, develop a fault or do not match their description, as well as requests made because you have changed your mind.

We aim to make the process clear, fair and straightforward. Our customer support team can explain what applies to your circumstances and help if you need an accessible way to contact us. This policy does not affect your legal rights as a consumer.

## General terms

### Changing your mind

You can return an unwanted appliance within 80 days of delivery.

This change of mind policy applies to appliances bought directly from Halvard Home for personal use. If you bought through a retailer or marketplace seller, please contact that seller about their returns policy and how to arrange your return.

You do not need to give a reason for changing your mind. We may ask for feedback to help improve our service, but choosing not to provide it will not affect your eligibility to return an appliance.

To arrange a return, contact our customer service team using the contact details on our website or your order confirmation. Please provide proof of purchase and tell us which appliance you would like to return.

We will explain the return process and confirm whether collection or another approved return method is available for your address. Please contact us before sending anything back so that we can provide the correct instructions.

You may inspect the appliance as you would in a shop to understand its appearance, features and suitability. Please handle it carefully and avoid using it for household tasks if you intend to return it.

Under this policy, appliances should be returned clean, dry and free from damage caused by use or handling. Please include the accessories, fittings, instructions and other items supplied with the appliance.

If something supplied with your appliance is missing, tell us when arranging the return. We will explain whether this affects acceptance under our change of mind policy or could lead to a deduction from your refund.

Please use the original packaging if you still have it. If you do not, contact us for advice about suitable protection. Missing original packaging does not, by itself, remove your statutory cancellation rights.

The appliance must be packed securely for transport, with loose parts protected and movable parts secured. Please follow any packing instructions we provide and tell us if you need help preparing it safely.

If the appliance has been connected or installed, it must be safely disconnected before collection. Where specialist disconnection is needed, use a suitably qualified person. Our collection service does not include disconnection unless we expressly agree otherwise.

Remove your personal belongings and clear any personal information stored on the appliance before handing it over. Sign out of connected services where relevant, following the instructions supplied with the appliance.

Please tell us about access restrictions, stairs or other circumstances that could affect collection. We will discuss suitable arrangements with you. Do not move an appliance yourself if doing so could cause injury or damage.

A return collection charge may apply when you change your mind. We will explain any applicable charge before you agree to the collection. Charges will not be introduced without being disclosed to you.

If you arrange your own return with our agreement, use a suitable carrier and keep proof of dispatch. Responsibility for loss or damage during transport will depend on who arranged the carrier and your statutory rights.

Refunds are paid within 5 working days of a returned appliance arriving at our warehouse.

We will normally refund the original payment method. If that is not possible, we will contact you to agree a suitable alternative. We will not require you to accept store credit instead of a refund.

Your payment provider controls when a refund appears in your account after we issue it. If you cannot locate your refund, please contact us so that we can check its status and help you trace it.

Any refund of delivery charges will reflect the terms of your purchase and your statutory rights. We will explain how delivery charges are treated when arranging your return, including any implications of choosing an enhanced delivery service.

Where handling has reduced the appliance’s value, we may make a deduction where the law allows. We will explain the reason and basis for any proposed deduction rather than applying an unexplained charge.

If the appliance arrives damaged or incomplete, we will contact you to discuss what we found. You can provide photographs, collection records or other relevant information if you disagree with our assessment.

This policy does not limit your statutory rights, including rights relating to faulty, misdescribed or unsuitable goods. If your appliance has a fault, tell us so that we can explain the appropriate options rather than treating it as an unwanted return.

### Damaged deliveries

We take care to protect your order during delivery, but damage can still happen. If your delivery arrives damaged, Halvard Home will help you arrange a suitable remedy. You do not need to raise a separate claim with the delivery company.

Report damage found on delivery within 73 hours.

This reporting request helps us investigate what happened and organise the return. It does not remove or restrict your legal rights. If you discover damage after unpacking, or cannot contact us within that window, please still get in touch.

Check the packaging and its contents where it is safe to do so. Look for signs of impact, crushing, tearing or moisture. Packaging damage does not necessarily mean that the contents are damaged, but please tell us about anything that concerns you.

If damage is visible when the delivery arrives, you may refuse to accept it. Ask the driver to record the reason for refusal, if possible, and contact us so that we can explain what happens next.

Accepting or signing for a delivery does not mean that you have accepted damage or given up your legal rights. You can still report a problem that was not apparent when the delivery was handed over.

Do not use anything that appears damaged or unsafe. Keep people and pets away from sharp edges, exposed parts or leaking material. Do not attempt repairs or dismantle the damaged item to find the cause.

Contact our customer care team through the contact details on our website or in your order confirmation. Explain what has happened and whether the damage affects the packaging, the contents or their safe handling.

Please provide the name and contact details used for your order, together with a description of the damage. If you are contacting us on someone else’s behalf, we may need their permission before discussing their order.

Photographs can help us understand the problem and decide how to handle it safely. Where practical, include clear views of the damaged area, the packaging and the delivery label. Avoid including unrelated personal information in your photographs.

You do not need to put yourself at risk to gather evidence. If taking photographs is unsafe or difficult, tell us. We will discuss an alternative way to assess the damage rather than ask you to handle something hazardous.

Keep the packaging if it is safe and practical to do so. It may help protect the contents during collection or support our enquiries with the carrier. Missing packaging will not automatically prevent us from helping you.

We may ask questions or arrange an inspection to understand the damage. We will explain why this is needed and what you should expect. Our enquiries with the delivery company will not replace our responsibility to deal with your claim.

Depending on the circumstances and your legal rights, the remedy may be a replacement, repair or refund. We will explain the available options clearly and will not require you to accept a repair where you have a legal right to reject the goods.

A replacement for a damaged delivery is sent within 19 working days.

If a replacement is unavailable, we will discuss another suitable remedy with you. We will not substitute something different without your agreement.

Where a return is needed, we will arrange collection or provide suitable return instructions. Halvard Home covers the return delivery costs for damage that occurred before the goods reached you. Please do not arrange your own courier without checking with us.

Tell us about access restrictions or handling concerns when arranging collection. We will explain how to prepare the return safely. If the damaged contents cannot be moved or packed safely, leave them undisturbed and ask us for guidance.

Do not dispose of the damaged goods unless we confirm that you should, or keeping them would create a safety risk. If disposal is necessary for safety, record the condition where possible and tell us what happened.

An approved refund will be returned through the original payment method unless we agree another suitable method with you. We will explain how delivery charges are treated in your circumstances, in line with your legal rights.

If you disagree with our assessment or the remedy offered, ask our customer care team to review the matter. You can explain your concerns and provide supporting information. Nothing in this section limits your statutory rights.

### Faulty appliances

An appliance that develops a fault within 100 days of delivery can be returned for a full refund. This promise applies where the appliance is faulty, rather than where you have changed your mind. It does not limit your legal rights.

If you think your appliance is faulty, stop using it if continued use could be unsafe or cause damage. Disconnect it from the power supply if you can do so safely. Do not attempt repairs or remove protective covers to investigate the problem.

Contact Halvard Home customer support to tell us what has happened. Please describe the fault, explain how it affects use and provide your order details or other evidence of purchase. If you cannot find your receipt, contact us so we can discuss suitable alternatives.

We may ask for photographs or a description of an error message to help us understand the problem. Only provide these if it is safe to do so. You do not need to recreate a dangerous fault or continue using an appliance that appears unsafe.

Our support team may suggest straightforward checks where these are appropriate and safe. These checks are intended to clarify the issue, not to make you carry out repairs. Tell us if a suggested check is unsuitable because of your circumstances or the appliance’s condition.

If the appliance needs to be returned, we will explain how to arrange this and provide suitable return instructions. We cover the reasonable costs of returning a faulty appliance. Please speak to us before arranging your own transport so that we can agree a safe, suitable method.

Where an appliance is fitted or connected to services, tell us before arranging its return. We will discuss safe removal and whether specialist help is needed. Do not disconnect fittings yourself if doing so could create a risk or damage your home.

Please follow our packing instructions and use the original packaging if you still have it. Keeping the original packaging is not a condition of returning a faulty appliance. If suitable packaging is unavailable, let us know so we can help arrange a practical alternative.

Remove personal belongings and prepare the appliance for transport as instructed, where safe and practical. Tell us about leaks, contamination or damage that could affect handling. We will explain which accessories should accompany the return and how to deal with anything that cannot safely be packed.

Returned faulty appliances are tested within 11 working days of arrival. We use testing to assess the reported fault and understand its cause. Please include a clear description of any problem that occurs intermittently, as it may not appear during a routine check.

We will explain the outcome of our assessment in plain language. If testing does not reproduce the reported fault, we will discuss the evidence with you rather than assume that no fault exists. Relevant photographs, correspondence and descriptions of the circumstances can help us reach a fair decision.

Where your return qualifies under our refund promise, we will refund the purchase price and the original delivery charge you paid for the appliance. We will not apply a restocking charge or deduct money simply because you opened the packaging or used the appliance before discovering the fault.

Refunds are normally made through the payment method used for the purchase. If that method is unavailable, we will agree a suitable alternative with you. We will confirm when the refund has been issued, although your payment provider controls when it appears in your account.

Damage caused by misuse, accidental damage or changes made against our instructions is not treated as a manufacturing fault under this promise. Normal wear is not itself evidence of a fault. We will consider the circumstances and explain the reasons if we believe these exclusions apply.

If we believe a return does not qualify, we will tell you why and discuss the available options. We will not carry out chargeable repairs or arrange chargeable work without your agreement. You can challenge our assessment and provide further evidence for us to review.

If your appliance does not qualify under this refund promise, you may still have rights to a repair, replacement or refund under consumer law. Any guarantee we offer is additional to those rights. Contact us so that we can explain the options relevant to your circumstances.

Please tell us if you need an accessible way to contact us or help with the return arrangements. If you disagree with how your claim has been handled, ask customer support to refer it through our complaints process. We will explain how to raise your concerns.
