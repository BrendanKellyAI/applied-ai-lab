# Returns and refunds

This policy explains how returns and refunds work at Halvard Home. It is for customers who buy directly from us for personal or household use, and for people helping them with a return. If you bought through a retailer or marketplace, please contact the seller about its returns process.

Here you can find guidance on requesting a return, providing proof of purchase, preparing goods for collection or delivery, and receiving a refund. The policy also explains how we handle goods that arrive damaged, develop a fault or do not match their description, as well as requests made because you have changed your mind.

We aim to make the process clear, fair and straightforward. Our customer support team can explain what applies to your circumstances and help if you need an accessible way to contact us. This policy does not affect your legal rights as a consumer.

## General terms

### Changing your mind

You can return an unwanted appliance within 80 days of delivery. This applies if you have changed your mind, whether the appliance does not suit your home or you simply no longer want it. Please contact our customer support team to arrange the return rather than sending the appliance back without letting us know.

When you contact us, have your order confirmation ready and tell us which appliance you would like to return. We will explain how to prepare it and confirm the available return arrangements. You do not need to give a reason for changing your mind, although we welcome feedback if you would like to share it.

You may unpack and inspect the appliance as you would in a shop. Please handle it carefully and avoid using it beyond what is needed to check its nature, features and operation. If handling or use has reduced its value, we may make a deduction from your refund where the law allows. We will explain the reason for a deduction and how we have assessed it.

Please return the appliance with its supplied accessories, instructions and removable parts. Use the original packaging if you still have it. If you do not, let us know so we can advise on suitable protection. Missing packaging does not automatically prevent a return, but the appliance must be packed securely enough to protect it during transport.

Before the appliance is collected or sent back, remove your belongings and make sure it is clean, dry and safe to handle. Where relevant, disconnect it safely and remove any personal information stored on it. If disconnection requires specialist help, arrange this with a suitably qualified person. Our customer support team can explain what preparation is needed, but please do not attempt work you cannot carry out safely.

We will tell you whether collection is available and whether you will be responsible for return transport costs before you agree to the arrangements. Please tell us about access restrictions or anything that could make collection difficult. Keep your collection receipt or proof of postage until your return has been resolved.

Refunds are paid within 5 working days of a returned appliance arriving at our warehouse. We will send your refund to the payment method used for the purchase and confirm when it has been issued. Your payment provider controls when the refund appears in your account.

These terms cover returns because you have changed your mind. They do not limit your statutory rights, including rights that may apply when you cancel a purchase made online or when an appliance is faulty, damaged or not as described. If your return concerns a problem with the appliance, please tell us so we can handle it under the appropriate terms.

### Damaged deliveries

If your delivery arrives damaged, please contact Halvard Home so we can help. We understand that damage can be disappointing and inconvenient. We will explain the options available and arrange a suitable resolution, taking account of the condition of the delivery and your legal rights.

Report damage found on delivery within 73 hours.

Please use the contact details provided in your order confirmation or on our website. Tell us what has happened and include your order reference, your contact details and a clear description of the damage. Let us know whether the damage affects the packaging, the contents or anything else delivered with your order.

If it is safe to do so, please take clear photographs of the damage and the packaging. These can help us understand the problem and decide how to handle the return. If you cannot provide photographs, tell us. We will discuss an alternative way to assess the damage rather than automatically refuse your request.

You may refuse a delivery if you can see that it is damaged. Ask the delivery driver to record the reason for refusal, where possible, and contact us to explain what happened. Accepting a delivery or signing a delivery record does not remove your right to report damage or seek a remedy.

Please do not use anything that appears unsafe. Avoid attempting repairs or handling exposed or broken parts. Keep the delivery somewhere secure, away from children and pets, if you can do so safely. Tell us about any hazard so we can give appropriate advice about collection.

Keep the packaging if it is available and safe to store. It may help protect the contents during collection and support our assessment. Missing or damaged packaging will not, by itself, prevent us from helping you. Please tell us if you need suitable packaging or assistance preparing the return.

We will explain whether collection is needed and agree the arrangements with you. We cover the reasonable costs of returning a delivery that arrived damaged. Please contact us before arranging your own return service, so we can confirm a suitable method and explain how any agreed costs will be reimbursed.

Depending on the circumstances and your legal rights, you may be entitled to a repair, replacement or refund. We will discuss your preferred remedy and explain any relevant limitations. We will not require you to accept store credit instead of a refund to which you are legally entitled.

A replacement for a damaged delivery is sent within 19 working days.

If a replacement is unavailable, we will contact you to discuss an appropriate alternative or refund. Any refund will be returned through your original payment method unless we agree otherwise with you.

If you discover damage outside the reporting window, please still contact us. The reporting request helps us investigate delivery problems; it does not limit your statutory rights. We will consider the circumstances fairly and explain our decision clearly, including how to raise a complaint if you are unhappy with the outcome.

### Faulty appliances

An appliance that develops a fault within 100 days of delivery can be returned for a full refund. If you believe your appliance is faulty, contact Halvard Home customer support so we can help you arrange its return. Please describe the fault and explain how it affects the appliance.

We may ask for proof of purchase, such as an order confirmation, receipt or bank statement showing the purchase. We may also ask for photographs or a description of any visible damage. Please do not attempt to reproduce a fault if doing so could be unsafe.

Stop using the appliance if you notice signs that it may be dangerous. Disconnect it from the power supply if this is safe to do, and keep it away from children and pets. Do not dismantle it or attempt a repair to support your return request.

Our support team will explain how the appliance should be returned and arrange a suitable collection or return method. Tell us if its location, condition or installation could make removal difficult. Please do not send it back without contacting us, as we need to provide the correct return instructions.

You do not need the original packaging to return a faulty appliance. However, the appliance must be prepared for transport safely. We will explain what protection is needed and discuss suitable arrangements if you cannot pack or move it safely yourself.

Please remove personal belongings and follow our instructions about emptying, cleaning or disconnecting the appliance where relevant. Tell us if the fault prevents you from doing this. If the appliance stores personal information, remove that information where you can do so safely.

Returned faulty appliances are tested within 11 working days of arrival. Testing helps us understand the reported problem and assess the appliance’s condition. We will consider your description of the fault alongside our findings, including whether the problem occurs only under particular conditions.

We will explain the outcome of our assessment in plain language. If we cannot reproduce the fault, we will discuss the findings with you and consider any further information you provide. A fault that does not appear during testing will not automatically be treated as an unfounded claim.

Where a refund is due, we will normally return it through the payment method used for the purchase. We will explain how any applicable delivery charges are handled. Please tell us if the original payment method is no longer available so we can discuss an appropriate alternative.

Damage caused by misuse, accidental damage or failure to follow safety instructions may not qualify as a fault for the purposes of this policy. We will explain any decision to decline a return and the evidence supporting it. We will not reject a claim simply because packaging is missing.

These terms do not limit your statutory rights. Depending on the circumstances, you may be entitled to a repair, replacement or refund under consumer law. If you disagree with our decision, contact customer support and ask for your complaint to be reviewed.
