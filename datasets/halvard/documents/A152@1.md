Mira robot vacuum lid will not close

If the door or lid on your Halvard Home Mira robot vacuum will not close, switch the vacuum off and remove it from its charging dock before checking it.

Look for dust, hair or small objects around the opening, hinges and catch. Wipe accessible edges with a dry, soft cloth. Debris trapped along the edge can stop the lid from sitting flush.

Check that the dust bin is seated correctly and that nothing is sticking out above it. Then lower the lid gently, keeping your fingers clear of the edges. Do not force it shut.

If the lid still will not close, or a hinge or catch looks damaged, stop using the vacuum and contact Halvard Home support.
