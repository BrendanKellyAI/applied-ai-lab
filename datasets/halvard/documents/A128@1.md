Setting a delayed start on your Lumen thermostat

Use the heating schedule on your Halvard Home Lumen smart thermostat to start heating later. Set the time you want heating to begin, rather than the number of minutes you want to wait.

For a one-hour delay at 18:00, set the start time to 19:00. Choose your target temperature, then save the schedule. Check that the schedule is enabled and applies to the correct day.

Heating will only be needed if the room is cooler than your target temperature. If nothing happens at the scheduled time, check the room temperature and the thermostat’s clock.

To change your plans, edit or remove the scheduled start so it does not run unexpectedly.
