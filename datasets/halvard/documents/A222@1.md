Keel washing machine: flashing warning light

A flashing warning light on your Halvard Home Keel washing machine means it needs attention. Check which light is flashing before trying these steps.

If the drum is still moving, let it stop. Do not force the door open. Check that the door is firmly closed and that no clothing is trapped around the seal.

If the warning continues, switch the machine off and unplug it for 5 minutes. With the power disconnected, check that the water supply tap is open and the inlet hose is not kinked.

Reconnect the power and try starting the programme again. If the light keeps flashing, contact Halvard Home support. Tell them which light flashes and when it started.
