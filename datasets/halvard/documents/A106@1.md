Water leaking underneath your Lumen thermostat

Water beneath your Lumen smart thermostat can be caused by a split cable-entry seal. This lets warm, damp air reach the cold wall behind the thermostat, where moisture forms and drips from the lower edge.

KX-6818 is the replacement cable-entry seal for this product, not an error message.

If you notice dripping:

- Do not touch the thermostat while it is wet.
- Switch off the heating controls at their electrical isolator, provided the switch and surrounding area are dry.
- Contact Halvard Home support or a qualified heating engineer to inspect the seal and replace it if damaged.

Ask the engineer to check for nearby pipe leaks too. Do not restore power until the thermostat has been checked and declared safe.
