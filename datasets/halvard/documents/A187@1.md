# Tessa dishwasher child lock

The child lock on your Halvard Home Tessa dishwasher helps prevent accidental changes to the controls.

To switch it on, press and hold the button marked with a padlock for three seconds. The padlock light comes on to confirm that the controls are locked. If a wash is already running, it continues with the selected settings.

To switch the child lock off, press and hold the same button for three seconds. Wait for the padlock light to go out before choosing a programme or changing a setting.

The child lock does not physically lock the door. Keep children away from the dishwasher during use, as opening the door can release hot steam. Store dishwasher detergent out of their reach.
