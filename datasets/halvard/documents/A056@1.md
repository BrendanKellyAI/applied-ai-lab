# Fenwick cooker hood: strange noise during use

A rattling or grinding noise with E-3223 means your Fenwick cooker hood has detected resistance at the fan, usually caused by something obstructing its movement.

Switch the hood off and isolate it from the electricity supply before checking it. Wait ten minutes for it to cool.

Remove the grease filters as described in your user guide. Check for loose debris around the filter opening, but do not reach into the fan or remove internal covers. Refit the filters securely, as a loose filter can also vibrate during use.

Restore power and briefly test the hood on its lowest speed. If the noise or warning returns, switch it off and contact Halvard Home support. Do not continue using it.
