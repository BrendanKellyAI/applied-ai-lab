Ordering a spare accessory for your Fenwick cooker hood

To order a spare accessory for your Fenwick cooker hood, first write down the full model details from the appliance’s rating label. This helps identify an accessory that fits your hood.

Contact Halvard Home customer support with those details and the name of the accessory you need. If you are unsure what it is called, send one clear photo of the existing accessory and explain what you want to replace.

Before confirming an order, ask support to check compatibility, availability, the total price including delivery, and the expected delivery time.

Do not choose an accessory by appearance alone: similar-looking items may have different fittings. Keep your order confirmation for reference.
