Cleaning the Fenwick cooker hood filter

KX-3926 is the washable metal grease filter for the Fenwick cooker hood. If cooking smells linger and extraction seems weaker than usual, grease blocking the filter mesh may be the cause.

Switch off the hood at the mains and let it cool before cleaning. Support the filter with one hand, release its retaining catch and lower it carefully.

Soak the filter in warm water with washing-up liquid for 15 minutes. Gently brush away grease using a soft brush, then rinse thoroughly. Avoid abrasive cleaners and do not bend the mesh.

Leave the filter to dry completely before refitting it. Make sure the catch locks securely, then restore power. If extraction remains weak, contact Halvard Home support.
