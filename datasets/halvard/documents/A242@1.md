Booking an engineer for your Brio kettle

To arrange an engineer visit for your Brio kettle, contact Halvard Home customer support and explain what is happening. Have your name, address, contact number and proof of purchase ready, if available.

Describe the fault clearly, including when it started and whether it happens every time you use the kettle. Tell the team about any checks you have already tried.

Before confirming a booking, ask about available appointment times, any visit charge and whether your warranty covers the work.

Switch off and unplug the kettle before checking it for visible damage. If it leaks, sparks or has a damaged lead, stop using it. Do not remove the casing or attempt internal repairs while waiting for advice.
