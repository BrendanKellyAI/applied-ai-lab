Flashing warning light on your Orrin heat pump

A flashing warning light on your Orrin heat pump needs attention, but the light alone does not identify the cause.

First, check the thermostat display and write down any message before changing settings. Note whether the heat pump is still heating and when the flashing started.

From a safe position, check whether leaves, snow or other loose debris are blocking airflow around the outdoor unit. Do not remove covers, reach inside or chip away ice.

If the light keeps flashing, contact Halvard Home support with your observations. Avoid repeatedly restarting the heat pump.

If you notice smoke, a burning smell or unusual loud noises, stop using the system and seek professional help promptly.
