Recycling your old Lumen smart thermostat

Recycle your old Lumen smart thermostat as electrical waste, not in your household rubbish bin. Take it to a local recycling centre that accepts small electrical appliances. Check your council’s website for opening times and any booking requirements before travelling.

If the thermostat is still installed and wired into your heating system, ask a qualified electrician or heating engineer to disconnect it safely. Do not cut wires or open the casing yourself.

If the unit still works, use its factory reset option to clear saved settings before recycling. Remove it from your smart home account too.

Remove any batteries only if they are designed to be user-removable. Recycle these separately at a battery collection point.
