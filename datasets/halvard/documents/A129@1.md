Moving your Ashby tumble dryer

Switch off the dryer and unplug it from the wall socket before moving it. If it has been running, let it cool completely first.

Remove all laundry and clean the lint filter. If your dryer has a water tank, empty it to prevent spills. Disconnect any external drain or vent hose, then secure the hoses and power cable so they cannot drag or catch.

Use two people and a suitable appliance trolley. Keep the dryer upright, and never lift it by the door or control panel. Protect the cabinet with blankets and secure it against movement during transport.

At its new location, place the dryer on a firm, level floor. Reconnect it following its installation instructions before use.
