Water leaking underneath your Vale fridge freezer

Water beneath your Vale fridge freezer can come from a spill, a blocked defrost drain or a door that is not closing properly.

Before checking, switch off the appliance and unplug it, provided the plug and surrounding area are dry. If they are wet, isolate the power at the fuse box instead.

Wipe up the water, then check that food packaging is not stopping either door from closing. Clean the door seals with a soft, damp cloth.

If you can see a drain hole inside the fridge, gently remove any loose debris around it. Do not insert sharp objects or remove panels.

If water returns after restarting, contact Halvard Home support for help.
