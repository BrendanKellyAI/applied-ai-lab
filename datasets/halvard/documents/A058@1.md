Weak extraction from your Fenwick cooker hood

If your Fenwick cooker hood runs but removes steam slowly, its grease filter may be clogged. A build-up of cooking grease blocks airflow, making extraction feel weak even at the highest fan setting.

KX-8303 is the washable metal grease filter for this cooker hood. Clean it to restore airflow:

- Switch off the hood and isolate it from the electricity supply.
- Support the filter, release its retaining catch and lower it carefully.
- Soak it in warm water with washing-up liquid for 10 minutes, then gently brush away grease.
- Rinse and let it dry completely before refitting.

Restore power and test the fan. If extraction remains weak, contact Halvard Home support for further checks.
