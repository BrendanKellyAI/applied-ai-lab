Replacing a Vale freezer drawer

If a freezer drawer in your Halvard Home Vale fridge freezer is cracked or no longer slides properly, check which drawer needs replacing before ordering.

Empty the damaged drawer before removing it. Do not force it past a stop or pull on cracked plastic, as sharp edges could cause injury. Follow the removal instructions in your appliance manual.

Find the appliance’s model details on its rating label and use them to check that the replacement drawer will fit. Include the drawer’s position when asking for help, as upper and lower drawers may differ.

Once fitted, check that the new drawer slides freely and that the freezer door closes fully before putting food back inside.
