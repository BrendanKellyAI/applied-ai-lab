Replacing a cracked drain tray

For the Halvard Home Orrin heat pump, KX-1160 is the replacement condensate drain tray. This tray collects water and directs it into the drain hose. A crack can let water leak underneath the unit instead.

If you notice a persistent puddle, switch the heat pump off using its controller. Do not remove any covers or try to seal the tray with glue. Water beneath an outdoor unit can also occur during normal defrosting, so a puddle alone does not confirm a cracked tray.

Contact Halvard Home support and describe where the water appears. Quote the part number above and arrange for a qualified engineer to check the tray and replace it if damaged.
