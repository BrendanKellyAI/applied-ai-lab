Corra induction hob: child lock fault

On the Corra induction hob, E-5170 means the child lock touch control has remained active for more than 10 seconds. Moisture, grease or an object resting on the control can cause this fault and prevent the child lock from releasing.

Remove anything covering the control panel. With dry hands, switch the hob off at its wall isolator and allow the surface to cool. Wipe the controls with a soft, dry cloth, then leave the power off for 60 seconds.

Restore the power and touch and hold the lock symbol for three seconds to release the child lock.

If the fault returns, stop using the hob and contact Halvard Home support. Do not attempt to open the hob or repair its controls.
