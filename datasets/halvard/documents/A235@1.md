Fenwick cooker hood door or lid not closing

If your Fenwick cooker hood door or lid will not close, switch the hood off at the mains and let it cool before checking it.

Look for grease build-up or trapped objects around the closing edge. Wipe accessible edges with a soft cloth dampened with warm, soapy water, then dry them.

Check that the grease filter sits fully in its holder. A filter sticking out of position can obstruct the cover and prevent it from closing.

Try closing the door or lid gently. Do not force it, bend the hinges or adjust the latch with tools.

If it still will not close, leave the hood switched off and contact Halvard Home support.
