Ordering a spare accessory

To order a spare accessory for your Halvard Home Tessa dishwasher, first identify what you need, such as a cutlery basket or cup holder.

Take one clear photograph of the existing accessory, including any damaged area. If the accessory is missing, photograph the space where it should fit instead.

Contact Halvard Home customer support with the product name, a description of the accessory and your photograph. Ask them to confirm compatibility with your dishwasher before you place an order.

Request the total price, including delivery, and the estimated delivery time. Keep your order confirmation until the accessory arrives.

Before fitting the replacement, check that it matches your order and follow any supplied instructions.
