Mira robot vacuum leaking underneath

If water collects underneath your Mira robot vacuum, stop cleaning and switch it off. If the charging dock or plug is wet, switch off the mains socket before handling it.

Remove the water tank and empty it over a sink. Check that the filler cap is fully closed and look for cracks around the tank and its outlet. A loose filler cap can allow water to escape.

Wipe the tank and the underside of the vacuum with a dry cloth. Do not rinse the vacuum body or return it to the dock while wet.

If the tank is cracked, or water leaks again after refitting it securely, stop using the mopping function and contact Halvard Home support.
