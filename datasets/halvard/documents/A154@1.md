Ordering a spare accessory

To order a spare accessory for your Halvard Home Keel washing machine, contact Halvard Home customer support with the appliance’s model details and a description of the accessory you need.

Before contacting support, take one clear photo of the appliance’s identification label. If you still have the original accessory, photograph it too. This helps support check compatibility rather than relying on appearance alone.

Explain whether you need a replacement for a missing accessory or an additional item. Ask support to confirm availability, the total price including delivery, and the expected delivery time before you agree to purchase.

Do not order an accessory simply because it looks similar. Check that it is suitable for your exact Keel model first.
