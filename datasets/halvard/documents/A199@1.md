Vale fridge freezer not found in the app

If the app cannot find your Vale fridge freezer, try these checks:

- Check that the fridge freezer is switched on and your phone is connected to your home Wi-Fi.
- Move your phone closer to the appliance during setup.
- Turn on Bluetooth and allow any nearby device or local network permissions requested by the app.
- Follow the pairing instructions in the guide supplied with your fridge freezer.
- Fully close the app, wait 10 seconds, then reopen it and try adding the appliance again.

If it still does not appear, check for an app update. Contact Halvard Home support if the problem continues, and tell them which steps you have tried.
