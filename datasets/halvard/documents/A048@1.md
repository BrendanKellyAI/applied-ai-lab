Ordering a spare Brio kettle lid

E-6736 means your Brio kettle’s lid locking clip is not holding the lid securely shut. If the clip is cracked or broken, you will need a replacement lid.

Unplug the kettle and let it cool completely before checking the clip. Do not try to hold the lid closed while boiling or repair the clip with glue.

To order a spare lid, contact Halvard Home customer support with the model name and serial number printed on the kettle’s base. Ask for a replacement lid for your Brio kettle, and confirm compatibility and the total price before paying.

Fit the new lid following its supplied instructions. Stop using the kettle if the lid still will not lock securely.
