Recycling your Mira robot vacuum

On the Mira robot vacuum, E-7679 means the battery has reached the end of its safe charging life. Stop using the vacuum, switch it off and unplug its charging dock from the mains. Do not try to recharge it.

Contact Halvard Home support to check whether a battery replacement is suitable before recycling the whole unit.

If you are disposing of your Mira, take it to a local household waste recycling centre that accepts electrical appliances. Never put the vacuum or its battery in household rubbish or kerbside recycling.

Leave the battery inside the vacuum unless a qualified technician removes it. Tell recycling centre staff about the battery fault so they can arrange appropriate handling.
