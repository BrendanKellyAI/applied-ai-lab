Unusual vibration from your Corra induction hob

On the Corra induction hob, E-9865 means the cooling fan is vibrating excessively, usually because its mounting has worked loose. You may notice a rattling sound or feel vibration through the worktop, even after removing your cookware.

Stop cooking and switch the hob off using its controls. Once the cooling fan has stopped, turn off the hob’s electricity supply at the wall isolator. Leave it switched off until it has been checked.

Do not remove the glass surface or try to tighten internal fittings yourself. Contact Halvard Home support to arrange an inspection, explaining when the vibration started and whether it continues after cooking. A technician will check the fan mounting and secure or replace it as needed.
