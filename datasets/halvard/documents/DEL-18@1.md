Orrin heat pump travel case notice

Stop using travel cases from batch 6453 with the Orrin heat pump, and return them for a refund.

Check the batch label on your travel case to see whether it is affected. If the label is missing, damaged or unclear, please contact Halvard Home customer support for advice before using the case again.

Keep the affected case separate from your heat pump while arranging its return. Our customer support team can explain the returns process and help you request your refund. Please have the case label available when contacting us, if possible.

We apologise for the inconvenience and appreciate your cooperation. Thank you for helping us keep Halvard Home products safe to use.
