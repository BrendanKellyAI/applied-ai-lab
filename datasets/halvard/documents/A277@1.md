Fenwick cooker hood: power cut during use

If the power fails while your Fenwick cooker hood is running, the fan and any hood lights will stop working.

Set the hood’s fan and light controls to off. If the controls do not respond without power, switch off the hood at its wall isolator, if accessible. This helps prevent an unexpected restart when electricity returns.

Turn off your hob and pause cooking while extraction is unavailable. Open a window if it is safe to do so.

Once power is restored, switch the hood’s supply back on, then test the fan and lights.

If the hood still does not work, check whether other kitchen appliances have power. Do not remove covers or touch wiring. Contact Halvard Home support for further help.
