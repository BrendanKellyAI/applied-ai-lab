Connecting your Mira to home wi-fi

KX-0356 is the part number for Mira’s wi-fi module, which connects the vacuum to your router. A pairing fault can leave the module searching for your old network after you replace your router.

To reconnect your Mira:

1. Connect your phone to your home’s 2.4 GHz wi-fi network. Mira cannot connect to a 5 GHz-only network.
2. Place the vacuum on its charging dock near the router.
3. Open the Halvard Home app, select your Mira and choose **Wi-fi settings**, then **Change network**.
4. Enter your new wi-fi password and follow the pairing instructions.

If pairing still fails, restart your router and try again. Contact Halvard Home support if Mira keeps searching for the old network; do not open the vacuum.
