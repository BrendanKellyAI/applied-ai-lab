Connecting your Ashby tumble dryer to wi-fi

Connect your Ashby tumble dryer to a 2.4 GHz home wi-fi network. Your phone should be connected to the same network during setup.

1. Switch on the dryer and open the Halvard Home app on your phone.
2. Sign in, then select **Add appliance** and choose **Tumble dryer**.
3. Follow the app’s instructions to put the dryer into pairing mode.
4. Select your home network and enter its wi-fi password. Passwords are case-sensitive.
5. Keep your phone near the dryer until the app confirms the connection.

If setup fails, check the password and make sure the wi-fi signal reaches the dryer. Restart the app and try again. Avoid using a guest network that requires signing in through a web page.
