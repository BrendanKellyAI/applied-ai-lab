Corra induction hob not switching on

If your Corra induction hob has no lights and does not respond to touch, first check that its wall isolator switch is on. Make sure your hands and the control panel are dry.

Switch the hob off at the wall for five minutes, then switch it back on and try the power control again.

A failed internal power supply module can leave the hob completely unresponsive. KX-2464 is the replacement part number for this module in the Corra induction hob.

If the hob still will not switch on, leave it isolated and contact Halvard Home support. Ask for a qualified technician to check the power supply module. Do not open the hob or attempt to replace the module yourself.
