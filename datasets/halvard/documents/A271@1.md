Burning smell from your Brio kettle

If your Halvard Home Brio kettle smells as though it is burning, switch it off and unplug it immediately, if safe to do so. Do not boil it again to test whether the smell has gone.

Leave the kettle to cool for at least 30 minutes before checking it. Look for visible damage, discolouration or melted plastic around the plug, cable and base. Do not touch damaged electrical parts.

Once everything is cool, check that the outside of the kettle and its base are dry. Never immerse the base in water.

Stop using the kettle and contact Halvard Home support for advice before using it again. Describe when the smell started and whether you noticed smoke or unusual heat.
