Using the Vale child lock

The child lock on your Halvard Home Vale fridge freezer prevents accidental changes to the temperature and other control panel settings. It does not lock the doors.

To switch the child lock on, press and hold the lock button for three seconds. A lock symbol on the display shows that it is active.

To unlock the controls, press and hold the same button for three seconds again. Wait for the lock symbol to disappear before changing a setting.

If the controls do not respond, check whether the lock symbol is still showing. Make sure your finger and the control panel are clean and dry, then try again.

Keep children supervised around the appliance, even when the child lock is on.
