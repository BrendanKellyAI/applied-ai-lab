Brio kettle display staying blank

If your Brio kettle’s display stays blank, check the power supply first. Make sure the plug is fully inserted and the wall socket is switched on.

Switch off the socket and unplug the kettle. Leave it unplugged for five minutes before trying again. While it is disconnected, check that the base and the underside of the kettle are dry. Never immerse the base in water.

Place the kettle firmly on its base, reconnect the plug and switch on the socket. If the display remains blank, try a different socket that you know works.

Stop using the kettle if the cable is damaged or you notice a burning smell. Contact Halvard Home support if these checks do not restore the display.
