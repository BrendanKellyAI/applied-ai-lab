Brio kettle: power cut during use

If a power cut interrupts boiling, switch off your Brio kettle at the socket and unplug it. Wait until the electricity supply is stable before trying again.

A sudden interruption can leave the kettle’s power switch stuck in the off position. To check, let the kettle cool for 10 minutes, then make sure the water is between the minimum and maximum marks. Plug it directly into a wall socket and try switching it on once.

If the switch will not stay on, stop using the kettle. KX-5607 is the replacement power switch module for this product. Contact Halvard Home support to arrange inspection and, if needed, replacement by a qualified technician. Do not open the kettle yourself.
