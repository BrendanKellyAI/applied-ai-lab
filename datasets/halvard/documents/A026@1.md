Strange noises from your Corra induction hob

A gentle hum or buzz during cooking is normal, especially at high power. However, a loud rattling or scraping sound needs attention.

On the Corra induction hob, E-1107 means the cooling fan cannot turn freely, usually because its airflow is obstructed. Stop cooking and switch the hob off at the wall isolator. Leave it switched off for at least 10 minutes.

Once the hob has cooled, check that accessible ventilation openings are clear of objects or loose debris. Do not remove covers or put anything into the vents.

Restore power and try the hob once. If the noise or warning returns, switch it off again and contact Halvard Home support. Do not continue using it.
