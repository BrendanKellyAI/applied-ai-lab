Recycling your old Vale fridge freezer

Your old Vale fridge freezer should be recycled through an authorised electrical waste service, not placed in a household rubbish bin.

Before collection, remove all food and unplug the unit from the wall socket. Let any ice melt naturally, then wipe the inside dry. Put towels around the base to catch water, and never use sharp tools to remove ice.

Contact your local council to ask about fridge freezer collections or an approved recycling centre. If you are buying a replacement, ask the retailer whether it offers an old-appliance collection service and whether charges apply.

Do not cut pipes or damage the cooling system: refrigerant needs specialist handling. Keep children away from the unit while it awaits collection.
