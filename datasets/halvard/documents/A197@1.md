App cannot find your Fenwick cooker hood

If the app cannot find your Fenwick cooker hood, try these steps:

1. Check that the hood has power and your phone is connected to your home Wi-Fi, rather than mobile data.
2. Move your phone within one metre of the hood while setting it up.
3. Turn the hood off at its wall switch for 30 seconds, then switch it back on.
4. Enable Bluetooth on your phone and allow any nearby-device or local-network permissions requested by the app.
5. Put the hood into pairing mode using the instructions in its user guide, then try adding it again.

If it still does not appear, restart your phone and router. Contact Halvard Home support if the problem continues.
