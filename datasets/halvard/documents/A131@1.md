Using your Fenwick cooker hood in a holiday home

Switch on the hood when you start cooking and leave it running for 10 minutes afterwards to help clear lingering steam and smells. Use a higher fan setting when frying or preparing food that produces more steam.

If the property has been empty for a while, check that the hood’s air openings are clear before use. With the hood switched off and cool, wipe accessible surfaces with a soft, damp cloth. Follow the supplied instructions for cleaning or replacing filters.

Leave guests a short note showing the fan and light controls, and remind them to switch both off after use.

Before closing the holiday home, check that the hood and its light are off.
