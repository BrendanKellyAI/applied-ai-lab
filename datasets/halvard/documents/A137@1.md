# Pair Mira with a voice assistant

Before you begin, check that your Mira robot vacuum is online in the Halvard Home app and that you can start a clean from the app.

Open your voice assistant’s app and find the section for adding smart home devices. Search for Halvard Home, select it, then follow the prompts to sign in to your Halvard Home account and allow access.

Use the same Halvard Home account that you used to set up Mira. Once linking is complete, ask your assistant to discover devices.

Give the vacuum a simple name, such as “Mira”, then try saying, “Start Mira.”

If Mira does not appear, check its internet connection, unlink Halvard Home in the assistant’s app, then link it again.
