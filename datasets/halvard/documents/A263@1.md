Water leaking underneath your Fenwick cooker hood

Water beneath your cooker hood may be condensation: steam from cooking cools on the hood’s surfaces and turns into droplets.

If water is dripping, stop cooking and switch off the hood. If you can safely reach a dry socket or isolator, disconnect its power before wiping the underside. Do not touch wet switches or electrical connections.

Once the hood is cool, check whether the grease filters need cleaning. Follow the cleaning instructions in your user manual and let the filters dry fully before refitting them.

If the leak continues, occurs when you are not cooking, or appears to come from inside the hood, leave it switched off and contact Halvard Home support. Do not remove electrical covers or attempt internal repairs.
