Mira robot vacuum not found in the app

If the Halvard Home app cannot find your Mira robot vacuum, check that the vacuum is switched on and placed near your router.

Connect your phone to a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network before starting setup. A 5 GHz-only connection will not work for pairing. If your router shows separate network names, choose the one marked 2.4 GHz.

Turn on Bluetooth on your phone and allow the app access to nearby devices when prompted. Close and reopen the app, then try adding the vacuum again.

If it still does not appear, restart your phone and router, then repeat setup. Avoid a factory reset unless Halvard Home support advises it. Contact support if these checks do not help.
