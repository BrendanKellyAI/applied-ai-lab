Replacing the Mira’s main brush

Part number KX-5377 identifies the replacement main roller brush for the Halvard Home Mira robot vacuum.

If your Mira leaves a strip of dust behind despite an empty dust bin, check the main brush. Split or flattened bristles can stop it lifting dirt properly. Replace the brush if the bristles are damaged.

Switch off the vacuum and remove it from its charging dock. Place it upside down on a soft cloth, release the two clips on the brush cover and lift out the old brush.

Remove any trapped hair, then fit the replacement brush with its ends seated securely. Refit the cover until both clips click shut.

Turn the Mira upright and run a short clean to check that it picks up dust evenly.
