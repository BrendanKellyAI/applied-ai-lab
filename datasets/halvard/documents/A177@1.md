Strange noises from your Keel washing machine

A rattling or banging noise can come from loose objects, an uneven load or a machine that is not level.

Pause the programme and wait until the drum has stopped and the door unlocks. Never force the door open. Spread heavy items evenly around the drum, then check for coins, keys or other loose objects.

If the machine rocks when gently pressed at a corner, switch it off and unplug it before adjusting its feet.

For a newly installed machine, check the installation guide to confirm that the transit bolts have been removed.

Stop using the machine if you hear grinding or notice a burning smell or leaking water. Switch it off and contact Halvard Home support.
