Corra hob not heating after a power cut

A power cut during cooking can leave the Corra induction hob’s power recovery control module stuck in its safety lock state. The display may light up when electricity returns, but the cooking zones will not heat.

KX-1060 is the part number for this control module, not a setting you need to enter.

Turn all cooking zones off, then switch off the hob at its wall isolation switch for two minutes. Restore power and select a cooking zone manually. Do not leave pans unattended while testing.

If the hob still will not heat, switch it off and contact Halvard Home support. Explain that the problem started after a power cut. Do not open the hob or attempt to replace the module yourself.
