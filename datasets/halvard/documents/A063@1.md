Pairing your Ashby dryer with a voice assistant

Part number KX-9996 identifies the voice connection module in your Ashby tumble dryer. A pairing fault can leave this module stuck in setup mode, so your voice assistant cannot find the dryer.

To reset the connection, stop any drying programme and switch the dryer off at the wall for 30 seconds. Switch it back on, then hold the Wi-Fi button for five seconds until its light flashes.

Open the Halvard Home app and follow the prompts to reconnect your dryer. In your voice assistant’s app, enable Halvard Home, link your account and search for devices again.

If the dryer still does not appear, contact Halvard Home support. Do not open the casing or try to replace the module yourself.
