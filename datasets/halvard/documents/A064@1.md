Resetting your Vale fridge freezer

KX-9969 is the control panel module in your Vale fridge freezer. A stored-settings fault in this module can leave Holiday mode active even after you switch it off. Resetting to factory settings clears the saved preferences and may resolve this fault.

To reset:
1. Keep the appliance connected to the power supply.
2. Press and hold the Settings button for 10 seconds, until the display flashes.
3. Release the button and wait one minute before using the controls.

The reset restores the fridge setting to 4°C and the freezer setting to −18°C. Actual temperatures will take time to adjust.

If Holiday mode remains active, contact Halvard Home support. Do not remove or replace the control panel yourself.
