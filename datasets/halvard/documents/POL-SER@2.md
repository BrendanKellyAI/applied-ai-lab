# Servicing and engineer visits

This policy explains how Halvard Home manages servicing and engineer visits in customers’ homes. It covers requesting a visit, assessing a reported fault, arranging access, carrying out work safely and explaining the outcome. It also sets out the responsibilities of customers, engineers and service partners, including how concerns about a visit should be raised and handled.

The policy is for customers who request support from Halvard Home, people acting on their behalf, and colleagues or authorised service partners involved in arranging or delivering that support. It is intended to help everyone understand what to expect and how to prepare for an engineer’s attendance.

The policy describes our approach to respectful conduct, accessibility needs, protection of the home and personal information, and decisions about further work. It should be read alongside the terms that apply to your service arrangement and does not affect your statutory rights.

## General terms

### Booking a visit

You can request an engineer visit by contacting Halvard Home through the contact options on our website. Tell us what needs attention, describe the fault and explain whether it affects safe use. We may ask questions to help us understand the problem and arrange suitable support.

Please provide your name, contact details and the address where the engineer is needed. Let us know about access restrictions, parking arrangements or anything else that could affect the visit. If you need support with communication or access, tell us when booking so we can discuss suitable arrangements with you.

We may suggest checks that could resolve the issue without a visit. Only carry out checks that you understand and can perform safely. Do not remove protective covers, attempt repairs or continue using anything that appears unsafe. Tell us if you cannot follow the suggested checks.

Standard engineer visits are booked within 16 working days of a request. We will discuss availability with you and confirm the agreed booking using the contact details you provide. Your request is not a confirmed booking until you receive our confirmation. Please check the details and contact us if they are incorrect.

Each visit is given an arrival window of 5 hours. The arrival window will be included in your booking confirmation. We cannot guarantee arrival at a particular point within that window. If travel disruption or an earlier job affects the engineer’s arrival, we will contact you to explain and discuss the booking.

A responsible adult who can authorise access and discuss the fault must be present for the visit. Please make sure the engineer can reach the area safely and has space to work. Keep pets away from the work area and tell us about hazards that may require special arrangements.

We will explain whether the visit is covered by your guarantee or service agreement. If charges may apply, we will explain the basis of those charges before you agree to the booking. The engineer will seek your approval before carrying out chargeable work outside what has already been agreed.

Please contact us if you need to change or cancel your booking. Tell us if your contact details or access arrangements change. If the engineer cannot gain access or cannot work safely, the visit may need to be rearranged. We will explain any applicable charges before you agree to the booking.

Booking a visit does not guarantee that the fault will be resolved during attendance. The engineer may need to assess the issue, obtain parts or arrange specialist support. We will explain the outcome of the assessment and discuss what happens next, including whether a further booking is needed.

We use the information you provide to arrange and manage your visit in line with our privacy notice. Nothing in these booking terms limits your statutory rights. If you are unsure about the booking arrangements, please contact us so we can explain them before you agree to proceed.

### Charges

We will explain the charges that apply to your booking before you agree to an engineer visit. These may include a call-out charge, labour, parts and any specialist work needed. Prices quoted to you include VAT where it applies, unless we clearly state otherwise.

A call-out charge covers the engineer’s attendance and assessment of the reported fault. It may still be payable if you decide not to proceed with a repair. We will tell you whether this charge applies when you book, including where a repair may be covered by your warranty.

Labour after the first hour is charged at £55.00 an hour. The engineer will explain how labour charges are calculated and ask for your agreement before carrying out chargeable work. You can ask for clarification before deciding whether to proceed.

An estimate reflects the information available when we assess the work. If the engineer finds that the repair needs different parts or further work, we will explain the revised charges and seek your approval. We will not carry out that work without your agreement.

Parts fitted outside the warranty carry a handling charge of 28%. We calculate this charge from the price of the parts and show it separately on your bill. We will explain the parts charges before asking you to approve the repair.

Where a fault is covered by your warranty, we will apply the warranty terms to the visit and repair. If we believe a charge is payable because the fault is not covered, we will explain why and give you the opportunity to discuss our assessment.

A charge may apply if the engineer cannot safely access the property or the place where the work is needed. We will explain any access requirements when arranging your visit. Please tell us about anything that could prevent the engineer from carrying out the agreed work.

If you need to cancel or rearrange your booking, please contact us. Any cancellation charge must have been explained when you booked and will reflect reasonable costs incurred. We will explain the reason for the charge rather than applying it without an explanation.

Payment is due as agreed when you book or approve the work. We will confirm the available payment methods and provide a bill describing the charges. Please do not make payments to an engineer unless we have confirmed that they are authorised to collect payment.

If you believe your bill is incorrect, contact us using the details on your booking confirmation. Tell us which charge you are questioning and why. We will review the bill, explain our findings and correct any error. These charging terms do not affect your statutory rights.

### Cancelling a visit

Cancel a visit at least 56 hours before it is due to avoid a charge. A visit cancelled later than that is charged at £47.00.

If you need to cancel, please contact Halvard Home using the contact details in your booking confirmation. Tell us that you want to cancel and provide enough information for us to find your booking, such as your name and the address where the engineer was due to attend.

We use the date and time we receive your cancellation request to decide whether the cancellation charge applies. You do not need to wait for us to reply for your request to count as received, provided it reaches the contact channel shown in your booking confirmation.

Please make your request clear. Asking a question about your booking does not cancel it. If you are unsure whether you still need the visit, we can help you understand your options, but the booking remains in place unless you ask us to cancel it.

We will confirm your cancellation using the contact details held with your booking. Please keep that confirmation for your records. If you do not receive it, contact us so that we can check the booking status. We will not charge you simply because our confirmation was delayed.

If you would prefer to rearrange the visit, please tell us rather than cancelling without explanation. We will explain the available options and whether moving the booking would attract the cancellation charge before making the change. A replacement appointment is subject to engineer availability.

If someone else is contacting us on your behalf, we may need to check that they have your permission to cancel. This helps us protect your booking and personal information. Please make sure they have the details needed for us to identify the visit.

Where a cancellation charge applies, we will explain why and tell you how payment will be handled. If you have already paid towards the visit, we will explain how the cancellation affects that payment and whether a refund is due.

We understand that unexpected circumstances can make it difficult to keep an appointment. If this happens, please contact us and explain your situation. We will consider the circumstances fairly, including any accessibility needs, and let you know whether we can waive the cancellation charge.

You will not be charged for a cancellation made by Halvard Home. If we cannot carry out the visit as agreed, we will contact you to explain what has happened and discuss rearranging it or cancelling the booking.

If you believe a cancellation charge has been applied incorrectly, please contact us with your booking details and any relevant correspondence. We will review the information and explain our decision. Nothing in these cancellation terms limits your statutory rights.

## Brio kettle

### Servicing schedule

The Brio kettle should be serviced every 44 months. This schedule helps keep your kettle in good working order and gives an engineer an opportunity to check for wear, damage and faults that may not be obvious during everyday use.

Use the purchase date to work out when your kettle is due for its initial service. After a service has been completed, use the service date as the starting point for the next scheduled visit. Keep your purchase receipt and service records somewhere accessible.

If you do not know the purchase date or cannot find your service record, contact Halvard Home. We can help you check the information available and agree an appropriate service date. Do not assume that a previous owner arranged servicing if you bought your kettle used.

A reminder is sent 4 weeks before the Brio kettle is due for a service. Please keep your contact details up to date so that the reminder can reach you. You remain responsible for arranging a service if you do not receive it.

To arrange a visit, contact Halvard Home through our customer support service. Tell us that you would like to book a routine service for your Brio kettle. Please also mention any changes in its performance or any damage you have noticed.

A routine service of the Brio kettle takes about 135 minutes. Please allow the engineer access to the kettle throughout the appointment. Tell us when booking if access to your home is restricted or if you need adjustments to help you manage the visit.

A routine service of the Brio kettle costs £78.00. This covers the scheduled inspection and routine servicing work. If the engineer identifies a repair outside that work, we will explain what is needed and ask for your agreement before proceeding.

Before the engineer arrives, unplug the kettle, allow it to cool and empty it. Leave it with its base in a clear, accessible area. Have your service record available if you have it, and keep children and pets away from the work area.

The engineer will check the kettle’s condition and operation, carry out routine servicing and explain any findings. If the kettle needs further attention, the engineer will tell you whether it is safe to use and what action is recommended.

Do not wait for a scheduled service if the kettle leaks, gives off a burning smell or has visible damage to its cable, plug or casing. Stop using it and contact Halvard Home for advice. Unplug it only if you can do so safely.

Routine servicing does not replace the cleaning and care described in the user instructions. Follow that guidance between visits, and keep the engineer’s service notes with your records so they are available when you arrange future servicing.

### Common repairs

Common repairs to the Brio kettle include problems with heating, the switch, the lid and the filter. A kettle that leaks, cuts out unexpectedly or fails to switch off may also need attention. Contact Halvard Home if you are unsure whether the fault can be resolved through routine care or needs an engineer’s assessment.

Stop using the kettle if it leaks, smells of burning, has a damaged lead or shows signs of electrical damage. Disconnect it from the mains if it is safe to do so. Do not open the casing, alter the plug or attempt an electrical repair yourself. Keep the kettle disconnected until we advise that it is safe to use.

If the kettle is noisy, heats poorly or pours unevenly, limescale or a blocked filter may be responsible. Follow the cleaning and descaling instructions supplied with your kettle. Let it cool before cleaning, and keep the base, plug and electrical connections dry. Do not use sharp tools to remove deposits or force a stiff lid or switch.

Replace the filter of the Brio kettle every 18 weeks. Use a compatible replacement supplied or approved by Halvard Home, and follow the fitting instructions. If the filter does not sit securely, stop using the kettle and contact us. Damage to the filter housing may need assessment rather than a replacement filter.

When requesting a repair, describe the fault and tell us about visible damage, unusual smells or leaking. We may ask for proof of purchase and details from the product label. This helps us check the support available and decide whether an engineer visit is suitable. Do not reconnect an unsafe kettle to demonstrate a fault.

We will explain the proposed repair and any applicable charges before asking for your agreement. The engineer will inspect the kettle and confirm whether the work can proceed safely. If the inspection reveals a different fault or changes the proposed work, we will explain the revised recommendation and seek your approval before proceeding.

Paid repairs to the Brio kettle come with their own guarantee. Repairs are guaranteed for 122 days. This guarantee covers the work carried out and the parts fitted as part of that repair. Contact us if the repaired fault returns, and keep your repair confirmation. The repair guarantee does not affect your statutory rights.

Spare parts for the Brio kettle are stocked for 9 years after the model is discontinued. Availability of a suitable part will be checked when we assess your request. We will tell you if a required part is unavailable and explain the options rather than fit an unsuitable substitute.

If the kettle cannot be repaired safely, we will explain why and discuss the appropriate next steps. The engineer may advise against further use. Follow that advice and keep the kettle disconnected. We can also provide guidance on responsible disposal of electrical appliances.
