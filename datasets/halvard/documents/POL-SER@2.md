# Servicing and engineer visits

This policy explains how Halvard Home manages servicing and engineer visits in customers’ homes. It covers requesting a visit, assessing a reported fault, arranging access, carrying out work safely and explaining the outcome. It also sets out the responsibilities of customers, engineers and service partners, including how concerns about a visit should be raised and handled.

The policy is for customers who request support from Halvard Home, people acting on their behalf, and colleagues or authorised service partners involved in arranging or delivering that support. It is intended to help everyone understand what to expect and how to prepare for an engineer’s attendance.

The policy describes our approach to respectful conduct, accessibility needs, protection of the home and personal information, and decisions about further work. It should be read alongside the terms that apply to your service arrangement and does not affect your statutory rights.

## General terms

### Booking a visit

You can request an engineer visit through Halvard Home’s customer service team or booking service. Tell us what has happened and describe the fault clearly so we can assess whether a visit is appropriate.

We may ask you to carry out safe checks before arranging an appointment. These checks can help identify the cause of the problem. Do not remove covers, interfere with wiring or attempt repairs yourself.

If you believe the appliance is unsafe, stop using it and tell us when you contact us. Follow the safety instructions supplied with it. Do not continue using it simply because a visit has been booked.

To arrange a visit, we need your name, contact details and the address where the appliance is installed. We may also ask for proof of purchase and information about your warranty or service cover.

Please make sure the details you provide are accurate. Tell us about access restrictions, parking arrangements or anything at the property that could affect the engineer’s ability to attend or work safely.

Standard engineer visits are booked within 16 working days of a request. We will discuss available appointments with you and confirm the booking arrangements through your chosen contact method, where that method is supported.

Each visit is given an arrival window of 5 hours. The engineer may arrive at any point within that window. We cannot promise a particular arrival time unless we expressly confirm this when booking.

A request for a visit is not a confirmed appointment. Your appointment is confirmed when we send you a booking confirmation or our customer service team expressly confirms it with you.

Check your confirmation carefully and contact us if the address, contact details or appointment arrangements are incorrect. Please also tell us if your circumstances change after the booking has been confirmed.

We may contact you to clarify the fault or discuss preparations for the visit. Please use contact details where we can reach you and check messages relating to your appointment.

An adult who can authorise access and discuss the fault must be present. If you arrange for someone else to attend, make sure they understand the problem and are authorised to agree to proposed work.

The engineer must be able to reach the appliance safely. Clear the surrounding area and keep pets away from the work space. Tell us beforehand if moving the appliance requires special arrangements.

Please explain any accessibility requirements when requesting your appointment. We will discuss reasonable adjustments with you and explain what we can arrange. You do not need to share medical information that is unrelated to those arrangements.

If you rent your home, check whether you need permission from your landlord or property manager before booking. You are responsible for obtaining permission for access and for work affecting the property.

We will explain whether the visit is expected to be covered by your warranty or service agreement. Booking an appointment does not itself confirm that the fault or the proposed repair is covered.

If a charge may apply, we will explain the basis of that charge before you agree to the booking. If the engineer identifies work outside the agreed scope, we will seek your approval before proceeding.

An engineer visit does not guarantee that a repair can be completed during the appointment. The engineer may need to investigate the fault, obtain suitable parts or arrange specialist support before work can proceed.

If you need to change or cancel your appointment, contact us using the details in your booking confirmation. Please give reasonable notice so we can update the engineer’s schedule and discuss alternative arrangements where needed.

Tell us if the fault disappears before the visit. We can discuss whether attendance is still appropriate. Do not assume the booking has been cancelled unless we have confirmed this to you.

If the engineer cannot gain access, or the property is not safe to work in, the visit may not proceed. We will explain what needs to change before a replacement appointment can be arranged.

We may need to change an appointment because of engineer availability, travel disruption or circumstances outside our control. If this happens, we will contact you, explain the position and discuss a replacement booking.

We use the information provided for your booking to arrange attendance and manage the service request, in line with our privacy notice. Nothing in these booking terms limits your statutory consumer rights.

### Charges

We will explain the charges that may apply to your engineer visit before you confirm your booking. These may include attendance, assessment, labour and replacement parts. Your booking confirmation will set out the agreed charges and explain which work they cover. Prices include VAT where applicable.

Whether you need to pay depends on your warranty cover, the reason for the visit and the work required. We may ask for proof of purchase or other information to check your cover. If we cannot confirm it, we will explain your options before arranging chargeable work.

An attendance or assessment charge may apply even if you decide not to proceed with a repair. We will tell you about this when you book. The charge covers the engineer attending and investigating the reported fault, rather than a guarantee that a repair will be possible.

Labour after the first hour is charged at £55.00 an hour. We will explain how the labour charge is calculated and recorded before you agree to chargeable work. The engineer will seek your approval if the expected labour cost changes as the assessment or repair progresses.

Parts fitted outside the warranty carry a handling charge of 28%. We calculate this charge on the price of the replacement parts, not on labour or attendance charges. Your quotation and invoice will show the handling charge separately so that you can see how it affects the total.

Where we can identify the work needed from the information you provide, we may offer a quotation before the visit. Otherwise, the engineer will assess the fault and explain the proposed repair and its cost. You can ask for clarification without agreeing to the work.

An estimate is a guide based on the information available, while a quotation states the price for the work described. We will make clear which we are providing. If the scope of the work changes, we will explain the reason and ask you to approve any revised price.

You do not have to accept a proposed repair. If you decline, you will only need to pay charges that were clearly explained and agreed, such as an applicable attendance or assessment charge. We will not charge for replacement parts that you have not authorised us to fit.

Work covered by your warranty will be handled under its terms. If an assessment shows that the fault is not covered, the engineer will explain why and discuss any charges with you. We will not treat your request for a warranty visit as permission to carry out chargeable repairs.

Please tell us about access restrictions, parking arrangements or anything else that could affect the visit. If these are likely to create an additional cost, we will discuss it with you. Expenses that were not disclosed and agreed will not be added to your bill without your consent.

If the engineer cannot gain access or cannot safely carry out the agreed work, a charge may apply where this was explained in your booking terms. We will consider the circumstances, including whether the problem was within your control, and explain the basis of any charge we propose.

If you need to change or cancel your booking, please contact us using the details in your confirmation. Any cancellation charge must have been made clear when you booked. We will explain how it applies to your circumstances and take account of any legal right you have to cancel.

We will not charge you for a visit we cancel or for work needed solely to put right an error we have made. If you have already paid for work that we cannot provide, we will explain the refund due and arrange it through an appropriate payment method.

Your invoice will describe the work carried out and show the charges clearly. Payment arrangements will be explained when you book or approve the repair. Please use an accepted payment method and ask for a receipt if you need confirmation that your payment has been received.

If you think a charge is wrong, contact us with your booking details and explain your concern. We will review the agreed work, the engineer’s record and the invoice, then explain our decision. These charging terms do not limit your statutory rights or prevent you from challenging an incorrect bill.

### Cancelling a visit

If you no longer need your engineer visit, please let Halvard Home know so that we can cancel the booking and release the appointment. Please contact us directly rather than relying on a message passed through someone who is not acting on our behalf.

Cancel a visit at least 56 hours before it is due to avoid a charge. A visit cancelled later than that is charged at £47.00.

The cancellation deadline is based on the scheduled start of your visit, as shown in your booking confirmation. If your confirmation does not make this clear, please contact us so that we can explain which appointment details apply to your booking.

Use the contact details in your booking confirmation to request a cancellation. If you cannot find that confirmation, use the customer service contact details on the Halvard Home website. Please make clear that you want to cancel an engineer visit.

Give us the name on the booking, the address the engineer is due to attend and suitable contact details. Include any booking reference available to you. We may ask for further information if we cannot confidently identify the visit you want to cancel.

Please do not send payment card details or sensitive personal information with your cancellation request. We only need enough information to locate the booking, check that you are entitled to change it and confirm what you have asked us to do.

We treat a cancellation request as received when it reaches the contact channel you have used, provided that the request clearly identifies the booking and asks us to cancel it. An unanswered call does not cancel your visit, so please use a channel that lets you submit your request.

We will confirm that your visit has been cancelled using the contact details held with your booking. Our confirmation will explain whether a cancellation charge applies. Please check that the confirmation refers to the correct address and appointment.

If you do not receive confirmation, contact us to check that your request has reached us. Keep a copy of any message you send and any acknowledgement you receive. These can help us resolve a disagreement about when you asked to cancel.

A delay in our handling of a clear cancellation request will not change when we received it. If you believe a charge has been applied because of an administrative error, tell us and provide any relevant confirmation or correspondence you have.

Someone else may request cancellation on your behalf if you have authorised them to manage the booking. We may need to check their authority before sharing booking information. Please make sure they have the details needed to identify your appointment.

If your landlord, managing agent or another organisation arranged the visit, contact them about cancellation unless your booking confirmation tells you to deal with us directly. A message to the person who arranged the visit is not necessarily a cancellation request received by Halvard Home.

If you want to rearrange rather than cancel, please say so when you contact us. We will explain the available options and any applicable terms before making the change. Do not assume that asking about availability has cancelled your existing booking.

Please also tell us if access to the property has become difficult or if the engineer may be unable to carry out the planned work. We can discuss whether the visit should go ahead. Reporting an access problem does not itself cancel the appointment.

If we cancel your visit, we will not apply a customer cancellation charge. We will contact you using the details on your booking and explain how to arrange a replacement appointment if you still need an engineer.

Where a cancellation charge applies, we will explain how payment will be collected or requested. We will not describe the charge as payment for work completed at your home. It relates to the cancellation of the booked visit.

If you have already paid towards the visit, we will explain how cancellation affects that payment and whether a refund is due. Any refund or deduction will be set out clearly so that you can understand what has happened to your payment.

Please contact us if you think we have applied these terms incorrectly or if you need help making a cancellation request. We will review the booking information and explain our decision. These cancellation terms do not limit any rights you have under consumer law.

## Brio kettle

### Servicing schedule

The Brio kettle should be serviced every 44 months. This schedule applies to normal household use. Servicing helps keep the kettle in safe working order and gives an engineer an opportunity to check for wear, damage and faults that may not be obvious during everyday use.

We calculate the service due date from the delivery date recorded on your account. After a completed routine service, we calculate the next due date from the date of that visit. If your records are missing or incorrect, contact Halvard Home so we can check them with you.

A reminder is sent 4 weeks before the Brio kettle is due for a service. We use the contact details held on your account, so please tell us if these change. The reminder invites you to arrange a visit; it does not mean an appointment has already been booked.

You remain responsible for arranging servicing even if a reminder does not reach you. You can contact Halvard Home to check when your kettle is due or to discuss a suitable appointment. Please mention any access requirements or communication preferences when making the booking.

A routine service of the Brio kettle takes about 135 minutes. This is a guide rather than a guarantee. The engineer will explain if the condition of your kettle affects the planned work or if a fault means that a separate repair appointment is needed.

A routine service of the Brio kettle costs £78.00. This price includes the engineer’s routine inspection, standard service checks and assessment of the kettle’s condition. Repairs, replacement parts and work outside the routine service are not included unless we expressly confirm otherwise when you book.

We will explain any proposed additional work and its price before asking for your agreement. You do not have to accept a repair simply because an engineer recommends it. If you decline work needed for safe operation, the engineer will explain why the kettle should not be used.

To arrange a service, contact Halvard Home through our usual customer support channels. Have your purchase details available if you can, and describe any changes in how the kettle works. Tell us about leaking, unusual smells, unexpected switching behaviour or visible damage rather than leaving these concerns until the visit.

A service appointment is not a substitute for reporting a suspected safety fault. If the kettle appears unsafe, stop using it and disconnect it from the electricity supply if you can do so safely. Contact Halvard Home for advice before using it again.

Before the engineer arrives, make sure the kettle is unplugged, empty and cool. Leave it somewhere accessible, together with its base and any documents relevant to a reported fault. Do not dismantle it or attempt electrical repairs in preparation for the appointment.

Please provide a clear working area and keep children and pets away from the engineer’s tools and equipment. Someone able to describe the kettle’s condition and authorise any agreed work should be available. Let us know beforehand if there are restrictions on entering your home.

During the service, the engineer will inspect the kettle and its accessible components, check its controls and assess whether it operates safely. The work may include cleaning accessible areas where appropriate. Servicing does not cover damage caused by an unsafe electricity supply or unsuitable conditions in the home.

Routine care remains important between services. Follow the care instructions supplied with your kettle, including the guidance on cleaning and descaling. Avoid abrasive materials or cleaning products that the instructions do not recommend. Regular servicing does not replace this care or remove the need to report faults.

If you need to change or cancel your appointment, contact Halvard Home as soon as you know. We will explain the available arrangements and any applicable charge before confirming a replacement booking. If we need to change the visit, we will contact you to discuss alternatives.

After the visit, the engineer will explain the findings and provide a service record showing the work completed and any recommendations. Keep this with your purchase documents. We will update your service due date where the routine service has been completed.

An incomplete visit does not automatically reset the servicing schedule. If access, the kettle’s condition or an unresolved fault prevents completion, we will explain what needs to happen next. Nothing in this servicing schedule limits your legal rights if your kettle is faulty or does not meet the standards required by law.

### Common repairs

Common repairs to the Brio kettle include faults with the lid, switch, heating element and automatic shut-off. Leaks, damage to the power base and problems with the power connection may also need attention. We assess the reported fault before recommending a repair, replacement part or engineer visit.

Stop using the kettle if it leaks, smells of burning, gives an electric shock or has a damaged cable or plug. Switch off the electricity supply and unplug it if safe to do so. Do not handle a wet plug or base, and keep the kettle out of use until it has been assessed.

If the kettle does not heat, check that the socket is switched on and that the kettle is seated correctly on its base. Only make these checks if the kettle, cable, plug and base appear undamaged and dry. Do not open the casing or attempt to repair electrical components yourself.

Limescale can affect heating, cause unusual noise and interfere with normal operation. Follow the cleaning and descaling instructions supplied with your kettle, using a suitable descaling product as directed. Let the kettle cool before cleaning. Do not immerse the kettle or its power base in water.

Replace the filter of the Brio kettle every 18 weeks. Use a filter approved for this model and follow the fitting instructions. If the filter does not sit securely, do not force it into place. Contact us for advice rather than trying to alter the filter or its housing.

A lid that sticks, fails to close or opens during use needs attention. Do not hold the lid down while the kettle boils or use anything to keep it shut. If cleaning the accessible surfaces does not resolve the problem, stop using the kettle and contact us.

When you request a repair, describe what happens and whether you have noticed leaking, unusual smells or visible damage. We may ask for proof of purchase and photographs to help assess the fault. Do not plug in or operate an unsafe kettle to demonstrate the problem.

We will explain whether the fault can be dealt with through an approved replacement part, an engineer visit or an assessment at a service centre. An engineer visit is not required for every fault. The available route depends on the condition of the kettle and the work needed.

Before you agree to a paid repair, we will explain the proposed work and any charges that apply. Tell us if you need clarification before giving approval. If the assessment reveals additional work, we will seek your agreement before proceeding with work that changes the agreed price.

For an engineer visit, make sure the kettle and its base are accessible, cool and empty. Keep the surrounding area clear and dry. Tell us about access needs when arranging the visit so that we can discuss suitable arrangements and explain any limits on the service.

The engineer will inspect the kettle and decide whether it can be repaired safely. They may replace a faulty component rather than the complete appliance. If the repair cannot be completed during the visit, we will explain the reason and discuss the available options with you.

Spare parts for the Brio kettle are stocked for 9 years after the model is discontinued. Availability of a particular part may depend on stock and supply conditions. We will check availability before confirming work that needs a replacement component and tell you if a supply problem affects the proposed repair.

Paid repairs to the Brio kettle come with their own guarantee. Repairs are guaranteed for 122 days. This guarantee covers the work carried out and parts supplied as part of that repair. It does not replace your statutory rights or limit any separate rights you have under an applicable appliance guarantee.

If a repaired fault returns, contact us with your repair confirmation and a description of the problem. We will assess whether it relates to the guaranteed work and explain how it will be resolved. Avoid arranging further work without contacting us, as this may make the original fault harder to assess.

Accidental damage, misuse and faults unrelated to the repair are not covered by the repair guarantee. We will explain our findings if we believe a reported problem falls outside that cover. You can ask us to review the assessment if you disagree, and we will explain how to raise a complaint.
