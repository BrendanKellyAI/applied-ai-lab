Saving energy on your Corra induction hob

Choose the lowest power setting that keeps your food cooking as needed. Once water reaches the boil, turn the power down until it maintains a gentle simmer, rather than leaving the zone on high.

To reduce energy use:
- Keep a lid on saucepans where the recipe allows. This holds in heat, so you can use a lower setting.
- Use an induction-compatible pan with a flat base that matches the cooking zone.
- Heat only the amount of water you need.
- Avoid heating empty pans or preheating longer than your recipe requires.
- Switch each zone off as soon as cooking is finished.

If cooking slows too much, increase the power gradually rather than immediately selecting the highest setting.
