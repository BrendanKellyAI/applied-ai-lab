Corra induction hob: fault on the display

If your Corra induction hob shows a fault message, stop cooking and switch off the hob using its controls. Remove pans only when it is safe to do so.

Check that the control area is clean and dry, with no cloths, utensils or spills covering it. Allow hot surfaces to cool before wiping them.

To try a reset, switch off the hob at its wall isolator for 60 seconds, then restore power. Only do this if the isolator is safely accessible.

If the message returns, leave the hob switched off and contact Halvard Home support. Note exactly what the display shows and when the fault appears. Do not remove the glass or attempt internal repairs.
