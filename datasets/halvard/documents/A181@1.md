Updating your Vale’s firmware

Keep your Vale fridge freezer’s firmware up to date to receive the latest software improvements.

Before you start, make sure the appliance is connected to your home Wi-Fi and has a steady power supply. On the control panel, open **Settings**, select **Software update**, then choose **Check for updates**. If an update is available, follow the on-screen instructions to install it.

Leave the fridge freezer switched on throughout the update. Do not unplug it or restart it while installation is in progress. Keep the doors closed as much as possible to help maintain the temperature inside.

Wait for the control panel to confirm completion. If the update fails, check your Wi-Fi connection before trying again.
