Lumen thermostat display stays blank

If your Lumen smart thermostat display stays blank, start by checking its power supply. A loss of power can leave the display completely blank.

Try these checks in order:

- Tap the display once to check whether it is simply asleep.
- If your thermostat uses replaceable batteries, fit fresh batteries of the type shown inside the battery compartment. Match the positive and negative markings.
- If it is mains-powered, check whether the heating system’s power switch is on. Do not remove the thermostat cover or touch any wiring.

If the display remains blank, contact Halvard Home support and explain which checks you have completed. If you notice heat, a burning smell or damaged wiring, stop using the thermostat and seek help from a qualified electrician.
