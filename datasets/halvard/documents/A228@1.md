Brio kettle: frost or condensation

Condensation forms when warm steam meets a cooler surface. Small droplets around your Brio kettle’s lid after boiling can be normal.

Before cleaning, unplug the kettle and let it cool. Wipe the outside with a soft, dry cloth. Never immerse the kettle or its power base in water.

If frost appears after storage in a freezing location, leave the kettle unplugged in a dry room until all ice has melted and the kettle and base are completely dry. Do not use the kettle’s heating function to thaw it.

To reduce condensation, keep the kettle in a ventilated indoor space and avoid filling above the maximum mark.

If water repeatedly collects underneath, stop using the kettle and contact Halvard Home support.
