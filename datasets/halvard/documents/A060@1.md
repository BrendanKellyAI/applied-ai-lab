Orrin heat pump warranty period

Your Halvard Home Orrin heat pump has a five-year warranty from the installation date, covering manufacturing faults in parts and labour.

If the display shows E-7160, the heat pump’s warranty record is missing its installation date, so the system cannot confirm the remaining warranty period. This is a registration fault, not a heating fault, and does not automatically mean your cover has expired.

Find your installation certificate or dated installer’s invoice, then contact Halvard Home support to have the warranty record corrected. Keep a copy of the document for future claims.

You can continue using the heat pump normally while the record is updated. Do not change the installation date yourself or reset the unit to clear the message.
