Tessa dishwasher door will not close

On your Halvard Home Tessa dishwasher, E-4875 means the door latch has not fully engaged, so the machine cannot start a wash.

Switch the dishwasher off before checking the door. Pull out both baskets and reposition any plates, pan handles or utensils sticking beyond their edges. Push the baskets fully back into place: a basket left slightly forward can prevent the door from reaching the latch.

Check the rubber door seal for trapped food and wipe it with a soft, damp cloth. Then close the door gently until you hear a click.

Do not slam the door or force the latch. If the door still will not click shut, leave the dishwasher switched off and contact Halvard Home support for a repair.
