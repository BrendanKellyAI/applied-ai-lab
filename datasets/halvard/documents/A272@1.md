Ashby tumble dryer: using delay start

Delay start lets you postpone the beginning of a drying programme. A delay of 3 hours means the dryer waits 3 hours before starting; it does not mean your clothes will be dry in 3 hours.

To set a delay, load the drum, close the door and select your drying programme. Use the delay start control to choose the waiting time, then press Start to confirm.

If the dryer does not begin tumbling straight away, check whether a delay is active before assuming there is a fault. The waiting time and drying time are separate.

Only use delay start when you will be at home and awake throughout the drying cycle.
