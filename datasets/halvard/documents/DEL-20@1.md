Corra cleaning gel pouch notice

Stop using cleaning gel pouches from batch 2046 with the Corra induction hob, and return them for a refund.

Halvard Home is recalling these pouches because they may damage the hob’s surface. Check the batch details on the packaging and keep affected pouches away from children and pets.

Please keep the pouches in their original packaging and contact Halvard Home customer support for return instructions.

If you have already used an affected pouch, let the hob cool before wiping it with a soft, damp cloth. Do not use abrasive cleaners.

We apologise for the inconvenience and thank you for your cooperation.
