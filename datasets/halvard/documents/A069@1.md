Ashby tumble dryer: frost or condensation build-up

Heavy condensation or frost inside your Ashby tumble dryer may be caused by a split condenser housing seal. This fault lets damp air reach cold internal surfaces, where moisture collects and may freeze in an unheated room.

KX-5912 is the replacement condenser housing seal for this product. If moisture repeatedly collects beneath the dryer or frost returns after thawing, stop using the appliance and contact Halvard Home support to arrange inspection and seal replacement if needed.

Switch off and unplug the dryer before wiping any accessible moisture. Leave it unplugged until all visible frost has thawed. Do not remove casing panels or use a hairdryer to melt ice. A technician should replace the seal before you use the dryer again.
