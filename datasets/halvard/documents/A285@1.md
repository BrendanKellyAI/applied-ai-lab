Brio kettle heating slowly

If your Brio kettle takes longer than usual to boil, limescale build-up may be reducing heat transfer to the water.

Unplug the kettle and let it cool before checking inside. If you see chalky deposits, use a descaler suitable for kettles and follow the instructions on its packaging. Rinse the kettle twice with fresh water afterwards, then boil fresh water and discard it before making a drink.

For quicker boiling, fill only with the amount you need, keeping the water between the minimum and maximum marks. Make sure the lid is fully closed.

If heating remains slow after descaling, stop using the kettle and contact Halvard Home support. Do not open the base or attempt electrical repairs.
