Brio kettle: flashing warning light

A flashing warning light can mean the Brio kettle’s boil-dry protection has activated because there is too little water inside.

Switch the kettle off and unplug the base from the wall socket. Leave it to cool for at least 15 minutes before checking it. Do not touch the inside while it is hot.

Once cool, fill the kettle with fresh water between the minimum and maximum marks. Check that the lid is fully closed and the base and kettle underside are dry. Place the kettle securely on its base, reconnect the power and try again.

If the light keeps flashing, stop using the kettle and contact Halvard Home support. Do not open the base or attempt electrical repairs.
