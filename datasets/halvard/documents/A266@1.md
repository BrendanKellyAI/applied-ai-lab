Booking a Tessa dishwasher engineer visit

To book an engineer visit for your Tessa dishwasher, contact Halvard Home customer support and ask for a repair appointment.

Before you contact us, write down what happens when you start a wash. Tell us whether the dishwasher fills, drains or stops during the cycle. Have your address, contact number and proof of purchase ready, if available.

Customer support will discuss appointment availability and explain any call-out charges before you confirm the booking. Let us know about parking restrictions or difficult access to your home.

Before the engineer arrives, empty the dishwasher and clear the space in front of it. Do not pull out a built-in appliance yourself; leave it in place for the engineer.
