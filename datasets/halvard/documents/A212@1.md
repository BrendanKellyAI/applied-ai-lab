Replacing a Lumen thermostat wall plate

If your Lumen smart thermostat’s wall plate is cracked or no longer holds the thermostat securely, ask Halvard Home support for a compatible replacement. Do not glue a damaged plate back together or force the thermostat onto it.

Include two photos with your request: one showing the thermostat and one showing the damage. Only photograph damage you can see without removing covers or exposing wiring. This helps support identify the replacement you need.

Before any removal work, switch off power to the heating system at the consumer unit. If replacing the plate involves disconnecting wires, use a qualified installer.

Keep the thermostat somewhere dry while it is detached, and follow the fitting instructions supplied with the replacement.
