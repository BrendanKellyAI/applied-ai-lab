Flashing warning light on your Fenwick cooker hood

On the Fenwick cooker hood, a flashing warning light with E-0216 means the metal grease filter is blocked, restricting airflow. Clean the filter before using the hood again.

Switch the hood off at the wall and let it cool. Release the filter catch and carefully lower the filter. Soak it in warm water with washing-up liquid for 15 minutes, then gently brush away grease. Rinse thoroughly and leave it to dry completely before refitting it securely.

Restore power, then press and hold the light button for five seconds to reset the warning.

If the warning returns after cleaning, switch the hood off and contact Halvard Home support. Do not run the hood without its grease filter fitted.
