Booking an engineer visit

To arrange an engineer visit for your Ashby tumble dryer, contact Halvard Home customer support and ask to book a repair.

Before contacting us, write down the dryer’s serial number and a short description of the problem. Include when it started and whether the dryer still switches on. Have your address, contact number and proof of purchase ready, if available.

Ask the support adviser to confirm the appointment date, arrival window and any charges before you agree to the booking. Let them know about stairs, parking restrictions or other access needs.

On the day, clear the space around the dryer so the engineer can reach it safely. If you need to change the appointment, contact customer support as soon as possible.
