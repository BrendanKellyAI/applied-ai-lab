Moving your Mira robot vacuum

Before moving your Mira robot vacuum, stop cleaning, switch it off and empty the dustbin. Carry the unit level with both hands underneath it; do not lift it by the bumper.

KX-6507 is the removable transport clip that holds the Mira’s front bumper steady during travel. Fit it before packing the switched-off vacuum in its original box, then remove it before switching the vacuum on again.

If the Mira repeatedly reverses after transport, the clip may still be fitted, preventing the bumper from moving freely. Switch the vacuum off, remove the clip and gently check that the bumper presses in and springs back. If it remains stuck, contact Halvard Home support before using the vacuum.
