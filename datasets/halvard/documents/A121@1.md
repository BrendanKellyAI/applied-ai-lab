Using your Tessa dishwasher in a holiday home

A little preparation helps keep your Tessa dishwasher fresh between visits.

Before leaving, run a wash to clear any dirty dishes. Once the programme has finished, unload the dishwasher and allow the interior to cool. Clean the filter following the instructions in your user manual, then wipe the door seals with a soft, damp cloth.

If the property will be empty for an extended period, switch off the electricity supply and close the dishwasher’s water supply valve. Leave the door slightly open, where safe, to help moisture escape.

If the home could freeze, ask a qualified installer about draining the appliance before you leave.

On your return, restore both supplies and check for leaks before running a wash.
