Burning smell from your Ashby tumble dryer

Stop the dryer immediately if you notice a burning smell. Switch it off and unplug it from the wall socket, but only if it is safe to do so. Do not run another cycle to test it.

If there is smoke or flame, keep the dryer door closed, leave the property and call 999. Do not use water or try to move the appliance.

If there are no signs of fire, leave the dryer unplugged until it has cooled completely. You can then remove fluff from the lint filter and check that external air vents are clear.

A burning smell can indicate overheating or an electrical fault. Do not use the dryer again until a qualified appliance technician has checked it.
