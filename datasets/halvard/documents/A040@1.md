Saving energy with your Ashby tumble dryer

Choose Eco for everyday cotton loads and select Cupboard Dry rather than Extra Dry when you do not need additional drying. Spin washing thoroughly before loading, and clean the lint filter after every cycle to maintain airflow.

KX-0711 is the Ashby tumble dryer’s moisture sensor. It helps automatic programmes stop when your clothes reach the selected dryness level. A faulty sensor can make the dryer continue heating after the load is dry, wasting electricity.

If clothes are already dry but the programme repeatedly continues heating, stop the dryer and switch it off at the socket. Contact Halvard Home support to arrange a sensor check. Do not remove panels or attempt to replace the sensor yourself.
