Vale fridge freezer not switching on

On the Halvard Home Vale fridge freezer, E-9215 means the power control system has detected an unstable electrical supply and blocked startup to protect the appliance.

Switch off the wall socket and unplug the fridge freezer for 10 minutes. While it is unplugged, check the plug and cable for visible damage. If either is damaged, leave the appliance disconnected and contact Halvard Home support.

If there is no damage, check that the socket works using a lamp. Reconnect the fridge freezer directly to the wall socket, without an extension lead, and switch it on.

If the appliance still will not start, contact Halvard Home support. Keep the doors closed to help preserve the cold inside.
