Updating your Brio kettle’s firmware

If a firmware update stops before completion, the kettle’s wireless connection may have dropped. This can leave the app showing ‘Updating’ even though no progress is being made.

KX-3356 is the Brio kettle’s wireless control module, which receives firmware updates. If updating repeatedly fails, contact Halvard Home support to have this module checked; do not open the kettle yourself.

To retry the update:

- Let the kettle cool, empty it and place it on its powered base.
- Connect your phone to the same home Wi-Fi network as the kettle.
- Open the Halvard Home app, select your kettle, then choose ‘Firmware update’.
- Keep the kettle powered and your phone nearby until the app confirms completion.

Do not boil water during the update.
