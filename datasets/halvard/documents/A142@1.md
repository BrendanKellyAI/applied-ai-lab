Moving your Keel washing machine

Before moving your Keel washing machine, switch it off at the socket and unplug it. Turn off the water supply, then disconnect the inlet hose. Keep a towel and shallow tray handy to catch remaining water.

Drain any water from the machine using the instructions in your user manual. Empty the drum and leave it dry.

Refit the original transit bolts before transport, following your manual. These secure the drum and help prevent damage. If the bolts are missing, contact Halvard Home before moving the machine.

Keep the machine upright and use two people to move it. Secure the hoses and cable without stretching them.

At its destination, remove the transit bolts before running a wash.
