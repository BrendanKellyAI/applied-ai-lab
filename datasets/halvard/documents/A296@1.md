Frost or condensation on your Lumen thermostat

Condensation forms when warm, damp air meets a cold surface. Moisture on your Lumen smart thermostat may indicate high humidity or a cold draught nearby.

Gently wipe the outside with a soft, dry cloth. Do not spray cleaner onto the thermostat, use a hairdryer or scrape away frost.

To reduce further build-up:
- Use an extractor fan when cooking or showering.
- Keep wet laundry away from the thermostat.
- Check for draughts around nearby windows and doors, without blocking ventilation grilles.

If frost returns, ask a qualified heating installer to check whether the thermostat’s position is suitable.

If moisture appears inside the display, or the controls stop responding, stop using the controls and contact Halvard Home support for advice.
