Connecting your Fenwick cooker hood to wi-fi

The Fenwick cooker hood connects to a 2.4 GHz home wi-fi network. It cannot connect to a network set to 5 GHz only.

Before you start, have your wi-fi password ready and connect your phone to the 2.4 GHz network.

1. Switch on the cooker hood at the mains.
2. Open the Halvard Home app on your phone and select **Add appliance**.
3. Choose **Cooker hood**, then follow the app’s instructions to put the hood into pairing mode.
4. Select your home network and enter its password.
5. Keep your phone near the hood until the app confirms the connection.

If pairing fails, check the password and make sure your router’s 2.4 GHz network is enabled, then try again.
