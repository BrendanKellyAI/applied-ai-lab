Lumen thermostat lid will not close

If the lid on your Lumen smart thermostat will not close, do not force it. Switch off power to your heating system at its isolation switch before checking the thermostat.

Look around the lid’s edges for dust, packaging or anything trapped in the gap. Wipe accessible edges with a dry, soft cloth. Do not touch wiring or use water inside the thermostat.

Check that the lid sits straight against the casing, then press gently along its edges. Stop if it needs firm pressure or springs open again.

If the lid or casing looks cracked, bent or loose, leave it alone and contact Halvard Home support. Do not use tape or glue to hold it shut.
