Ashby tumble dryer display stays blank

If your Ashby tumble dryer’s display stays blank, check its power supply first.

1. Make sure the plug is fully inserted and the wall socket is switched on. Use a wall socket directly, not an extension lead.
2. Check whether a circuit breaker has tripped at your consumer unit. If it trips again, stop using the dryer and contact a qualified electrician.
3. Switch the dryer off at the socket and unplug it for five minutes. Reconnect it, switch the socket on, then press the dryer’s power button.

If the plug or cable looks damaged, do not reconnect the dryer.

If the display remains blank, contact Halvard Home support. Do not remove any panels or attempt internal repairs.
