Cleaning the Mira robot vacuum filter

If your Mira robot vacuum leaves dust behind despite an empty dust bin, a clogged filter may be reducing suction.

Switch off the vacuum and remove it from its charging dock. Take out the dust bin, open its lid and lift out the filter. Empty the bin, then gently tap the filter over a rubbish bin to loosen trapped dust. Use a soft, dry brush to remove fluff from the filter surface. Do not wash the filter or refit it while damp.

Check for tears or damaged edges. If damaged, replace it with KX-8038, the replacement filter for the Mira robot vacuum. Refit the filter securely before closing the bin and restarting the vacuum.
