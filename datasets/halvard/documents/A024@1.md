Power cut during use

On the Orrin heat pump, E-5107 means mains power was interrupted while the compressor was running. The heat pump has paused operation to protect the compressor during restart.

When power returns, leave the heat pump connected and wait five minutes before trying to restart it. Then press the power button once on the controller and select your usual heating setting. Do not repeatedly switch the electricity supply off and on, as this can interrupt the restart delay.

If the message remains or heating does not resume, switch the heat pump off using its controller and contact Halvard Home support. Tell the adviser that the fault appeared after a power cut. Do not open the casing or attempt electrical repairs.
