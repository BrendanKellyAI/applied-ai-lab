Brio kettle: unusual vibration

If your Brio kettle vibrates unusually, switch it off and unplug the base before checking it. Allow the kettle to cool completely.

An uneven surface can make a kettle rock and amplify vibration. Place the base on a dry, flat, stable worktop, away from the edge. Check that the kettle sits securely on its base and that nothing is trapped underneath.

Check the water level against the kettle’s minimum and maximum marks. Do not run it below the minimum or fill it above the maximum.

If vibration continues after these checks, stop using the kettle and contact Halvard Home support. Stop immediately if you also notice leaking, a burning smell or visible damage. Do not open or attempt to repair the base.
