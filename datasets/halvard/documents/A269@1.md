Cleaning your Vale fridge freezer filter

Before cleaning the filter, switch off your Vale fridge freezer at the socket and unplug it. Keep the doors closed while you work to help food stay cold.

Check your user guide for the filter’s location and removal instructions. Do not force the cover or filter out.

If the filter is marked as washable, rinse it gently under lukewarm running water. Avoid detergent, bleach and abrasive brushes. Leave it to air-dry completely before refitting; do not use a hairdryer or radiator.

If it is a disposable filter, replace it rather than washing it. Water filter cartridges must not be cleaned and reused.

Refit the filter and cover securely, then plug the appliance back in and switch it on.
