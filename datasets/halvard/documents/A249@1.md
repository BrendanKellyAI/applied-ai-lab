Corra induction hob warranty

Your Halvard Home Corra induction hob comes with a two-year manufacturer’s warranty from the date of purchase. For example, if you bought your hob on 10 June 2026, its warranty runs until 10 June 2028.

The warranty covers faults caused by defective materials or workmanship during normal household use. It does not cover accidental damage, misuse or damage caused by incorrect installation.

Keep your receipt or order confirmation, as you will need proof of purchase when requesting a warranty repair. To make a claim, contact Halvard Home customer support with your purchase date and a brief description of the fault.

Your manufacturer’s warranty is in addition to your statutory rights and does not affect them.
