Mira side brush notice

Stop using side brushes from batch 1100 with the Mira robot vacuum, and return them for a refund.

We are recalling the affected side brushes as a precaution. Please check the batch marking on the packaging or brush label before using your Mira. If the marking matches the batch shown above, remove the brushes and keep them separate from your appliance. Contact Halvard Home customer support for help arranging your return and refund. If you cannot find the batch marking, or you are unsure whether your brushes are affected, please contact us before using them. We apologise for the inconvenience and appreciate your help.
