# Ashby tumble dryer: flashing warning light

A flashing warning light on your Ashby tumble dryer may mean that it needs attention before the programme can continue.

1. Stop the programme, switch off the dryer and unplug it. Allow the appliance to cool before checking it.
2. Remove the fluff filter and gently clear away any collected lint. Refit it securely before using the dryer.
3. If your model has a water tank, empty it and push it fully back into place.
4. Check that no clothing is trapped in the door, then close it firmly.

Reconnect the dryer and select your programme again. If the light keeps flashing, stop using the appliance and contact Halvard Home support. Describe which light is flashing and when it appears.
