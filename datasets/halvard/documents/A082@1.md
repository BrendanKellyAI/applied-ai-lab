Saving energy on your Mira

To reduce electricity use, open the Mira app, select Settings, then Power, and switch on Energy Saving. This setting reduces standby power after the battery finishes charging. Use Eco suction for everyday dust, and schedule cleaning only as often as your home needs it.

Once charging is complete, the robot’s power light should dim within 10 minutes. If it stays bright and the fan continues running, the standby power module may be faulty, preventing energy-saving mode from starting.

KX-5664 is the replacement standby power module for the Mira robot vacuum. If these symptoms persist after restarting the robot, contact Halvard Home support to arrange an inspection and replacement if needed. Do not open the robot or replace the module yourself.
