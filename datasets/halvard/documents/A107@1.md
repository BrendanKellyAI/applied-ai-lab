Ordering a replacement drying rack

If the removable drying rack in your Ashby tumble dryer wobbles because one of its feet has cracked, stop using the rack and order a replacement. A damaged rack may let items fall into the drum.

KX-1618 is the replacement drying rack accessory for the Ashby tumble dryer. To order it, contact Halvard Home customer support and quote this reference. Have your dryer’s model details ready so the team can confirm compatibility before you pay.

Switch off and unplug the dryer before removing the damaged rack. Do not try to repair a cracked foot with glue or tape. Keep the old rack out of use while you wait for the replacement.
