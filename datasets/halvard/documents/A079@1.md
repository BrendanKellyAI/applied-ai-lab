Timer or delay start will not run

On the Halvard Home Orrin heat pump, E-3496 means the internal clock has lost its time setting, so the unit cannot calculate when a timed or delayed start should begin. This can happen after a power cut.

To restore the timer:

1. Open Settings on the control panel and select Clock.
2. Set the current day and time, then press Save.
3. Cancel the existing timer or delayed start.
4. Enter a new start time and confirm it.

Choose a start time at least five minutes ahead when testing the timer. The heat pump should remain on standby until that time.

If the warning returns after saving the clock, contact Halvard Home support. Do not remove the unit’s casing.
