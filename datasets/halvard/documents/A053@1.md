Pairing your Lumen with a voice assistant

If your voice assistant cannot find your Lumen smart thermostat, its saved account link may have expired. This pairing fault prevents voice control, but you can still adjust the temperature on the thermostat.

KX-9491 is the part number for the thermostat’s wireless pairing module. You do not need to remove or replace it to refresh the account link.

To reconnect, open your voice assistant’s app and unlink your Halvard Home account. Wait 60 seconds, then link the account again and search for devices. Keep the thermostat connected to your home Wi-Fi throughout.

If pairing still fails, contact Halvard Home support and quote the module’s part number. Do not open the thermostat casing.
