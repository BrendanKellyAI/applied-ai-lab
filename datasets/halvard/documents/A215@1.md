Burning smell from your Mira robot vacuum

If your Mira robot vacuum smells of burning, stop cleaning immediately. Switch off the vacuum and unplug its charging dock from the wall socket, if it is safe to do so. Do not charge or run it again until the cause has been checked.

Once the vacuum is completely cool, check the brush and wheels for trapped hair, thread or debris. Remove only loose material you can reach without opening the casing. A jammed brush can put extra strain on the motor.

Do not use water, dismantle the battery or touch melted areas. Contact Halvard Home support before using the vacuum again. If there is smoke or fire, move away and call the fire service.
