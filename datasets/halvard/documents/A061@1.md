Booking an engineer for your Vale fridge freezer

On the Vale fridge freezer, E-7610 means the freezer temperature sensor has failed, so the appliance cannot reliably control freezer cooling. This fault needs an engineer visit; changing the temperature setting will not fix it.

Contact Halvard Home customer support to request a repair appointment. Have your appliance’s serial number, purchase date, postcode and a description of the fault ready. Tell the adviser whether frozen food has started to soften.

Keep the freezer door closed as much as possible. If food is softening, move it to another working freezer rather than waiting for the visit.

Before the engineer arrives, clear the area around the appliance so they can reach it safely. Do not remove any panels yourself.
