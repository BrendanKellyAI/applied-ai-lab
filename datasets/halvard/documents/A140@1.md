Using your Vale fridge freezer in a holiday home

Before leaving your holiday home, decide whether your Vale fridge freezer needs to stay running.

If you are keeping food inside, leave it switched on. Set the fridge to 4°C and the freezer to −18°C, and check that both doors close properly. Remove food that will expire before your next visit.

If you are emptying the appliance for a long absence, switch it off and unplug it before cleaning. Remove all food, allow any ice to thaw, and wipe the interior with warm water and mild detergent. Dry it thoroughly, then leave both doors ajar to help prevent smells and mould.

When you return, let the appliance reach its set temperatures before adding chilled or frozen food.
