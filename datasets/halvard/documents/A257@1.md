Orrin heat pump door or lid not closing

If the door or lid on your Halvard Home Orrin heat pump will not close, switch the unit off before checking it. Do not remove any service covers.

Check the closing edge for loose packaging, dirt or other visible obstructions. Packaging trapped between the lid and casing can prevent the latch from engaging. Remove only loose items that you can reach safely; do not move wires or internal components.

Gently close the door or lid, checking that it sits flush with the casing. Do not slam it, force the latch or tape it shut.

If the hinges look bent, the latch appears damaged or it still will not close, leave the unit off and contact Halvard Home support.
