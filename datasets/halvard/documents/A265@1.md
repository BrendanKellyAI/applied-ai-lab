Connecting your Lumen thermostat to wi-fi

To connect your Halvard Home Lumen smart thermostat, have your home wi-fi name and password ready. Use a 2.4 GHz network; the thermostat does not connect to a 5 GHz network.

On the thermostat, open Settings, select Wi-fi, then choose your home network from the list. Enter the password carefully, including any capital letters, and select Connect. A wi-fi symbol appears on the screen when the connection is complete.

If your network is not listed, check that your router is switched on and broadcasting its network name. If connection fails, check the password and restart your router before trying again.

Do not reset the thermostat as your first troubleshooting step, as this may remove your saved heating preferences.
