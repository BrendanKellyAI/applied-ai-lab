Burning smell from your Corra induction hob

If you notice a burning smell, turn off the hob and let it cool completely. Food spills, grease or packaging left near a hot pan can cause a burning smell.

Once the surface is cool, remove any cookware and check for residue. Wipe the glass with a soft, damp cloth and a cleaner suitable for induction hobs, then dry it thoroughly. Do not clean the surface while it is hot.

If the smell resembles burning plastic or electrics, switch off the hob at the mains if safe to do so. Do not use it again until it has been checked.

Contact Halvard Home support if the smell continues or has no obvious cause.
