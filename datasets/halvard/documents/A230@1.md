Brio kettle not switching on

If your Halvard Home Brio kettle will not switch on, try these checks:

1. Check that the plug is fully inserted and the wall socket is switched on. Test the socket with another appliance you know works.
2. Unplug the kettle before checking the base. Make sure the base and the underside of the kettle are dry, then place the kettle firmly on its base.
3. Check the water level. Fill between the minimum and maximum marks before trying again.
4. If the kettle recently boiled dry, unplug it and allow it to cool fully before refilling.

Stop using the kettle if the cable, plug or base looks damaged. If these checks do not help, contact Halvard Home support.
