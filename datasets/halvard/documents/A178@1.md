Resetting your Orrin heat pump

A factory reset returns your Orrin heat pump’s user settings to their original values. It removes saved heating schedules and temperature preferences, so note these down before you begin.

To reset:
1. Make sure the heat pump is switched on and not displaying a warning.
2. On the controller, open **Settings**.
3. Select **System**, then **Factory reset**.
4. Choose **Confirm** to start the reset.
5. Leave the power connected while the controller restarts.

When the home screen returns, check the date and time, then enter your preferred temperatures and heating schedule again.

If **Factory reset** is unavailable, or the controller does not restart, contact Halvard Home support. Do not open the unit or change installer settings to force a reset.
