Brio kettle lid not closing

If the lid on your Halvard Home Brio kettle will not close, switch the kettle off, unplug the base and let the kettle cool completely before checking it.

Empty the kettle, then look around the rim and underside of the lid for limescale or trapped debris. Wipe these areas with a soft, damp cloth. Do not immerse the kettle or base in water.

Check that nothing is blocking the hinge or catch, then gently lower the lid into place. Do not force it shut or try to bend the catch.

Do not use the kettle while the lid will not close securely. If cleaning does not help, contact Halvard Home support for advice.
