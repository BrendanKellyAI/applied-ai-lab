Mira lint filter notice

Stop using lint filters from batch 6263 with the Mira robot vacuum, and return them for a refund.

Please check the batch marking on your filter packaging before using your vacuum. If it matches, switch off the vacuum, remove the filter and keep it separate from household waste. Do not fit the affected filter again.

Contact Halvard Home customer support for guidance on returning the filter and receiving your refund. Please keep the packaging if available, as it may help us identify the affected product. If the packaging is missing or the marking is unclear, stop using the filter and ask our support team for advice. We are sorry for the inconvenience and appreciate your help.
