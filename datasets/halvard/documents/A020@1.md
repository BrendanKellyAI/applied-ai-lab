Timer will not start

On the Halvard Home Mira robot vacuum, E-6232 means the delay start has been cancelled because the vacuum’s internal clock is not set. This can happen after the battery has been completely drained.

To restore the timer, place the vacuum on its charging dock and leave it switched on. Open the Halvard Home app, select your Mira, then go to Settings > Date and time. Choose “Sync with phone” and save.

Next, open the timer menu and set a new start time at least five minutes ahead. The cancelled timer will not restart automatically.

If the message returns, switch the vacuum off for 30 seconds, switch it on again and repeat the clock sync. Contact Halvard Home support if the fault continues.
