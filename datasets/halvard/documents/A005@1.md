Lumen thermostat child lock

The child lock stops children changing the temperature using the Lumen smart thermostat’s screen. To switch it on, press and hold the padlock symbol for five seconds. A closed padlock confirms that the screen controls are locked. Repeat this step to unlock them.

If the lock switches off without anyone touching the screen, a faulty touch sensor may be registering an unlock command.

KX-1283 is the replacement touch-panel assembly for this product, including the sensor that detects child lock commands. Contact Halvard Home support to arrange inspection and, if needed, replacement. Do not open the thermostat yourself.

Until the fault is fixed, do not rely on the child lock to prevent temperature changes.
