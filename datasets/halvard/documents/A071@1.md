Recycling your old Keel washing machine

On the Keel washing machine, KX-5913 identifies the drain pump assembly. A cracked pump housing can cause water to leak beneath the machine during draining.

If you notice this fault, stop using the machine, turn off its water supply and unplug it only if the plug and surrounding area are dry. Ask a qualified repairer to assess it before deciding to recycle it.

If repair is not practical, book an electrical appliance collection through your local council or ask your retailer about removal when buying a replacement. Do not put the machine in household rubbish.

Before collection, remove all laundry and follow the user manual’s draining instructions. Arrange help with disconnection and lifting; do not move the machine alone.
