Child lock warning on your Orrin heat pump

On the Orrin heat pump, E-1294 means the child lock button has been detected as continuously pressed, so the control panel remains locked. Moisture or dirt on the touch panel can cause this warning.

To clear it:

- Remove anything resting against the control panel.
- Wipe the panel with a soft, dry cloth. Do not spray cleaner directly onto it.
- Make sure your hands are dry, then press and hold the child lock button for five seconds.

When the lock symbol disappears, the controls are available again. The heat pump keeps its previous heating setting while locked.

If the warning returns, contact Halvard Home support. Do not open the casing or attempt to repair the controls yourself.
