Cleaning the Ashby tumble dryer filter

On the Halvard Home Ashby tumble dryer, E-3837 means fluff has blocked the filter enough to restrict airflow. Clean the filter before starting another drying cycle.

1. Switch off the dryer, unplug it and allow it to cool.
2. Open the door and lift the fluff filter out of its slot.
3. Remove fluff from both sides using your fingers or a soft brush.
4. If residue remains, rinse the filter under lukewarm water. Do not use abrasive cleaners.
5. Let the filter dry completely, then refit it securely.

Reconnect the dryer and try a cycle. Never run it without the filter fitted. Clean the filter after every load to help prevent blockages. If the warning returns, stop using the dryer and contact Halvard Home support.
