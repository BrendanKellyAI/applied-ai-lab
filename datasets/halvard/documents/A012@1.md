Tessa dishwasher: water not draining

On your Halvard Home Tessa dishwasher, E-6076 means water has remained in the base for more than three minutes after draining started. A blocked filter or kinked drain hose is the usual cause.

Switch the dishwasher off and unplug it before checking anything. If the water is hot, leave it to cool. Place towels around the door, then remove the bottom basket. Lift out the filter, rinse it under running water and refit it securely.

Check any visible section of the drain hose for kinks, without pulling out the appliance or disconnecting pipes.

Restore power and run a drain programme. If the fault returns or water remains, stop using the dishwasher and contact Halvard Home support.
