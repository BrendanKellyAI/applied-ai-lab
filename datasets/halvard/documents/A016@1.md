Replacing a lower basket wheel

If your Tessa dishwasher’s lower basket tilts or catches when you pull it out, a cracked basket wheel may be the cause.

For the Halvard Home Tessa dishwasher, KX-6373 is the replacement lower basket wheel kit. Order this kit from Halvard Home support if a wheel is cracked or missing.

Switch off and unplug the dishwasher before fitting the replacement. Let the appliance cool, empty the lower basket, then lift it out onto a flat surface. Unclip the damaged wheel and press the new wheel onto the same mounting point until it clicks into place.

Refit the basket and slide it fully in and out twice. If it still catches, stop using it and contact support.
