Tessa dishwasher warranty period

Your Halvard Home Tessa dishwasher has a two-year warranty from the date of purchase. It covers manufacturing faults during normal household use, but not accidental damage or faults caused by incorrect installation.

E-4805 means the dishwasher’s digital warranty record is missing its purchase date, so your cover cannot be confirmed automatically. This does not mean your warranty has expired.

Find your receipt or order confirmation, then contact Halvard Home customer support with the purchase date and your dishwasher’s serial number. Support can check your proof of purchase and correct the warranty record.

You do not need to reset the dishwasher to resolve this record fault. Keep your proof of purchase for any future warranty claim.
