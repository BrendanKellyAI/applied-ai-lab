Water leaking underneath your Corra induction hob

Water beneath your Corra induction hob can be caused by a split perimeter seal. KX-9945 is the seal that sits between the hob’s glass edge and the worktop; if it splits, spilled water can pass into the cupboard below.

Switch the hob off and isolate its electricity supply at the consumer unit before wiping up any water. Use dry hands and do not touch wet switches or wiring. Do not use the hob again until it has been checked.

Once the surface is cool, wipe away standing water without lifting the hob or removing its covers. Contact Halvard Home support to arrange inspection and seal replacement by a qualified technician. Explain that water appears beneath the hob after spills.
