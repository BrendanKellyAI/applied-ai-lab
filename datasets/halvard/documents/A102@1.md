Brio kettle: first installation

Before using your Brio kettle, remove all packaging and place the power base on a dry, level worktop.

During first installation, E-5328 means the kettle is not seated correctly on its base. A protective packing insert left beneath the kettle can cause this fault.

To fix it, switch off the socket and unplug the base. Lift off the kettle, remove any remaining packing material and check that both contact surfaces are dry. Put the kettle squarely back on the base, then reconnect the power.

Rinse the kettle, fill it to the MAX mark with fresh cold water, boil once and discard the water before making drinks.

If the warning returns, unplug the kettle and contact Halvard Home support.
