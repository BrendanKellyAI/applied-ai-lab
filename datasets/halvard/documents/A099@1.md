Pairing your Brio kettle with a voice assistant

KX-3056 is the part number for the Brio kettle’s wireless pairing module. This module lets the kettle connect to the Halvard Home app so you can link a supported voice assistant.

A stalled pairing session can leave the kettle visible in the app but unavailable to your voice assistant, particularly after a router restart.

To reconnect:

1. Connect your phone to your home’s 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.
2. Hold the kettle’s pairing button for five seconds, until the wireless light flashes.
3. Open the Halvard Home app and add the kettle again.
4. In the app’s Voice assistants menu, select your assistant and follow the linking instructions.

If pairing still fails, contact Halvard Home support. Do not open the kettle or replace the module yourself.
