Resetting your Keel washing machine

A factory reset restores your Halvard Home Keel washing machine’s original settings and clears saved wash preferences. Make a note of any settings you want to use again before you begin.

1. Wait until the current programme has finished and remove your laundry.
2. Leave the machine switched on, with the drum empty.
3. Open **Settings** on the control panel.
4. Select **Factory reset**, then choose **Confirm**.
5. Allow the machine to restart before selecting another programme.

You will need to set your preferred options again after the reset.

If the control panel does not respond or the reset option is unavailable, contact Halvard Home support. Do not remove any covers or attempt to reset internal components yourself.
