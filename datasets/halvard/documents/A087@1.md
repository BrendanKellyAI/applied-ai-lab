Lumen thermostat: room sensor fault

If your Lumen smart thermostat displays KX-7896, it is flagging a fault with the room temperature sensor module identified by that part number. This means the thermostat cannot get a reliable temperature reading, so it pauses automatic heating control.

Switch off power to the thermostat at its labelled isolation switch for 60 seconds, then switch it back on. Wait two minutes for the display to restart and check whether the warning clears.

If the warning returns, contact Halvard Home support and describe what appears on the screen. Do not remove the thermostat cover or attempt to replace the sensor yourself. A qualified technician should inspect the module and its connections.
