Burning smell from your Keel washing machine

Stop using your Keel washing machine immediately if you notice a burning smell. Switch it off and unplug it, but only if you can do so safely. Do not touch a plug or socket that appears scorched, damaged or sparking.

A burning smell can indicate overheating or an electrical fault. Do not run another cycle to see whether the smell clears, and do not remove any panels or attempt repairs yourself.

Contact Halvard Home support to arrange an inspection before using the machine again. Describe when the smell started and whether you noticed unusual noise or smoke.

If there is smoke or fire, leave the property and call 999. Never use water on an electrical fire.
