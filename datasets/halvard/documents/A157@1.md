Brio kettle leaking underneath

If you notice water underneath your Brio kettle, switch it off and unplug it before checking. Let the kettle cool completely. If the plug or socket is wet, do not touch it; switch off the electricity supply at the consumer unit first.

Lift the kettle off its base and wipe the outside dry. Check whether water has run down from the spout or lid after filling or pouring. Filling above the MAX mark can cause water to spill, so always keep the water level below this line.

Look for cracks or water escaping from the bottom of the kettle. If leaking continues, stop using it and contact Halvard Home support. Do not immerse the electrical base or attempt repairs yourself.
