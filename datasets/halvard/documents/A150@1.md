Connecting your Vale fridge freezer to wi-fi

Your Vale fridge freezer connects to a 2.4 GHz wi-fi network. Before you start, connect your phone to that network and have your wi-fi password ready.

1. Open the Halvard Home app and sign in, or create an account.
2. Select ‘Add appliance’, then choose ‘Fridge freezer’.
3. Press and hold the fridge freezer’s wi-fi button for three seconds, until the wi-fi light flashes.
4. Follow the instructions in the app to select your home network and enter its password.
5. Wait for the app to confirm the connection.

If setup fails, check the password and make sure your router’s 2.4 GHz network is enabled. Move your router closer if the signal is weak, then try again.
