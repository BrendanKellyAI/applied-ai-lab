Orrin heat pump display stays blank

If your Orrin heat pump display stays blank, first check that the wall isolator is switched on and that your home’s circuit breaker has not tripped.

Switch the heat pump off at the wall isolator, wait 60 seconds, then switch it on again. If the circuit breaker trips again, leave the heat pump switched off and contact a qualified electrician.

A loose connection in the display power module can stop electricity reaching the screen. KX-1693 is the replacement display power module for this product.

If the display remains blank after restarting, contact Halvard Home support to arrange an inspection. A technician should check the connection and replace the module if needed. Do not remove the casing yourself.
