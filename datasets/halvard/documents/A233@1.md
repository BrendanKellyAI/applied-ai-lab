# Ashby tumble dryer: unusual vibration

If your Ashby tumble dryer vibrates more than usual, stop the programme and wait until the drum has stopped. Switch it off and unplug it before checking the appliance.

Check that the dryer stands firmly on a hard, level floor. All four feet should touch the floor, and the cabinet should not rock when gently pressed at opposite corners. Adjust the feet if needed.

Make sure the dryer is not touching nearby cupboards or other appliances. Remove anything resting on top.

An uneven load can also cause vibration. Untangle bulky items and spread them loosely around the drum before restarting.

If the vibration continues, or you hear scraping or banging, stop using the dryer and contact Halvard Home support.
