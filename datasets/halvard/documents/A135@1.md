Reset your Lumen smart thermostat

A factory reset clears your Lumen smart thermostat’s saved Wi-Fi details, heating schedules and personal settings. Try restarting the thermostat first if you only need to resolve a temporary connection problem.

Before resetting, note down any schedules you want to use again. Make sure you have your Wi-Fi password and access to the Halvard Home app.

To reset the thermostat:

1. Open **Settings** on the thermostat’s screen.
2. Select **System**, then **Factory reset**.
3. Choose **Confirm** to erase your settings.

Keep the thermostat powered on while it resets. When the welcome screen appears, follow the on-screen instructions to reconnect to Wi-Fi and set up your heating preferences.

Check the time and target temperature before returning to normal use.
