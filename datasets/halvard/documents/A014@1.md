Water leaking underneath your Ashby tumble dryer

On the Ashby tumble dryer, E-6876 means the condensate drain pump is blocked. Water cannot reach the collection tank, so it can build up in the base and leak underneath.

Stop the programme and switch off the dryer. Unplug it only if the plug, socket and surrounding area are dry. If they are wet, switch off the circuit at the consumer unit instead.

Once the power is disconnected, wipe up the water and empty the collection tank. Do not remove the dryer’s panels or try to clear the pump yourself.

Leave the dryer switched off and contact Halvard Home support to arrange a repair. Tell them that water is leaking underneath and that the display shows a drain pump fault.
