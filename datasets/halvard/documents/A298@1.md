Using your Brio kettle in a holiday home

Before using your Brio kettle, check that the cable, plug and base are dry and undamaged. Place the base on a stable, level surface, away from the sink and the edge of the worktop.

If the kettle has been left unused, empty any old water and rinse the inside. Fill with fresh drinking water between the minimum and maximum marks, boil it, then discard that water before making drinks.

Never switch the kettle on when empty or fill it above the maximum mark. Keep the lid closed while boiling.

Before leaving the holiday home, unplug the kettle, let it cool and empty it. Leave it clean and dry, ready for your next stay.
