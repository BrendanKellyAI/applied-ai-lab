Important descaling tablet notice

Stop using descaling tablets from batch 2873 with the Corra induction hob, and return them for a refund.

Halvard Home is recalling the affected tablets because they may not be suitable for this appliance. Please check the batch details printed on the packaging. Keep affected tablets away from your hob and leave them in their original packaging, if available.

Take the tablets back to the shop where you bought them and ask for a refund. If the packaging is missing, or you need help with your return, please contact Halvard Home customer support for guidance.

We apologise for the inconvenience and appreciate your cooperation in helping us resolve this issue.
