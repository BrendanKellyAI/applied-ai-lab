Brio kettle: timer and delay start

The Halvard Home Brio kettle does not have a timer or delay-start setting. You must switch it on manually each time you want to boil water.

To use the kettle, fill it between the minimum and maximum marks, close the lid securely and place it on its base. Press the power switch to start heating. The kettle switches off automatically once the water boils; this is not a timer function.

Do not use a plug-in timer or smart plug to create a delayed start, as the kettle could be switched on unexpectedly or without enough water.

For your morning routine, fill and switch on the kettle when you are ready to use it.
