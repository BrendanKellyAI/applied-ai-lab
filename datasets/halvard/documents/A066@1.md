Fan speed signal fault

On the Halvard Home Fenwick cooker hood, E-1216 means the control board cannot detect the fan motor’s speed. The hood stops the fan to prevent it running without speed monitoring.

To reset the hood, turn it off using its controls, then switch off its power at the wall for five minutes. Restore the power and select the lowest fan setting.

If the message clears and the fan runs normally, you can use the hood again. If the message returns, or the fan hums without turning, switch off the power and contact Halvard Home support. Do not remove the motor cover or try to repair internal wiring.
