Replacing the glass on your Corra induction hob

If the glass surface of your Corra induction hob is cracked or chipped, stop using it. Switch off the hob’s electricity supply at the consumer unit before touching or cleaning the surface.

Contact Halvard Home support to ask about a replacement glass surface. Have the hob’s model details, proof of purchase and a clear photo of the damage ready. Check your user guide or purchase documents for the model details; do not lift the hob out to find them.

Ask support to confirm which replacement is compatible with your hob and whether fitting is included in the quote. A qualified technician should carry out the replacement. Do not try to repair cracked glass with glue or sealant.
