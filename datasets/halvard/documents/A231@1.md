Removing limescale from your Corra induction hob

Your Corra induction hob does not need internal descaling. Limescale marks on the glass are mineral deposits left behind when water dries.

Switch the hob off and let the surface cool completely before cleaning. Wipe away loose dirt with a soft cloth dampened with warm water and a little washing-up liquid.

For stubborn white marks, use a cleaner labelled as suitable for induction hob glass, following its instructions. Apply it to a cloth rather than pouring it onto the hob. Wipe away any residue with a clean, damp cloth, then dry with a soft towel.

Do not use kettle descaler, abrasive powders or metal scourers. Avoid letting liquid enter the hob’s edges or controls.
