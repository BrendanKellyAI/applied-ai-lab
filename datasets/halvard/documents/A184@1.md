Cleaning your Brio kettle filter

Clean the filter regularly to remove loose limescale and keep water flowing freely.

1. Unplug your Brio kettle and let it cool completely before cleaning.
2. Empty the kettle, open the lid and gently remove the filter from behind the spout. Do not force it if it feels stuck.
3. Rinse the filter under warm running water. Use a soft brush to loosen any deposits.
4. For stubborn build-up, soak the filter in warm water for 10 minutes, then brush gently and rinse thoroughly.
5. Refit the filter securely before using the kettle again.

Avoid abrasive cleaners or metal brushes, which may damage the mesh. Never immerse the kettle body, base or plug in water.
