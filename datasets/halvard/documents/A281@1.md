Flashing light on your Corra induction hob

A flashing cooking-zone light can mean the hob is not detecting a suitable pan. Check that a pan is centred on the selected zone and that its base is clean and dry.

Use induction-compatible cookware. A magnet sticking firmly to the pan’s base is a useful check for compatibility. Switch the zone off before repositioning or replacing the pan, and use oven gloves if it is hot.

If the light still flashes, turn the hob off using its controls and allow it to cool. Wipe away any moisture covering the control area, then try again.

If the warning continues, stop using the hob and contact Halvard Home support. Describe which light flashes and when it happens.
