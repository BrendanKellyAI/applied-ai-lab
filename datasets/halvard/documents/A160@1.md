Unusual vibration from your Keel washing machine

If your Halvard Home Keel washing machine vibrates more than usual, pause the programme and wait for the drum to stop before checking it.

An uneven load can cause vibration during the spin cycle. If the door unlocks, spread heavy items, such as towels, evenly around the drum, then restart the programme. Do not force the door open.

Check that the machine stands firmly on a hard, level floor. All four feet should touch the floor, and the machine should not rock when gently pushed at opposite corners.

If it is newly installed, check the installation guide to confirm the transit bolts were removed.

Stop using the machine and contact Halvard Home support if strong vibration continues.
