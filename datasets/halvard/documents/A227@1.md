Resetting your Tessa dishwasher

A factory reset returns your Halvard Home Tessa dishwasher’s saved settings to their original values. Use it if you want to clear your preferences and set the appliance up again.

Before starting, let any wash programme finish and empty the dishwasher.

1. Switch the dishwasher on, but do not start a programme.
2. Press and hold the Programme and Start buttons together for five seconds, until the indicator lights flash.
3. Release both buttons, then switch the dishwasher off.
4. Wait 30 seconds before switching it back on.

The reset clears your saved wash preferences. Afterwards, check the water hardness and rinse aid settings and adjust them for your household.

If the lights do not flash, contact Halvard Home support for help.
