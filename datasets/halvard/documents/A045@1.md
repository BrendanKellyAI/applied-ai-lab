Installing your Mira robot vacuum

For the Mira robot vacuum, KX-1639 is the part number of the supplied charging dock. Place this dock against a wall on a level floor, leaving at least one metre of clear space in front. Connect the power lead and switch on the wall socket.

If the vacuum does not begin charging during first installation, a clear transport strip may still be covering its charging contacts. This prevents the vacuum from making an electrical connection with the dock.

Switch off the vacuum and unplug the dock. Remove the strip from the contacts underneath the vacuum, then reconnect the dock and switch the vacuum on. Place it on the dock and check that the charging indicator lights up.
