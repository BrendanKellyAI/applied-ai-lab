Keel washing machine not switching on

If your Keel washing machine shows no lights and does not respond to the power button, start by checking its power supply.

Make sure the plug is firmly inserted and the wall socket is switched on. Test the socket with a working lamp. If the lamp does not light, have the socket checked by a qualified electrician.

Unplug the washing machine for 60 seconds, then reconnect it and try again.

KX-3100 is the Keel’s internal power switch module. A failed module can stop power reaching the controls, leaving the display dark.

If the socket works but the machine remains off, unplug it and contact Halvard Home support to arrange an inspection. Do not remove the casing or attempt to replace the module yourself.
