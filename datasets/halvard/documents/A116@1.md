Mira robot vacuum: unusual vibration

On the Mira robot vacuum, E-7215 means the main brush is rotating unevenly, causing unusual vibration. Hair or thread wrapped around one end of the brush is the most likely cause.

Switch the vacuum off and remove it from its charging dock before checking underneath. Place it upside down on a soft cloth, open the main brush cover and lift out the brush. Carefully remove any wrapped hair or thread, paying particular attention to both ends.

Refit the brush and close the cover securely. Run the vacuum on a clear, level floor for one minute.

If the vibration continues, switch it off and contact Halvard Home support. Do not keep using the vacuum if the brush looks bent or damaged.
