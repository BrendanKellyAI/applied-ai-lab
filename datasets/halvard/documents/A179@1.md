Recycling your old Corra induction hob

Your old Halvard Home Corra induction hob should be recycled as electrical equipment, not placed in your household rubbish bin.

Before removal, switch off the hob’s electricity supply at the consumer unit. If the hob is wired directly into your home’s electrical circuit, arrange for a qualified electrician to disconnect it. Do not cut or pull out the wiring yourself.

Contact your local council to find a household waste recycling centre that accepts electric hobs. Check whether you need to book a visit and whether help with unloading is available.

If you are buying a replacement, ask the retailer whether it offers an old-appliance collection service. Confirm any charges and disconnection requirements before booking.
