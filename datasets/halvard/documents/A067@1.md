Burning smell from your Orrin heat pump

On the Halvard Home Orrin heat pump, E-1261 means the outdoor fan motor has overheated, potentially scorching its electrical insulation and causing a burning smell.

Stop using the heat pump immediately. If there is no smoke or fire and it is safe to approach, switch it off at the controller, then disconnect its power using the labelled isolation switch. Switching off at the controller alone does not isolate the electricity.

Do not restart the unit, remove its covers or attempt repairs. Contact Halvard Home support to arrange an inspection by a qualified engineer, and describe when the smell started.

If you see smoke or flames, keep away, leave the property and call 999. Do not use water on the appliance.
