Brio kettle rinse aid notice

Halvard Home is asking customers to check the batch details on their rinse aid sachet packaging.

Stop using rinse aid sachets from batch 8708 with the Brio kettle, and return them for a refund.

Please keep the sachets in their packaging and separate from products you are still using. Do not put the affected sachets into your kettle or pass them on to anyone else. Contact Halvard Home customer support for guidance on returning them and arranging your refund. If you cannot read the batch details, contact us before using the sachets.

We apologise for the inconvenience and appreciate your help.
