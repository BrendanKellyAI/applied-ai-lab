Mira robot vacuum warranty

The Halvard Home Mira robot vacuum comes with a two-year warranty from the date of purchase. Keep your receipt or order confirmation, as you will need it to show when you bought your vacuum.

The warranty covers faults caused by defects in materials or workmanship during normal household use. It does not cover accidental damage, misuse or normal wear, such as brushes wearing down through regular cleaning.

If your Mira develops a fault within the warranty period, contact Halvard Home customer support with your proof of purchase and a short description of the problem. Avoid attempting repairs yourself before seeking advice.

Your warranty does not affect your statutory rights.
