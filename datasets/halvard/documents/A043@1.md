Booking an Orrin engineer visit

On the Halvard Home Orrin heat pump, E-4085 means the outdoor coil temperature sensor is not sending a reading. The heat pump pauses heating because it cannot safely control defrosting. This fault needs an engineer’s inspection.

Turn heating off at the controller and contact Halvard Home customer support to book a visit. Have your model name, installation address and a description of when the fault appeared ready. Tell the adviser if the heat pump is your only source of heating or hot water.

Do not remove the outdoor unit’s cover or try to reconnect wiring. Before the appointment, clear access to both the outdoor unit and indoor controls. Ask the adviser to confirm any call-out charge before accepting the booking.
