Side brushes for the Vale fridge freezer

Stop using side brushes from batch 2897 with the Vale fridge freezer, and return them for a refund.

Halvard Home is asking customers to check the batch details printed on the side brush packaging before using these accessories with their appliance.

If your brushes are affected, please set them aside and contact Halvard Home customer support for guidance on returning them and receiving your refund.

If the packaging is unavailable, or the batch details are difficult to read, please contact customer support for advice rather than continuing to use the brushes.

We apologise for the inconvenience and appreciate your help in handling this return safely.
