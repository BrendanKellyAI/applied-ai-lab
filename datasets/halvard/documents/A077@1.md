Flashing warning light on your Lumen thermostat

A flashing amber warning light on the Halvard Home Lumen smart thermostat means its backup temperature sensor module, part number KX-2456, is not responding. The thermostat pauses scheduled heating because it cannot confirm a safe room temperature.

First, check that the thermostat is firmly seated on its wall mount. Do not remove the mount or touch any wiring. Next, select **Settings**, then **Restart** on the thermostat screen. Wait two minutes for it to reconnect and check the sensor.

If the light stops flashing, your heating schedule will resume automatically. If it continues, leave scheduled heating paused and contact Halvard Home support to arrange a sensor check. Do not attempt to replace the module yourself.
