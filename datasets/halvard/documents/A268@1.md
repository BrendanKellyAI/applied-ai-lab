Descaling your Mira robot vacuum

If your Mira has a water tank, check its care instructions before using any descaling product. Do not put kettle descaler, vinegar or other acidic cleaners into the tank unless those instructions specifically allow it.

Switch the vacuum off and remove it from its charging dock before cleaning. If the tank is removable, take it out and empty any remaining water. Gently wipe accessible limescale deposits with a soft cloth dampened with clean water. Do not immerse the robot or its charging dock.

Do not scrape deposits with a knife or other sharp tool, as this may damage surfaces or seals. If scale remains or water flow is reduced, contact Halvard Home support for guidance before trying a chemical treatment.
