Using your Keel in a holiday home

A few simple checks can help keep your Keel washing machine ready for each visit.

On arrival, check the inlet and drain hoses for leaks or damage. Turn on the water supply before starting a wash. Choose a programme that matches the laundry’s care labels, and measure detergent for the load size and local water hardness.

Before leaving the property:
- Remove all laundry and check the drum.
- Wipe the door seal and remove any trapped items.
- Switch off the machine and turn off its water supply.
- Leave the door slightly open to help the drum dry, keeping children and pets away.

If the property may freeze while empty, arrange suitable frost protection before departure.
