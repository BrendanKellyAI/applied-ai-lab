Unusual vibration from your Lumen smart thermostat

An unusual vibration may come from a loose wall fixing or nearby heating equipment rather than the thermostat itself.

If the heating is running, switch it off using the thermostat’s controls and wait five minutes. Check whether the vibration stops. This helps establish whether it is linked to the heating system.

Look for a visible gap between the thermostat and the wall, but do not pull the unit away, open its casing or tighten hidden fixings yourself.

If the vibration continues, contact Halvard Home support. Tell us when it happens, whether the heating is running and whether you can hear buzzing.

If you notice excessive heat, smoke or a burning smell, stop using the thermostat and seek urgent professional assistance.
