Energy saving settings for your Vale fridge freezer

Set the fridge to 4°C and the freezer to −18°C for everyday use. These temperatures keep food safely chilled or frozen without making the appliance work harder than necessary. Colder settings usually use more electricity.

If you have temporarily selected a colder setting for a large load of food, return to your usual setting once the food has chilled or frozen.

To help maintain a steady temperature, keep door openings brief and check that both doors close fully. Avoid blocking internal air vents with food or packaging.

Let hot food cool before placing it in the fridge, but refrigerate it within two hours. Keep the appliance away from direct sunlight and heat sources where possible.
