Lumen thermostat making a buzzing noise

A brief click when heating starts or stops is normal. A continuous buzz lasting more than 10 seconds can indicate a loose coil inside the heating relay module.

KX-9495 is the part number for this module in the Lumen smart thermostat. The loose coil vibrates while the relay is powered, causing the unusual noise.

Turn the heating off using the thermostat, then switch off power to the heating system at its labelled isolation switch. Do not remove the thermostat cover or try to tighten internal components.

Contact Halvard Home support and describe when the buzzing occurs. A qualified heating engineer should inspect the relay module and replace it if the loose-coil fault is confirmed.
