# Water leaking underneath your Keel washing machine

If water is leaking underneath your Keel washing machine, stop the cycle and turn off the water supply tap. Switch off and unplug the machine only if the plug and surrounding area are dry. Do not touch a wet socket or plug.

Once it is safe, wipe up the water and check these possible sources without moving the machine:

- Look for drips at any visible inlet hose connections.
- Check whether the drain hose has slipped out of its drain.
- Look for water running down from the detergent drawer. Too much detergent can create excess foam and cause leaks.

Do not run another cycle while the leak remains unexplained. If the hoses appear secure or water comes from beneath the casing, contact Halvard Home support.
