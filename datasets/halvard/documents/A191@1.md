Installing your Fenwick cooker hood

Before starting, switch off the electricity supply at the consumer unit. Keep it off until the hood is securely fitted and all electrical work is complete.

Check the installation guide supplied with your hood for the required clearance above your hob. Do not guess this distance: it depends on the hob type and installation requirements.

Use wall fixings suitable for your wall, and check for hidden pipes and cables before drilling. Arrange for a qualified electrician to make any fixed electrical connection.

If using ducted extraction, follow the guide’s instructions for routing the outlet outdoors. Once installation is complete, remove packaging, check that the filters are seated correctly, then test the fan and light.
