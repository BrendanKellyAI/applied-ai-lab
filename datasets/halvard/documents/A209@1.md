Strange noises from your Tessa dishwasher

A gentle hum or occasional gurgle during use can be normal. A new rattling, scraping or banging sound is worth checking.

Pause the programme and wait for spraying to stop before opening the door carefully, keeping clear of hot steam. Switch the dishwasher off before checking inside.

Check that plates and utensils are secure. A utensil hanging below the lower basket can catch the spray arm and cause a repeated knocking sound. Move anything sticking through the basket, then gently turn the spray arm by hand to check that it moves freely.

Close the door and restart the programme. If the noise continues, or you hear loud grinding, stop using the dishwasher and contact Halvard Home support.
