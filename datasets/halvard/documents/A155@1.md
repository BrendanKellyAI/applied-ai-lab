Descaling your Brio kettle

Limescale can build up inside your Brio kettle, especially in hard water areas. Descale it when you notice white deposits inside or flakes in the water.

Switch off and unplug the kettle, then let it cool completely. Empty any remaining water. Use a descaler labelled as suitable for kettles, following the pack instructions for the amount, water temperature and treatment time. Never fill above the maximum mark or mix cleaning products.

After descaling, empty the kettle and rinse it thoroughly with fresh water. Fill to the maximum mark, boil, then discard the water. Repeat this boil-and-discard step once before making drinks.

Keep the base, plug and outside of the kettle dry. Do not immerse the kettle in water.
