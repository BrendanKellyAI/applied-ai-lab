Flashing warning light on your Vale fridge freezer

On the Halvard Home Vale fridge freezer, a warning light that flashes twice, pauses, then repeats means the freezer door sensor is not detecting a closed door. KX-3252 is the part number for this sensor.

First, check that drawers and food packets are not stopping the door from closing. Wipe the door seal with a damp cloth, then close the door firmly. Allow two minutes for the warning light to stop flashing.

If the light keeps flashing with the door fully closed, the sensor may be faulty. Contact Halvard Home support to arrange an inspection. Do not remove or replace the sensor yourself. Keep the door closed as much as possible while awaiting help.
