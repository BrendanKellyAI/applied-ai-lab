Mira robot vacuum running slowly

On the Halvard Home Mira robot vacuum, E-9251 means a blocked suction inlet has triggered reduced-power mode. This protects the motor but makes cleaning slower and pickup weaker.

To restore normal performance:

1. Stop the vacuum, switch it off and remove it from its charging dock.
2. Empty the dust bin.
3. Check the suction inlet beneath the bin and gently remove any trapped fluff or debris with a soft, dry brush.
4. Remove hair wrapped around the main brush, following the removal instructions in your user guide.
5. Refit the bin securely, then switch the vacuum on and start a cleaning run.

Do not push sharp tools into the inlet. If performance remains weak, contact Halvard Home support.
