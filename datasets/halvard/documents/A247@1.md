Pairing your Fenwick cooker hood with a voice assistant

Connect your Fenwick cooker hood to the Halvard Home app before linking it to your voice assistant. Use a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network during setup.

1. Switch on the hood, then press and hold its Wi-Fi button for five seconds, until the indicator flashes.
2. Open the Halvard Home app, select **Add appliance** and follow the instructions.
3. In your voice assistant’s app, find Halvard Home under services or skills.
4. Enable the service and sign in with the same Halvard Home account.
5. Search for devices, then give the hood a simple name, such as “Kitchen hood”.

Try asking your assistant to switch on the kitchen hood. If it does not respond, check that the hood is online in the Halvard Home app.
