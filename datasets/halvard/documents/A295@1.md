Unusual vibration from your Fenwick cooker hood

An unusual vibration may be caused by a grease filter that is not seated correctly, or by an object touching the hood.

Switch the hood off and isolate it from the electricity supply before checking it. Allow the hob and hood to cool.

Remove any items resting on or touching the hood. Check that the grease filters sit flat and their catches are fully engaged. Do not remove the outer casing or adjust internal components.

Restore the power and run the hood on its lowest speed for 30 seconds. If the vibration continues, switch it off again and contact Halvard Home support. Tell us whether the vibration changes with fan speed and whether it started after cleaning.
