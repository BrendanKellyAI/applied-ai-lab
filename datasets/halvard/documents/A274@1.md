Keel washing machine display stays blank

If your Halvard Home Keel washing machine’s display stays blank, first check that the plug is fully inserted and the wall socket is switched on. With dry hands, press the machine’s power button once.

If nothing happens, switch off the socket and unplug the machine for five minutes. Plug it back in, switch on the socket and press the power button again.

If the display is still blank, check whether another appliance works in the same socket. A socket without power cannot supply the washing machine.

Do not remove the machine’s panels or attempt internal repairs. If the socket works but the display remains blank, contact Halvard Home support. If you notice scorching or a burning smell, stop using the machine.
