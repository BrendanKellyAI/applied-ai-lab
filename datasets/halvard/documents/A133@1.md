Moving your Orrin heat pump

Arrange for a qualified heat pump installer to disconnect, move and recommission your Orrin heat pump. Do not move the unit while it is connected to electrical wiring or pipework.

Before any work begins, switch the heat pump off at its controller and isolate its electrical supply. Let the installer make the connections safe and check whether any water needs draining. Never disconnect refrigerant pipes yourself.

Keep the unit upright during transport. Secure it against sliding or tipping, and protect exposed connections from dirt and moisture. Do not lift it by its pipes, fan grille or casing panels.

At the new location, ask the installer to check the base, airflow clearance and connections before restoring power. Do not switch it on until these checks are complete.
