Fenwick cooker hood not switching on

If your Fenwick cooker hood will not switch on, start by checking its power supply.

Make sure the cooker hood’s wall switch or fused connection switch is on. If it has a plug, check that it is fully inserted into the socket, but only if you can reach it safely.

Switch the power off at the wall for 60 seconds, then switch it back on and try the fan control again.

Check whether other kitchen appliances have power. If they are also off, the problem may be with the household supply.

If the hood still does not respond, leave it switched off and contact Halvard Home support. Do not remove covers or attempt internal electrical repairs.
