Connecting your Tessa dishwasher to wi-fi

On the Halvard Home Tessa dishwasher, E-3865 means the appliance has detected a 5 GHz wi-fi network, which it cannot join. The dishwasher needs a 2.4 GHz connection.

Open your router’s settings and check that its 2.4 GHz network is switched on. If your router gives each band a separate network name, connect your phone to the 2.4 GHz network before restarting dishwasher setup in the Halvard Home app.

Keep your phone near the dishwasher during setup and enter your wi-fi password carefully. Passwords are case-sensitive.

If the connection still fails, restart your router, wait two minutes, then try again. Contact Halvard Home support if the same message returns after these checks.
