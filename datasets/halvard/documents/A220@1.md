Resetting your Fenwick cooker hood

A factory reset returns your Fenwick cooker hood’s controls to their original settings and clears your saved preferences.

Before you begin, finish cooking and allow the hob to cool. Leave the hood connected to the electricity supply, but switch off its fan and light.

1. Press and hold the fan and light buttons together for 10 seconds.
2. Release both buttons when the control lights flash twice. This confirms that the reset is complete.
3. Wait until the lights stop flashing, then switch on the fan to check that the controls respond.

You can now choose your preferred fan speed and lighting again. If the control lights do not flash, contact Halvard Home support for help.
