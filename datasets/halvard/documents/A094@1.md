Replacing a damaged Fenwick cooker hood filter

If the grease filter no longer sits flush beneath your Fenwick cooker hood, its frame may be bent. A gap around the frame lets greasy air pass around the mesh rather than through it.

KX-8465 is the replacement metal grease filter for this product. Replace the filter if its frame is bent or its mesh is torn.

Switch off the hood at the wall and allow it to cool. Support the old filter with one hand, release its retaining latch and lower it carefully. Fit the replacement into the rear slots, then raise it until the latch clicks shut.

Check that the filter sits flat and is secure before restoring power. Contact Halvard Home support to order a replacement.
