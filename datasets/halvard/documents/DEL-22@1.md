Ashby tumble dryer side brush notice

Stop using side brushes from batch 8826 with the Ashby tumble dryer, and return them for a refund.

Halvard Home is asking customers to check the batch marking on their side brush packaging before use. If your brushes match the batch shown above, keep them aside for return. Please contact the retailer you bought them from or Halvard Home customer support for help arranging your refund.

If the marking is missing or unclear, do not use the brushes until customer support has confirmed whether they are affected.

We apologise for the inconvenience and thank you for your cooperation.
