Cleaning the Tessa dishwasher filter

Food trapped in the filter can cause water to pool at the bottom of your Tessa dishwasher after a wash.

KX-8645 is the part number for this dishwasher’s removable filter. Clean it once a week to help prevent blockages.

Switch off and unplug the dishwasher, then let it cool. Pull out the lower basket. Turn the filter anticlockwise and lift it out from the bottom of the tub.

Rinse the filter under warm running water, using a soft brush to remove food and grease. Avoid wire brushes, which can damage the mesh.

Refit the filter and turn it clockwise until secure. Never run the dishwasher without it. If water still pools after cleaning, contact Halvard Home support.
