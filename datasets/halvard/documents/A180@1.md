Brio kettle: using the child lock

The child lock stops the Brio kettle from starting a heating cycle when its controls are pressed accidentally. It does not lock the lid or make hot water safe to handle.

To switch the child lock on, make sure the kettle is not heating, then press and hold the lock button for three seconds. The lock indicator lights up to confirm that the controls are locked.

To unlock the kettle, press and hold the same button for three seconds, until the indicator goes out. You can then use the heating controls normally.

Always keep the kettle and its cable out of children’s reach. The child lock is not a substitute for adult supervision.
