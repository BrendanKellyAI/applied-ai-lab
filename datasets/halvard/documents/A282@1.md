Energy-saving settings for Lumen

Use a heating schedule on your Halvard Home Lumen smart thermostat to match your daily routine. Set lower temperatures for times when you are out or asleep, rather than keeping the same temperature all day.

Try setting your daytime heating temperature to 19°C, then adjust it for comfort. Some people, including older adults and those with health conditions, may need a warmer home.

Review your schedule when your routine changes. If you use a temporary temperature override, return to the schedule afterwards so the higher setting does not continue unnecessarily.

Avoid turning the temperature up sharply to warm a room faster: a higher target does not make most heating systems heat more quickly. Keep furniture and curtains clear of the thermostat for accurate readings.
