Important rinse aid sachet notice

Stop using rinse aid sachets from batch 1908 with the Keel washing machine, and return them for a refund.

Halvard Home is recalling these sachets because they may not work as intended with this appliance. You can find the batch details on the sachet packaging. Keep affected sachets away from your laundry supplies so they are not used by mistake.

Please contact Halvard Home customer support for help arranging your return and refund. If you no longer have the packaging, our team can help you check whether your sachets are affected.

We apologise for the inconvenience and thank you for your understanding.
