Mira robot vacuum making a strange noise

On the Mira robot vacuum, E-3873 means the main brush is struggling to turn because hair, thread or another object is caught around it. This can cause a rattling, clicking or grinding noise during cleaning.

Stop the cleaning cycle, switch the vacuum off and remove it from its charging dock. Turn it upside down on a soft cloth. Open the main brush cover and lift out the brush. Gently remove anything wrapped around the brush, checking both ends for trapped debris.

Refit the brush and secure the cover before switching the vacuum on. Try a short clean on a clear floor.

If the noise continues, stop using the vacuum and contact Halvard Home support.
