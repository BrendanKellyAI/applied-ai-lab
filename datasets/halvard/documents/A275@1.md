Ashby tumble dryer drying slowly

If your Ashby tumble dryer is taking longer than usual or leaving clothes damp, start by checking airflow and the load.

Switch off and unplug the dryer before cleaning. Remove lint from the filter after every load: a blocked filter restricts airflow and can slow drying. Refit the filter securely before using the dryer again.

Avoid packing the drum tightly. Clothes need room to tumble so warm air can move between them. Separate heavy towels from lighter fabrics, and use your washing machine’s spin cycle to remove excess water before drying.

Check that any external ventilation openings are clear. If drying remains weak after these checks, contact Halvard Home support. Stop using the dryer if you notice a burning smell.
