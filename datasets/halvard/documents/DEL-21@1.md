Brio kettle wall bracket notice

Stop using wall brackets from batch 4867 with the Brio kettle, and return them for a refund.

We have identified a safety concern with these brackets, which may not hold the kettle securely. Please remove your kettle from the bracket when it is cool and empty, and place it on a stable, level worktop away from the edge.

Check the batch details on the bracket or its packaging. If you cannot confirm whether your bracket is affected, stop using it and contact Halvard Home customer support for advice and return arrangements.

We are sorry for the inconvenience and appreciate your help in keeping your home safe.
