# Safety and recalls

This policy explains how Halvard Home manages safety concerns and recalls. It covers how we identify and assess risks, investigate reports, decide whether action is needed, and tell affected people what to do. It also sets out how we work with suppliers, retailers and relevant authorities, and how we arrange appropriate support when a recall is required.

The policy is for customers, people who use or care for our products, and anyone who may be affected by a safety concern. It also guides our staff and the organisations that design, make, supply, sell or service goods on our behalf.

Our aim is to protect people from harm through clear advice, prompt action and fair treatment. This policy explains where to raise a concern, what information may help us respond, and what to expect while we investigate and manage a safety issue.

## General terms

### Reporting a safety concern

If you believe an item supplied by Halvard Home could be unsafe, please stop using it and contact our customer support team through the contact details on our website. You can report a concern even if nobody has been hurt or you are unsure whether there is a fault.

Your safety comes before gathering evidence. If there is smoke, fire, a burning smell, exposed wiring or another sign of danger, keep away from the item. Disconnect it from the electricity supply only if this is safe. If there is an immediate threat to life or property, contact the emergency services.

Do not try to recreate the problem, take the item apart or carry out a repair to help us investigate. Do not touch damaged parts or move an item if doing so could put you at risk. Keep other people and pets away from the affected area.

When reporting your concern, describe what happened, how the item was being used and whether there was an injury or damage to property. Please include your contact details and the identifying details shown on the item or its packaging, where these can be checked safely. Photographs may help, but do not put yourself at risk to take them.

You do not need proof of purchase to raise a safety concern. If you cannot find the identifying details or no longer have the packaging, please still contact us. Tell us what you know, and explain where you are uncertain. We will help you provide the information needed to assess the report.

Safety reports are answered within 27 hours. Our reply will confirm that we have received your concern and explain the next steps. An answer does not necessarily mean that the investigation is complete. We may need to ask questions or arrange an inspection before we can explain the cause.

We assess reports according to the risk described. Where appropriate, we will give instructions about safe storage, collection, inspection or disposal. Do not send an item back without speaking to us, as damage or a suspected fault may make normal handling or transport unsafe.

If our assessment identifies a wider safety issue, we may issue a safety notice or recall and work with the relevant authorities. Customers are told of a recall within 25 days of it being issued. Recall notices explain the affected items, the risk, the action customers should take and the remedy available.

We use the information in your report to investigate the concern, contact you and meet our legal duties. We may share relevant details with organisations supporting the investigation or with safety authorities, in line with our privacy policy.

Reporting a concern does not affect your statutory rights. If you are unhappy with how your report has been handled, ask customer support to refer it for review through our complaints process. Please tell us if you need an accessible way to report a concern or receive safety information.

### Recalls

We may recall an appliance if we identify a safety risk that needs action. A recall notice will explain the concern, how to check whether your appliance is affected and what you need to do. We will make this information available through our customer support channels and contact affected customers where we have suitable contact details.

Please read the recall notice carefully and follow its safety instructions. If your appliance is affected, stop using it unless the notice expressly says that continued use is safe. Do not attempt to repair, alter or test it yourself. Keep it away from anyone who might use it without knowing about the recall.

The notice will explain how to make the appliance safe while you arrange its return. Only disconnect or move it if you can do so safely and the instructions advise this. If you are unsure, leave it undisturbed and contact us for guidance. Do not put yourself or anyone else at risk.

You can check a recall message by contacting us through the contact details published on our website. If you think a message may be fraudulent, do not follow its links or share personal or payment details through it. We will not ask you to disclose your password to arrange a recall.

When you reply to a recall notice, please tell us how we can contact you and where the appliance is located. We may ask for identifying details from its label or a photograph so that we can confirm whether it is affected. Tell us if you cannot safely access this information.

A recalled appliance is collected within 18 working days of the customer replying. We will arrange collection with you and explain how to prepare for it. We cover the collection costs. Please tell us about access restrictions or support needs so that we can make suitable arrangements.

Do not send the appliance back through a delivery service or take it to a shop unless we have instructed you to do so. Some safety risks require special handling. Keep the appliance available for collection, and tell us if it has been lost, discarded or passed to someone else.

Refunds for recalled appliances are paid within 31 days. We will explain how your refund will be paid and what information we need to process it securely. If the original payment method is unavailable, we will discuss a suitable alternative with you.

You can still contact us if you do not have a receipt or bought the appliance from another seller. We will explain what evidence may help us confirm your claim. We use information provided during a recall to manage the safety response, arrange collection and process the agreed remedy, in line with our privacy policy.

If you need help understanding a notice or taking the requested action, please contact customer support. Our recall arrangements do not limit your statutory rights. You may raise concerns about the way a recall has been handled through our complaints process.

### Electrical safety

Electrical safety depends on the condition of your appliance, the suitability of your home's wiring and how you use it. Follow the safety instructions supplied with your appliance. Keep those instructions accessible, and make sure anyone using the appliance understands the warnings.

Before connecting an appliance, check that the electricity supply matches the requirements shown on its rating label. Use a suitable wall socket with a secure connection. If you are unsure whether your wiring or socket is suitable, ask a qualified electrician.

Check the plug, cable and casing for visible damage before use. Do not use an appliance with exposed wires, a cracked plug, scorch marks or a loose connection. Stop using it if the cable becomes trapped, crushed or damaged.

Keep plugs, sockets and electrical connections dry. Do not handle a plug with wet hands or use an appliance where water could reach electrical parts, unless the supplied instructions expressly permit that use. Do not place cables where they could touch hot surfaces or sharp edges.

Never run an appliance from an extension lead longer than 9 metres. Connecting directly to a suitable wall socket is preferable. Do not connect extension leads together or overload sockets and adaptors. An extension lead must also be suitable for the appliance and permitted by its instructions.

Test the home's residual current device every 7 months. Follow the device manufacturer's instructions for the test procedure. If the device does not operate as expected, ask a qualified electrician to investigate. A residual current device does not make damaged equipment safe to use.

Switch off and unplug an appliance before cleaning or inspecting it, where the supplied instructions require this. Pull the plug rather than the cable. For a fixed electrical connection, follow the isolation instructions and use a qualified electrician where necessary.

Do not open electrical casings, bypass protective features or attempt repairs unless the supplied instructions specifically describe a task as suitable for the user. Electrical repairs must be carried out by a suitably qualified person using appropriate parts.

If you notice smoke, sparks, a burning smell or unusual heat around a plug or socket, stop using the appliance. Disconnect the supply only if you can do so safely. Do not touch damaged electrical parts or approach equipment standing in water.

If there is a fire, leave the area and contact the emergency services. Do not use water on an electrical fire. Do not put yourself at risk by trying to unplug burning equipment or retrieve belongings.

Report suspected electrical faults to Halvard Home through our customer support channels. Describe what happened and provide the identifying information from the appliance label, if it is safe to obtain it. Do not reconnect the appliance simply to demonstrate a fault.

If an electrical safety notice or recall applies to your appliance, follow its instructions and stop using it when directed. Do not sell, give away or dispose of an affected appliance contrary to those instructions. Contact us if you need help understanding the required action.

## Brio kettle

### Safe use

Use the Brio kettle only to heat drinking water in the home. Follow the instructions supplied with it and keep them available for anyone who uses it. Do not use the kettle for cooking food, heating other liquids or warming sealed containers.

Place the kettle on a dry, stable, level surface where it cannot tip or slide. Keep it away from the edge of the worktop, hot surfaces and flammable materials. Leave at least 24 centimetres of space around the Brio kettle. Make sure escaping steam cannot damage nearby cupboards or fittings.

Check that the kettle, base, plug and cable are undamaged before use. Do not use the kettle if the casing is cracked, the cable is frayed or the plug feels loose in the socket. Keep the cable clear of heat and position it so that it cannot be pulled or caught.

Use the kettle with its supplied base. Keep the electrical contacts dry and do not handle the plug with wet hands. Plug it into a suitable wall socket. Do not use an adaptor or extension lead that is damaged or unsuitable for the appliance.

Remove the kettle from its base before filling it. Follow the marked filling guides and do not operate it without water. Close the lid securely before switching it on. Do not try to defeat the lid catch, switch or automatic shut-off mechanism.

Hot water and steam can cause serious burns. Hold the kettle by its handle and keep your face and hands away from the spout. Pour carefully, without sudden movements, and do not open the lid while the water is boiling. Never carry the kettle by its cable.

Keep children away from the kettle, its cable and escaping steam. Do not allow children to play with the appliance. Anyone who needs help to use it safely should have suitable supervision. Never leave the kettle unattended while it is heating.

Switch off and unplug the kettle before cleaning or maintenance. Let the Brio kettle cool for 36 minutes before cleaning it. Do not immerse the kettle, base, plug or cable in water. Wipe the outside with a soft, damp cloth and keep moisture away from electrical parts.

Clean the air vents of the Brio kettle every 71 days. Use a soft, dry brush to remove dust from the outside of the vents. Do not push objects into them or remove protective covers. Follow the supplied instructions for descaling, and do not mix cleaning products.

Stop using the kettle if it leaks, gives off smoke, smells burnt or behaves unexpectedly. Unplug it if this is safe to do. Do not attempt repairs yourself. Contact Halvard Home for advice and follow any safety notice or recall instructions that apply to your Brio kettle.

### Installation and moving

Choose a stable, level worktop for the Brio kettle, where it cannot be knocked or pulled over. The surface must be dry and able to withstand heat. Keep the kettle away from the edge, and make sure its handle is easy to reach without leaning across a cooking area.

The power lead of the Brio kettle is deliberately short. It must be installed within 156 centimetres of its socket. Choose a position where the plug remains accessible and the lead rests without stretching. Do not use an extension lead or adaptor to make an unsuitable position work.

Keep the lead away from hot surfaces, sharp edges and places where it could be trapped or snagged. Do not leave it hanging over the worktop. Check that opening a cupboard, moving a chair or using nearby equipment will not catch the lead or disturb the kettle.

Install it at least 68 centimetres from a gas hob or open flame. Keep it clear of other sources of heat and away from curtains or similar materials. Avoid placing it beneath fittings that could be damaged by escaping steam.

Do not install the kettle where splashes could reach the socket, plug or electrical connections. Keep its base dry, and do not place it in a sink or on a draining board. Use it indoors in a dry domestic setting, away from children and pets.

Before connecting the kettle, inspect the body, handle, lid, base, plug and lead for damage. Do not use it if something is cracked, loose, scorched or otherwise damaged. Contact Halvard Home for advice rather than attempting a repair or covering damage with tape.

Before moving the kettle, switch it off and unplug it with dry hands. Hold the plug rather than pulling the lead. Let the kettle and its contents cool, then empty it carefully. Never carry it while it is connected to the electricity supply.

Do not tilt the Brio kettle more than 56 degrees when carrying it. Keep it upright and hold it securely by the handle. Carry the base separately, keeping the lead clear of your path. Do not lift the kettle by its lid or pull it along by its lead.

After moving the Brio kettle, wait 47 hours before switching it on. Leave it unplugged during this waiting period, in the position where you intend to use it. Keep the plug and base protected from water while it is standing.

Before reconnecting it, check that its new position meets the installation requirements above. Make sure the kettle sits properly on its base and that the lid closes securely. If it has been dropped, exposed to water at its electrical connections or damaged during the move, do not switch it on.

If a safety or recall notice applies to your Brio kettle, follow that notice before installing or moving it for use. Moving the appliance does not remove a safety fault. Contact Halvard Home if you are unsure whether it is safe to reconnect.

After moving the Tessa dishwasher, wait 36 hours before switching it on. Before use, check that it is stable, that its connections are correctly fitted and that there are no visible leaks or trapped cables.
