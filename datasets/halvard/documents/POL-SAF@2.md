# Safety and recalls

This policy explains how Halvard Home manages safety concerns and recalls. It covers how we identify and assess risks, investigate reports, decide whether action is needed, and tell affected people what to do. It also sets out how we work with suppliers, retailers and relevant authorities, and how we arrange appropriate support when a recall is required.

The policy is for customers, people who use or care for our products, and anyone who may be affected by a safety concern. It also guides our staff and the organisations that design, make, supply, sell or service goods on our behalf.

Our aim is to protect people from harm through clear advice, prompt action and fair treatment. This policy explains where to raise a concern, what information may help us respond, and what to expect while we investigate and manage a safety issue.

## General terms

### Reporting a safety concern

If you believe a Halvard Home appliance may be unsafe, please tell us. You can report a concern even if nobody has been hurt or you are unsure what caused the problem. We welcome reports from customers, people using our appliances, repairers and anyone who has noticed a possible safety risk.

Stop using the appliance if you suspect it could cause harm. If it is safe to do so, switch it off and disconnect it from the electricity supply. Do not touch damaged wiring, exposed electrical parts or an appliance that appears dangerous. Keep other people and pets away from the affected area.

If there is smoke, fire, a suspected gas leak or an immediate threat to someone’s safety, move to a safe place and contact the emergency services. Do not put yourself at risk to inspect the appliance, collect evidence or prevent damage. Reporting the concern to us must not delay getting emergency help.

You can report a safety concern through the contact options on our website. Tell our customer support team that your message concerns safety so it can be directed appropriately. If you need help communicating with us, please explain your needs and we will work with you to find an accessible way to report.

Describe what happened in your own words. Explain what you noticed, what the appliance was doing and whether anyone was injured or property was damaged. Tell us about unusual smells, sounds, heat, smoke or visible damage where relevant. Please distinguish between what you observed and what you think may have caused it.

Include any identifying details you can safely find on the appliance or its paperwork, together with your contact details. Information about where you obtained it and any previous repairs may help our investigation. You do not need proof of purchase to raise a safety concern, and missing information should not prevent you from contacting us.

Photographs, recordings or documents can help us understand a report, but only provide them if you can obtain them safely. Do not recreate the fault, open the casing or reconnect the appliance for our benefit. Avoid sending images that reveal unrelated personal information or show someone who has not agreed to be photographed.

Safety reports are answered within 27 hours. Our response will confirm that we have received your concern and explain the next steps available. An answer does not necessarily mean that the investigation is complete. We may need further information or specialist assessment before we can explain the cause or confirm an appropriate remedy.

We assess reports according to the possible risk of harm and the evidence available. We may ask questions about how the appliance was installed, used or maintained. These questions help us understand what happened; they do not mean that we have decided you are responsible or that your concern is being dismissed.

Please keep the appliance and relevant packaging or paperwork where you can do so safely. Do not post, transport, sell, give away or dispose of an appliance that may be unsafe without appropriate guidance. If keeping it creates a risk, tell us so we can discuss safe handling arrangements with you.

Where an inspection or collection is needed, we will explain the arrangements and any precautions you should take. We will not ask you to carry out work that requires specialist skills. Do not attempt a repair unless we have confirmed that the proposed action is safe and suitable for you to undertake.

If our assessment identifies a safety issue, we will explain the action required. This may involve advice to stop using the appliance, an inspection, a repair, a replacement or a refund, depending on the circumstances and your legal rights. We will make clear whether an action is precautionary or follows a confirmed finding.

Customers are told of a recall within 25 days of it being issued. Recall notices explain the affected appliances, the risk involved and what customers should do. We use appropriate contact channels and public notices to reach affected customers. Please follow the safety instructions in a recall notice even if your appliance appears to work normally.

We handle personal information in line with our privacy policy. Relevant information may be shared with repair partners, safety specialists or public authorities where needed to investigate a concern or meet legal duties. Raising a safety concern does not affect your statutory rights. If you remain concerned about our response, ask for your report to be reviewed.

### Recalls

If we identify a safety risk that requires an appliance to be recalled, we will explain the risk and the action you need to take. Our priority is to help you stop using the affected appliance safely and arrange a suitable remedy.

We may contact you using the details held with your order, account or product registration. We may also publish recall notices through our website, retailers and other appropriate channels. Please keep your contact details up to date so that important safety information can reach you.

A recall notice will explain how to check whether your appliance is affected. Follow the instructions carefully and contact us if you cannot find the identifying details or are unsure what they mean. Do not assume that an appliance is safe because it appears to work normally.

If your appliance is covered by a recall, stop using it and follow the safety advice in the notice. Disconnect it from the electricity supply only if this can be done safely. Do not attempt to dismantle, repair or test it yourself.

Keep the affected appliance away from anyone who might use it without knowing about the recall. Do not sell, donate or pass it on. If you have already given it to someone else, share the recall notice with them and tell us so that we can help.

To respond to a recall, use the contact method shown in the recall notice. We will ask for the information needed to identify the appliance, contact you and arrange the remedy. Tell us if you need help providing this information or understanding the instructions.

You can contact us about a recall even if you have lost your receipt, did not register the appliance or received it as a gift. We will explain what other information may help us confirm that the appliance is affected and establish the appropriate remedy.

The remedy will be explained in the recall notice. Depending on the safety issue, this may involve collection and a refund, replacement or an authorised repair. We will not ask you to carry out work that should be handled by a trained professional.

Where collection is required, we will agree the arrangements with you and explain how to prepare the appliance safely. A recalled appliance is collected within 18 working days of the customer replying. Please tell us about access difficulties or support needs when you respond.

Do not move or disconnect an appliance if doing so could put you or someone else at risk. Tell us if it is fitted in place, difficult to reach or unsafe to handle. We will arrange appropriate assistance rather than ask you to take that risk.

Please make the appliance available as agreed and tell us if your circumstances change. If collection cannot go ahead, we will contact you to arrange an alternative and explain what happens next. You should continue to follow the recall safety advice.

Refunds for recalled appliances are paid within 31 days. We will explain how your refund will be paid and what information we need to process it. If the original payment method is unavailable, we will discuss a suitable alternative with you.

We will explain whether accessories or other items supplied with the appliance need to be returned. Do not dispose of the appliance or arrange your own return unless we ask you to do so. This helps us manage the safety risk and handle recalled appliances responsibly.

If you bought the appliance through a retailer, the recall notice will say whether you should contact us or the retailer. We will work with those involved to make the process clear. You should not have to work out who is responsible without guidance.

We use the personal information you provide to manage the recall, arrange the remedy and meet our legal duties. We may share relevant details with organisations helping us carry out these tasks, such as collection providers, in line with our privacy policy.

If you are concerned that a recall message may not be genuine, contact us through our official website or your usual customer service channel. Avoid using links or sharing payment details in response to a message you do not trust.

Please tell us if you are unhappy with how your recall has been handled or if the proposed arrangements do not meet your needs. We will explain how to raise a complaint and seek a resolution. These recall terms do not affect your legal rights.

### Electrical safety

Electrical safety depends on the condition of your appliance, the suitability of your electrical supply and the way the appliance is used. Read the safety instructions supplied with your appliance and keep them available for reference. Follow any warning labels, and do not use an appliance for a purpose it was not designed to serve.

Check the appliance, plug and power lead for visible damage before use. Look for cracked casing, loose parts, exposed wiring, scorch marks or damage around the plug. Do not connect an appliance if you suspect it is unsafe. Damage may be present even when the appliance appears to work normally.

Use a socket that is suitable for the appliance and properly installed. The plug should fit securely without being forced. If a socket is loose, cracked, discoloured or unusually warm, stop using it and ask a qualified electrician to inspect it. Do not attempt to repair fixed electrical wiring yourself.

Connect appliances directly to a suitable wall socket wherever possible. Never run an appliance from an extension lead longer than 9 metres. This limit does not mean that an extension lead is suitable for your appliance. Follow the appliance instructions, including any restriction on extension leads, adaptors or shared sockets.

Do not overload sockets or connect adaptors together. Keep plugs and connections accessible so that the supply can be isolated safely. Do not place power leads where they may be trapped, stretched, walked on or damaged by furniture. Keep them away from hot surfaces, sharp edges and moving parts.

Your home's protective electrical devices must be suitable and working correctly. Test the home's residual current device every 7 months. Follow the device maker's testing instructions and any advice requiring more frequent testing from a qualified electrician. If the device fails its test, seek professional advice and do not assume that the circuit is protected.

A protective device is not a substitute for safe use or proper maintenance. If the supply trips when an appliance is connected or running, stop using the appliance. Do not repeatedly reset the protection to keep it operating. Ask a qualified electrician or an authorised repair provider to investigate the cause.

Keep your hands dry when handling plugs, switches and electrical connections. Protect appliances from water unless their instructions expressly permit the intended use. If liquid enters an appliance or reaches its electrical connections, stop using it. Isolate the supply only if you can do so without touching wet equipment or entering water.

Switch off and unplug an appliance before cleaning or carrying out maintenance permitted in its instructions. Pull the plug rather than the lead. For an appliance connected directly to the fixed wiring, follow its isolation instructions. If you are unsure how to make it safe, seek advice from a qualified professional.

Do not open electrical housings, bypass safety features or make unauthorised alterations. Internal components may remain dangerous after the appliance has been disconnected. Repairs involving electrical parts must be carried out by a suitably qualified person. Replacement plugs, leads and components must meet the appliance's safety requirements.

Stop using an appliance if it gives off a burning smell, sparks, produces smoke or behaves unexpectedly. Disconnect it only when safe. If there is a fire or an immediate threat to personal safety, move away, alert others and contact the emergency services. Never use water on an electrical fire.

Keep children and anyone who may not recognise electrical hazards away from damaged appliances, loose leads and accessible connections. Provide supervision where the appliance instructions require it. Do not allow packaging, coverings or stored items to obstruct ventilation or interfere with safe access to the plug or isolation switch.

Contact Halvard Home if you believe an appliance has an electrical fault or may be affected by a safety notice. Describe the problem and provide the identifying details requested by our support team. Do not reconnect the appliance simply to demonstrate the fault or obtain information that is not safely accessible.

If we issue a safety notice or recall, follow its instructions and stop using the affected appliance when directed. Do not sell, lend or donate an appliance that has been identified as unsafe. Keep it disconnected and stored safely while awaiting advice about repair, collection or disposal. Nothing in this policy limits your statutory rights.

## Brio kettle

### Safe use

Use the Brio kettle only to heat drinking water in a home setting. It is not intended for cooking food, warming other liquids or use outdoors. Follow the instructions supplied with your kettle and keep them available for anyone who may use it.

Place the kettle on a dry, level, stable surface that can withstand heat. Keep it away from the edge of the worktop, hot cooking surfaces and places where it could be knocked over. Do not place it on a tray that could collect spilled water around electrical connections.

Leave at least 24 centimetres of space around the Brio kettle. Keep curtains, packaging and other combustible materials away from it. Position it so that steam can escape freely without reaching cupboards, shelves, sockets or items that could be damaged by heat or moisture.

Check the kettle, base, plug and lead for damage before use. Do not use the kettle if the casing is cracked, the lead is frayed or the plug appears loose or scorched. A kettle that has been dropped or is leaking should be taken out of use and checked.

Make sure your electricity supply matches the information on the kettle’s rating label. Use the base supplied with the kettle and connect it to a suitable wall socket. Keep the plug and socket accessible. Do not use an adaptor or extension lead that could become overloaded.

Keep the lead away from heat, sharp edges and wet surfaces. Arrange it so that it cannot hang over the worktop or be pulled by a child. Do not carry the kettle by its lead or pull the lead to remove the plug from the socket.

Remove the kettle from its base before filling it. Follow the fill markings inside the kettle, and do not operate it without water. Keep the outside of the kettle and the underside dry. If water reaches the base or electrical connections, disconnect the supply safely and stop using it.

Close the lid securely before switching the kettle on. Do not open the lid during heating or while steam is escaping. Keep your face and hands away from the spout and lid opening. Steam and hot water can cause serious burns, even when the kettle appears quiet.

Stay nearby while the kettle is heating. Do not rely on its automatic shut-off as a reason to leave it unattended. If it fails to switch off, disconnect it safely and stop using it. Do not repeatedly restart it to test whether a suspected fault has cleared.

Lift the kettle by its handle and pour carefully, keeping the spout directed away from people. Avoid sudden movements and do not tilt it sharply. Take care when putting it back on its base. The body, lid and surrounding surfaces may be hot.

Keep children away from the kettle, its lead and escaping steam. Do not allow them to play with it or use it without appropriate supervision. Anyone who needs support to use the kettle safely should have help from someone who understands the risks.

Switch the kettle off and unplug it before cleaning. Let the Brio kettle cool for 36 minutes before cleaning it. Treat surfaces with care if they still feel hot. Do not put the kettle, base, plug or lead into water, and do not rinse electrical parts under a tap.

Wipe the exterior with a soft, damp cloth and dry it carefully. Avoid abrasive pads, harsh chemicals and tools that could scratch the surface or damage seals. Follow the supplied instructions for descaling, and keep cleaning products away from children and food preparation areas.

Clean the air vents of the Brio kettle every 71 days. Keep the kettle unplugged during this task. Gently remove dust from accessible vent surfaces with a soft, dry brush. Do not push objects through the vents or allow moisture to enter them. Never cover the vents during use.

Stop using the kettle if you notice smoke, sparks, a burning smell or unusual behaviour. Disconnect it if this can be done safely. Do not attempt to open the casing or repair electrical parts yourself. Contact Halvard Home for advice about inspection, repair or disposal.

If Halvard Home issues a safety notice or recall affecting your Brio kettle, follow the notice and stop using it when instructed. Do not pass an affected kettle to someone else. Keep its identification details available so that customer support can help you check whether it is covered.

### Installation and moving

Choose a stable, level worktop for the Brio kettle, where it cannot rock, slide or be knocked over. The surface must be dry and suitable for heat and splashes. Do not place the kettle on a cloth, tray with an uneven base, or unstable furniture.

Keep the kettle away from the edge of the worktop and out of the reach of children. Consider how people move through the room, especially where someone might catch the handle or pull the lead. Do not position it where a door could strike it.

The power lead of the Brio kettle is deliberately short. It must be installed within 156 centimetres of its socket. Choose a position that lets the lead rest without tension. Do not stretch the lead across a walkway or leave it hanging over an edge.

Use a wall socket that is suitable for the kettle and accessible without reaching over it. Check that the electrical supply matches the information on the rating label. Do not use an extension lead or adaptor to make an unsuitable position work. Ask a qualified electrician if you are unsure.

Install it at least 68 centimetres from a gas hob or open flame. Keep the kettle and its lead away from hot cooking surfaces, heaters and places where heat could damage them. Do not route the lead behind cooking equipment or trap it against a hot surface.

Choose a position where steam can escape without reaching electrical fittings, cupboards or items that could be damaged by moisture. Leave the spout clear and keep the kettle away from curtains. Do not place it beneath shelving that obstructs access or forces you to reach through escaping steam.

Keep the base, plug and socket away from sinks and other places where water may collect or splash. Do not install the kettle outdoors or where it could be exposed to rain. Never immerse the base, power lead or plug in water when cleaning the installation area.

Before putting the kettle into service, remove packaging and check the body, handle, lid, base, plug and lead for damage. Do not use it if anything is cracked, loose, scorched or otherwise damaged. Contact Halvard Home for advice rather than attempting a repair yourself.

Make sure the kettle sits correctly on its base and that the lead is not caught underneath. The handle must be accessible without reaching across the spout. Keep packaging away from children and dispose of it through the appropriate local waste or recycling service.

Before moving the kettle, switch it off and unplug it at the wall. Hold the plug rather than pulling the lead. Allow the kettle to cool, then empty it carefully. Do not carry it with hot water inside or move it while it is operating.

Do not tilt the Brio kettle more than 56 degrees when carrying it. Keep it upright and hold the handle securely. Carry the base separately so that it cannot fall or pull on the lead. Never lift the kettle by its lid, spout or power lead.

Check the route before carrying the kettle. Clear away obstacles and avoid carrying it while holding other objects that make your grip uncertain. If you cannot move it securely, ask for help. Put it down on a stable surface rather than trying to adjust your grip while walking.

For transport, protect the kettle and base against knocks and prevent them from moving around inside their packaging. Keep the plug from pressing against the kettle body. Do not place heavy objects on the packaging or leave the appliance where it may become wet.

After moving the Brio kettle, wait 47 hours before switching it on. During this waiting period, keep it unplugged in its intended position. Check that the kettle, base and electrical connections are dry and undamaged before reconnecting it. Waiting does not make damaged equipment safe to use.

Recheck the installation whenever the kettle changes position, including after cleaning or rearranging the worktop. If the new position does not meet these requirements, choose somewhere else. Do not compromise the placement requirements for convenience or hide a damaged lead behind surrounding items.

If you discover damage, suspect an electrical fault or receive a recall notice affecting your Brio kettle, stop using it and follow the advice from Halvard Home. Do not pass it on to someone else while its safety is in doubt. Contact customer support if you need help interpreting a recall notice.

## Tessa dishwasher

### Safe use

Use the Tessa dishwasher only for washing items that are marked as dishwasher safe. Follow the instructions supplied with the appliance and the care guidance for the items you load. Using the appliance for another purpose, or changing its parts or controls, can cause injury, damage or unsafe operation.

Keep the instructions accessible to anyone who uses the appliance. Make sure users understand how to stop a programme and disconnect the power safely. Children must not play with the appliance, its controls or its packaging. Anyone who needs help to operate it safely should have suitable supervision.

Have the appliance installed by a competent person in accordance with the installation instructions. The supporting surface must be stable, and the appliance must be secured as directed. Water connections, drainage and electrical work must comply with local requirements. Do not use the appliance if its installation appears unsafe.

Leave at least 4 centimetres of space around the Tessa dishwasher. Keep ventilation openings clear of packaging, cloths and stored belongings. Do not cover the appliance or push objects into its openings. Follow the installation instructions for its position, and ask Halvard Home for advice if the available space does not allow safe installation.

Connect the appliance to a suitable earthed electrical supply. Do not use an extension lead or an adaptor. Keep the plug, socket and cable away from water and sources of heat. If the cable or plug is damaged, stop using the appliance and arrange for a qualified professional to assess it.

Check that the door closes properly before starting a programme. Arrange dishes so that they cannot obstruct the spray arms, damage the door seal or prevent detergent from being released. Do not force the racks into place. Remove food scraps and unsuitable objects before loading dishes.

Handle knives and sharp utensils carefully, following the loading guidance supplied with the appliance. Position sharp edges so that they cannot injure someone loading or unloading dishes. Do not place items in the appliance if they are cracked, unstable or likely to break during washing.

Use detergent and rinse aid intended for domestic dishwashers. Follow the safety instructions on their packaging, including advice about handling and storage. Do not put washing-up liquid, solvents, flammable liquids or corrosive cleaning chemicals into the appliance. Keep cleaning products away from children and pets.

Keep the door closed when the appliance is not being loaded or unloaded. An open door can cause someone to trip. Do not stand, sit or lean on it, and do not let children climb onto it or reach inside. Check that pets are clear before closing the door.

Avoid opening the door during a programme. Hot water and steam can escape and cause burns. If opening is necessary, use the pause or stop control as described in the operating instructions, then open the door cautiously. Keep your face and hands away from escaping steam.

Dishes and internal surfaces can remain hot after a programme ends. Allow them to cool until they can be handled safely. Take care when unloading glassware, and do not reach into cloudy water or concealed spaces where broken glass or sharp objects may be present.

Let the Tessa dishwasher cool for 7 minutes before cleaning it. Disconnect it from the electrical supply before carrying out cleaning that the instructions require to be done without power. If surfaces remain hot, allow them to become safe to touch. Never spray water onto electrical parts or use a steam cleaner.

Clean the air vents of the Tessa dishwasher every 98 days. Follow the cleaning instructions for the filters, seals and accessible surfaces. Wear protective gloves if there is a risk of contact with sharp debris. Do not remove protective panels or attempt repairs yourself.

Stop using the appliance if it leaks, smells of burning, produces smoke or behaves unexpectedly. Disconnect the power and turn off the water supply if this can be done safely. Do not touch a wet plug or socket. If there is fire, leave the area and contact the emergency services.

Contact Halvard Home if you suspect a safety fault or need help understanding these instructions. Do not resume use until the fault has been assessed and the appliance is confirmed safe. If Halvard Home issues a recall or safety notice affecting your appliance, follow its instructions rather than attempting a repair or workaround.

### Installation and moving

Safe installation and careful moving help protect you, your home and the Tessa dishwasher. Follow the installation instructions supplied with the appliance alongside this policy. If you are unsure whether your home or connections are suitable, ask a qualified installer to assess them before work begins.

Check the appliance and its packaging for signs of damage before installation. Do not connect it if the casing, door, plug, cable or hoses appear damaged. Contact Halvard Home for advice and keep the appliance disconnected. Do not try to straighten damaged parts or cover damaged wiring with tape.

Choose a position on a firm, level floor that can support the appliance during use. Keep the surrounding area clear so the door can open freely and the appliance can be reached for servicing. Do not install it where frost, rain or standing water could affect its safe operation.

The Tessa dishwasher must not be placed near an open flame. Install it at least 146 centimetres from a gas hob or open flame. Keep packaging, cleaning cloths and other combustible materials away from cooking equipment while preparing the installation area. If the proposed position is unsuitable, choose a different location.

The electrical supply must be suitable for the appliance and properly earthed. It must be installed within 234 centimetres of its socket. The plug and socket must remain accessible after installation. Do not use an extension lead, an adaptor or a trailing socket to reach the appliance.

Ask a qualified electrician to resolve concerns about the socket, earthing or electrical supply. Never alter the plug or cable to make the appliance fit. Route the cable so it cannot become trapped beneath the appliance, caught by the door or pressed against a hot surface or sharp edge.

Water and drainage connections must follow the supplied installation instructions. Have a competent installer make these connections if you cannot do so safely. Use the specified connection parts and make sure hoses are not kinked, stretched or crushed. Keep the water shut-off valve accessible so the supply can be isolated.

Secure and level the appliance as described in the installation instructions. Do not improvise supports or remove protective parts to fit it into a cabinet. Check that neighbouring doors and drawers do not strike the appliance or interfere with its door, controls, hoses or electrical connection.

Before moving the appliance, switch it off, unplug it and turn off its water supply. Hold the plug rather than pulling the cable. Arrange for safe disconnection if the connections cannot be reached without moving the appliance or if you are uncertain how to disconnect them.

Drain the appliance as directed in its instructions before transport. Remove loose contents and secure movable internal parts using the recommended method. Water may escape when hoses are disconnected, so protect the floor and keep electrical connections dry. Do not leave disconnected hoses where they could cause a trip.

Plan the route before lifting or carrying the appliance. Clear obstacles and keep children and pets away from the work area. Use suitable moving equipment and arrange help rather than attempting an unsafe lift. Do not lift the appliance by its door, controls, hoses or power cable.

Do not tilt the Tessa dishwasher more than 40 degrees when carrying it. Keep it upright during transport and secure it against sliding or falling. Protect the casing and connections from impact. If the route cannot be managed safely, stop and ask a professional mover for assistance.

After moving the Tessa dishwasher, wait 36 hours before switching it on. Do not treat this waiting requirement as permission to use an appliance that may have been damaged. Inspect it after transport and contact Halvard Home if it has fallen, suffered an impact or shows signs of damage.

At the new location, repeat the installation checks before reconnecting the appliance. Confirm that the cable and hoses are correctly routed and that the appliance is stable. Check the water connections for leaks according to the installation instructions. Do not operate it if a connection is leaking.

If you notice smoke, a burning smell, unusual heat or an electrical fault, stop using the appliance. Disconnect it only if this is safe, and seek appropriate help. If Halvard Home has issued a safety or recall notice affecting your appliance, follow that notice before arranging installation, moving or reuse.
