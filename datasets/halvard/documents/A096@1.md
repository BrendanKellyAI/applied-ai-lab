Using your Lumen thermostat in a holiday home

On the Halvard Home Lumen smart thermostat, E-8255 means the holiday schedule has no return date, so holiday mode cannot start. Your existing heating schedule stays active until you correct this.

To fix it, open the thermostat’s menu, select Holiday, and enter the date and time when you next expect to arrive. Choose your holiday temperature, then select Save. Check that the display shows both your return date and that holiday mode is active before leaving.

If your plans change, update the return date rather than switching the thermostat off. Keep the heating system powered so it can maintain your chosen temperature while the property is empty.

If the warning remains, contact Halvard Home support.
