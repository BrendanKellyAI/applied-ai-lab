Updating your Tessa dishwasher

Update your Tessa dishwasher’s firmware through the Settings menu on its control panel. Select **Firmware update**, then **Check for updates**.

Before you start, make sure the dishwasher is connected to your home Wi-Fi and no wash programme is running. Empty the dishwasher and leave it switched on.

If an update is available, select **Install** and follow the instructions on the display. Do not switch off the power or disconnect the Wi-Fi while installation is in progress.

Wait until the display confirms that the update is complete before starting a wash.

If the download fails, check your internet connection and try again. If installation repeatedly fails, contact Halvard Home support and describe what appears on the display.
