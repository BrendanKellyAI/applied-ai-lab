Fault message on your Vale fridge freezer

A fault message on the display means your Vale fridge freezer has detected a problem that may need attention.

First, note exactly what the display shows, or take a clear photo. Check that both doors close fully and that food or packaging is not blocking the internal air vents.

If you have an appliance thermometer, check the temperature: the fridge should be at 5°C or below, and the freezer at −18°C.

Keep the doors closed as much as possible while checking the issue. Do not remove panels or attempt electrical repairs.

If the message remains, contact Halvard Home support with the display details and explain whether either compartment has stopped cooling.
