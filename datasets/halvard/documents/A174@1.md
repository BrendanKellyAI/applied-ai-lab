Ashby tumble dryer: power cut during use

If the power goes off while your Ashby tumble dryer is running, switch it off at the socket before unplugging it. Use a torch rather than a candle to check the area.

If it is safe to open the door, remove the laundry carefully, as the drum and clothes may be hot. Spread the items out to let heat escape; do not leave warm laundry in a pile.

Once the electricity supply is stable, plug the dryer back in and switch it on. Check the laundry, then select a suitable programme and start it again if further drying is needed.

If you notice a burning smell or visible damage, leave the dryer unplugged and contact Halvard Home support.
