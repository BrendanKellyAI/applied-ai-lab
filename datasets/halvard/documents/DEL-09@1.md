Wall bracket safety notice

Stop using wall brackets from batch 8556 with the Corra induction hob, and return them for a refund.

Halvard Home has identified a potential safety concern affecting these brackets. Please check the batch details on the packaging or product label. If the details are missing or unclear, contact our customer support team before using the brackets.

Do not attempt to adjust or repair them. If they are already fitted, stop using the hob and seek advice from a qualified installer about safe removal.

Contact Halvard Home customer support for return instructions and help arranging your refund. We apologise for the inconvenience.
