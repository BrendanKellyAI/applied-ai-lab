Connecting your Brio kettle to wi-fi

The Brio kettle connects to a 2.4 GHz home wi-fi network. If your router offers separate networks, choose the 2.4 GHz option rather than 5 GHz.

Before you begin, place the kettle on its powered base and keep your phone nearby. Connect your phone to the network you want the kettle to use.

Open the Halvard Home app, select ‘Add appliance’ and choose ‘Brio kettle’. Follow the prompts to put the kettle into pairing mode, then enter your wi-fi password. Keep the app open until it confirms the connection.

If pairing fails, check the password carefully and move the kettle closer to your router, keeping it on a dry, stable surface. Restart the app before trying again.
