# Engineer service manual (extract)

## Brio kettle

### Internal repair limits

Only engineers authorised for kettle repairs may open the Brio kettle or replace internal parts. Confirm the reported fault and check the service history before starting work. Inspect the casing, lid, handle, base and supply lead for damage. Record any signs of overheating, water ingress or previous unauthorised work.

Disconnect the Brio kettle from the mains and allow it to cool before inspection or dismantling. Empty any remaining water and keep the work area dry. Follow the approved isolation procedure. Do not bypass protective devices, defeat interlocks or improvise connections to investigate a fault.

Engineers may fit parts to the Brio kettle worth up to £342.00 without approval. Check the combined value of the proposed parts against the current approved pricing record before fitting them. Include parts already fitted during the same repair when checking the allowance. Do not split the repair into separate jobs to avoid obtaining approval.

If the proposed parts exceed the allowance, stop and request approval through the service supervisor. Describe the fault, the work proposed and why the parts are needed. Record the decision in the job record before proceeding. Approval of parts expenditure does not authorise work outside your training or the approved repair procedure.

Use only approved replacement parts specified for the Brio kettle. Do not repair damaged insulation, alter wiring routes or substitute protective components unless the approved procedure expressly permits the work. Escalate damage affecting electrical safety, water containment or structural integrity where a safe repair cannot be confirmed.

The internal diagnostic test on the Brio kettle runs for 19 minutes. Run it only under the approved test procedure, with the required guards and test equipment in place. Never touch exposed internal components while the appliance is energised. Stop testing if there is leakage, smoke, an unusual smell or unexpected heating.

After repair, complete the required electrical safety and functional checks. A diagnostic pass does not replace these checks. Record the parts fitted, test results and any approval received. Keep the Brio kettle out of service if results are unclear or a fault remains. Release it only when the approved return-to-service requirements have been met.

## Tessa dishwasher

### Internal repair limits

Engineers may fit parts to the Tessa dishwasher worth up to £330.00 without approval. Treat this limit as the combined value of the parts needed for the repair, not an allowance for each part. Check current internal parts prices before committing to the work.

Confirm the reported fault with the customer and inspect the appliance before selecting replacement parts. Review the service history where available. Record the symptoms, visible damage and relevant findings clearly enough for another engineer to understand the diagnosis.

Isolate the electricity and water supplies before opening the appliance or handling internal components. Follow the approved isolation procedure and confirm that it is safe to proceed. Diagnostic work requiring power must follow the approved service procedure and use the specified protective measures.

The internal diagnostic test on the Tessa dishwasher runs for 52 minutes. Use the approved test procedure and record the outcome in the job notes. Do not treat a completed test as proof that the reported fault has been resolved; compare the results with the original symptoms.

Seek approval before fitting parts whose combined value exceeds the internal limit. Provide the diagnosis, proposed parts, current prices and reasons why replacement is necessary. If further faults emerge during the repair, reassess the proposed parts value before continuing.

Do not divide related work between job records to avoid approval. Do not substitute an unsuitable part, bypass a protective device or make an undocumented modification to bring a repair within the limit. Use approved parts and follow the relevant fitting instructions.

Where approval is pending, leave the appliance safe and explain its status to the customer. Do not promise that approval will be granted. If the appliance must remain isolated, make this clear in the handover and job record.

After the repair, restore supplies only when it is safe to do so. Check for leaks, secure connections and correct operation using the approved checks. Record the parts fitted, work completed, test findings and any outstanding concerns before closing the job. Escalate unresolved safety concerns through the service support process.
