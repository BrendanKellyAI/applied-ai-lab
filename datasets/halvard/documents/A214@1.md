Saving energy with your Keel washing machine

Choose a 30°C wash for lightly soiled everyday clothes, provided their care labels allow it. Lower wash temperatures reduce the energy needed to heat the water.

If your Keel has an Eco programme, use it for suitable loads. It may take longer than a standard cycle, but a longer running time does not necessarily mean higher energy use.

For better results with less waste:

- Wash a full load where possible, without exceeding the programme’s load limit.
- Avoid prewash unless clothes are heavily soiled.
- Measure detergent according to the pack instructions and your water hardness.
- Select a higher spin speed, if fabric labels allow, to reduce drying time afterwards.

Check your model’s manual for available programmes and load limits.
