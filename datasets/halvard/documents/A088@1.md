Ashby tumble dryer door not closing

If your Ashby tumble dryer door will not close, switch the dryer off and unplug it before checking the opening. Remove any clothing trapped between the door and the seal, then wipe away fluff with a soft, dry cloth.

On this product, KX-7986 is the door catch that holds the door shut. A cracked catch can stop the door clicking into place, even when the opening is clear.

Check the catch for visible cracks or a loose edge. If it is damaged, do not force the door or try to hold it closed with tape. Contact Halvard Home support to arrange a replacement catch and fitting. Do not use the dryer until the door closes securely.
