Resetting your Ashby tumble dryer

On the Ashby tumble dryer, E-2194 means a factory reset was interrupted before the saved settings could be cleared. Your previous preferences may still be active.

To try the reset again:

1. Switch the dryer off, unplug it and wait five minutes.
2. Check that the drum is empty, then close the door.
3. Plug the dryer back in and switch it on without selecting a programme.
4. Press and hold the Temperature and Delay Start buttons together for ten seconds.
5. Release both buttons when the display shows “Reset”, then wait until the programme selection screen returns.

A completed reset clears saved drying preferences and delayed starts. If the warning returns, stop repeating the reset and contact Halvard Home support.
