Frost or condensation in your Tessa dishwasher

Condensation can form when warm, moist air touches cooler surfaces inside the dishwasher. A few droplets on the inner door after a wash are usually normal.

Once the cycle has finished and the dishes have cooled, unload the dishwasher and wipe the inner door with a soft, dry cloth. Leave the door slightly ajar, if safe to do so, to help moisture escape. Keep rinse aid topped up to support drying.

Frost or ice is not normal. If you find ice inside, do not start a programme. Switch the dishwasher off and contact Halvard Home support for advice before using it again. Do not use boiling water or a sharp tool to remove ice, as these can damage surfaces and seals.
