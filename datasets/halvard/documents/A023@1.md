Moving your Lumen smart thermostat

Before moving your Lumen smart thermostat, switch off power to the heating system at the mains. If you need to disconnect wiring, ask a qualified installer to remove and refit the unit.

KX-6505 is the replacement mounting backplate for this product. Check the backplate after transport: a cracked retaining clip can stop the thermostat sitting securely, causing the display to flicker as its contacts lose connection.

If a clip is cracked, do not tape the thermostat in place. Arrange for the backplate to be replaced before restoring power.

For transport, wrap the detached thermostat in soft padding and place it in a rigid box. Keep it dry, and avoid packing heavy objects on top.
