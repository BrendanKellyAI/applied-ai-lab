Corra induction hob heating slowly

If your Corra induction hob heats slowly or feels weak, E-6779 means restricted cooling airflow has triggered the hob’s protective power limit. The hob reduces heat output to prevent its internal electronics from overheating.

Switch off all cooking zones, then turn off the hob at its wall isolation switch. Leave it to cool for 20 minutes. Once cool, check that items in the cupboard or drawer beneath the hob are not blocking its ventilation openings. Move any obstructions away, but do not remove covers or reach inside the appliance.

Restore power and test one zone with an induction-compatible pan containing water. If heating remains weak or the warning returns, switch the hob off and contact Halvard Home support to arrange an inspection.
