Brio kettle carbon filter notice

Stop using carbon filters from batch 6304 with the Brio kettle, and return them for a refund.

Halvard Home is recalling the affected carbon filters. Please check the batch details printed on the filter packaging. If the packaging is missing or the batch details are unclear, contact our customer support team for advice before using the filter.

Keep the affected filters separate from your household supplies and arrange a return through Halvard Home customer support. Our team can explain the return process and help with refund enquiries.

We apologise for the inconvenience and thank you for helping us keep our products safe.
