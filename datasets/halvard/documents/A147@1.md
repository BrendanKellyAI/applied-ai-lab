Resetting your Mira robot vacuum

A factory reset removes your Mira’s saved Wi-Fi details, cleaning schedules and maps. You will need to set these up again afterwards. If you only want to resolve a temporary problem, try switching the vacuum off and on first.

To restore factory settings:

1. Place the vacuum on its charging dock and make sure the dock is plugged in.
2. Press and hold the Power and Home buttons together for 10 seconds.
3. Release both buttons when you hear the reset announcement.
4. Leave the vacuum on the dock while it restarts.

Once the restart is complete, open the Halvard Home app and add the vacuum again. Reconnect it to your Wi-Fi network before creating new maps or cleaning schedules.
