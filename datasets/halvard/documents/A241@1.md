Lumen smart thermostat: power cut during use

A power cut can interrupt your Lumen smart thermostat’s control of your heating. If the display goes blank, leave the thermostat in place and check whether other electrical appliances have also lost power.

Once power returns, allow 5 minutes before checking the thermostat. If it switches on, check the displayed time, target temperature and heating schedule, and correct anything that has changed.

If the thermostat appears offline in the app, check that your router has restarted and your home Wi-Fi is working.

Avoid a factory reset as your first troubleshooting step, as this may erase your settings. If the display remains blank after household power is restored, contact Halvard Home support. Do not open the thermostat or touch its wiring.
