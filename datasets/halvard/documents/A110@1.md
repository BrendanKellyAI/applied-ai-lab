Vale fridge freezer restart delay

On your Vale fridge freezer, E-9109 means the cooling restart delay timer is stuck, preventing the compressor from starting after a power interruption. This timer normally gives the compressor a short rest before cooling resumes.

To reset the timer, switch the appliance off at the wall and unplug it for five minutes. Plug it back in, switch it on and leave it undisturbed for ten minutes. Keep the doors closed while you wait.

If the warning remains or cooling does not restart, contact Halvard Home support. Do not keep switching the appliance off and on, or attempt to bypass the timer.

If food is warming, move it to another working fridge or freezer.
