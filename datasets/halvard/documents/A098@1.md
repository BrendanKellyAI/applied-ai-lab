Using Mira in a holiday home

If your holiday home has been left unheated, your Mira robot vacuum may display E-8250. This means the battery is too cold to charge safely, so charging has been paused.

Switch off the vacuum and unplug its charging base. Move both into a dry room at 15–25°C and leave them for at least two hours before reconnecting the base and placing Mira on it. Do not use a radiator, hairdryer or other direct heat source to warm the vacuum.

Once the warning clears, let Mira charge before starting a clean. Before leaving the property empty again, store it somewhere dry and frost-free. If the warning remains after warming and reconnecting, contact Halvard Home support.
