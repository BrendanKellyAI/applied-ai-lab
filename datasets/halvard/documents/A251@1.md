Tessa dishwasher: unusual vibration

An unusual vibration from your Halvard Home Tessa dishwasher can happen when dishes touch each other or a spray arm catches an item.

Pause the programme and wait for the spray to stop before opening the door slowly. Switch the dishwasher off before checking inside.

Check that plates, pans and cutlery are secure, with space between them. Turn each spray arm gently by hand: it should rotate freely without touching any dishes. Move anything that blocks it.

Check that the dishwasher stands firmly without rocking. Do not pull out a built-in appliance or remove its panels.

Restart the programme after adjusting the load. If strong vibration continues, stop using the dishwasher and contact Halvard Home support for advice.
