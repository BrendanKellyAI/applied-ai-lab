Descaling the Fenwick cooker hood

If your Fenwick cooker hood drips water from its lower edge, limescale may be blocking the drainage channel in its removable condensate tray. This can happen when hard water used during cleaning dries inside the channel.

KX-1737 is the replacement condensate tray for this product. Clean the existing tray first; replace it if it is cracked or still fails to drain after descaling.

Switch off the hood at the mains and let it cool. Remove the tray and soak it in equal parts white vinegar and warm water for 10 minutes. Clear loose deposits with a soft brush, then rinse and dry thoroughly before refitting.

Never pour descaler into the hood or wet its motor, lights or controls.
