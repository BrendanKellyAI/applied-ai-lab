Corra induction hob: frost or condensation

On the Corra induction hob, E-3293 means the moisture sensor has detected condensation beneath the control panel and stopped heating as a precaution. This can happen when warm, damp air meets a very cold hob.

Switch the hob off at its wall isolator and leave it for at least 30 minutes. Wipe visible surface moisture with a soft, dry cloth. If there is frost, let it melt naturally, then dry the glass. Do not use a hairdryer, hot water or a scraper to speed up thawing.

Restore power only when the surface is dry and frost-free. If the warning returns, switch off the hob and contact Halvard Home support. Do not remove the glass or attempt internal repairs.
