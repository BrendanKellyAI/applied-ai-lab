Ashby tumble dryer warranty

Your Halvard Home Ashby tumble dryer comes with a two-year warranty from the date of purchase. Keep your receipt or order confirmation, as you will need it to show when you bought the appliance.

The warranty covers faults caused by defective materials or workmanship during normal household use. It does not cover accidental damage, misuse or ordinary wear and tear.

If your dryer develops a fault within the warranty period, contact Halvard Home customer support before arranging a repair. Have your proof of purchase ready and describe what is happening. The support team will explain the next steps and confirm whether the repair is covered.

Your warranty does not affect your statutory rights.
