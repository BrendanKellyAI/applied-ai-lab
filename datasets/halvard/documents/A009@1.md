Pairing your Orrin heat pump with a voice assistant

If your voice assistant cannot find your Orrin heat pump, its voice pairing module may have stopped responding. This fault can prevent linking even when control through the Halvard Home app still works.

KX-3107 is the part number for the Orrin’s voice pairing module, not an error message. Try resetting its pairing connection before seeking a repair:

1. Open the Halvard Home app and select your heat pump.
2. Go to Settings, then Voice control, and select Reset pairing.
3. Wait 30 seconds, then select Pair voice assistant.
4. Follow the instructions to link your account again.

If pairing still fails, contact Halvard Home support. Do not open the heat pump or attempt to replace the module yourself.
