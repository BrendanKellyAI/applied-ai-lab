Fenwick cooker hood: interrupted firmware update

On the Fenwick cooker hood, E-3173 means the firmware download was interrupted before installation could begin. The hood keeps its existing firmware, so you can still use the fan and lights.

To try the update again:

1. Check that your home Wi-Fi is working and that the hood is connected.
2. Open the Halvard Home app and select your cooker hood.
3. Tap **Settings**, then **Firmware update**, then **Retry**.

Leave the hood connected to the mains throughout the update. Do not switch it off at the wall while installation is running.

If the download fails again, close and reopen the app, then retry once. If it still fails, contact Halvard Home support and describe the interrupted download.
