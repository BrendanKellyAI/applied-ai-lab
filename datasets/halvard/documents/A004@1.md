Moving your Brio kettle safely

Before moving your Brio kettle, unplug it, empty all water and let it cool completely. Lift the kettle off its base and dry both items before packing.

Check that the lid closes securely. A loose lid latch can allow the lid to spring open during transport. KX-2983 is the replacement lid latch for the Brio kettle. If the latch is cracked or the lid will not stay shut, stop using the kettle and contact Halvard Home support to arrange replacement. Do not tape the lid shut and continue boiling water.

Wrap the kettle and base separately in soft packing material. Keep both upright in a sturdy box, and never carry the kettle by its cable or lid.
