Frost or condensation on your Fenwick cooker hood

Condensation forms when warm, moist cooking air meets a cold surface. It can be more noticeable in winter, particularly if the extraction duct passes through an unheated space.

To reduce moisture:
- Switch the hood on when you start cooking and leave it running for 10 minutes afterwards.
- Use pan lids where practical and keep kitchen ventilation openings clear.
- Switch off the hood at the mains before wiping accessible surfaces dry with a soft cloth. Do not let water enter electrical controls.

If you see frost, do not chip it away or use direct heat to melt it. Ask a qualified installer to check the duct insulation and outside vent.

If water keeps dripping, stop using the hood and contact Halvard Home support.
