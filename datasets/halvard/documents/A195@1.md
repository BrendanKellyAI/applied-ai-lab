Updating your Keel washing machine firmware

Firmware updates help keep your Keel washing machine working smoothly. Before starting, finish any wash programme, empty the drum and leave the machine switched on.

Make sure your washing machine is connected to your home Wi-Fi. Open the Halvard Home app, select your Keel washing machine, then choose **Settings > Firmware update**. If an update is available, tap **Install** and follow the instructions.

Do not switch off the machine, unplug it or restart your router while the update is running. You cannot start a wash during installation.

Wait until the app confirms that the update is complete before using the machine. If installation fails, check your Wi-Fi connection and try again. Contact Halvard Home support if it still fails.
