Frost or condensation on your Keel washing machine

Condensation forms when warm, moist air meets a cold surface. If your Keel washing machine feels damp on the outside, wipe it with a soft, dry cloth and improve ventilation in the room. Check nearby hoses and connections: recurring puddles may indicate a leak rather than condensation.

If you notice frost, stop using the machine. Switch it off and unplug it with dry hands, provided the plug and socket are dry. Allow the room and machine to warm naturally before use. Do not use boiling water, a hairdryer or a portable heater to speed up thawing.

Once thawed, check for visible leaks. If frost returns or you find damaged hoses, contact Halvard Home support before running another wash.
