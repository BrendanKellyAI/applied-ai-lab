Unusual vibration from your Orrin heat pump

A new or unusually strong vibration can come from an object touching the unit, loose mounting points or an uneven base.

Switch the heat pump off using its normal controls before checking around it. Remove any loose items leaning against the casing, and check for leaves or debris around the outdoor unit. Do not remove covers, reach through the grille or adjust mounting bolts yourself.

If the area is clear and there is no visible damage, restart the heat pump and listen from a safe distance. If the vibration continues, switch it off and contact Halvard Home support or a qualified installer.

Keep the unit switched off if it shakes violently, moves on its base or makes a grinding noise.
