Mira robot vacuum: flashing warning light

A flashing warning light means your Mira robot vacuum needs attention. Start with these checks:

1. Switch the vacuum off and wait 30 seconds before inspecting it.
2. Remove and empty the dustbin, then fit it securely back into place.
3. Check the main brush and wheels for trapped hair, thread or other debris. Clear any blockages gently, without using sharp tools.
4. Wipe the sensors with a clean, dry cloth.
5. Place the vacuum on a clear, level area of floor, switch it on and try cleaning again.

If the light continues flashing, stop using the vacuum and contact Halvard Home support. Tell them when the flashing started and whether the vacuum still moves or charges.
