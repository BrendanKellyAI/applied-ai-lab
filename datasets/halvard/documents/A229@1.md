Recycling your old Ashby tumble dryer

Your old Halvard Home Ashby tumble dryer should be recycled as electrical waste, not placed in your household rubbish bin.

Before collection or transport, switch it off at the wall and unplug it. Allow it to cool completely, then empty the water container if your model has one. Remove any laundry and secure the power cable so it cannot trail during handling.

Contact your local council to find a recycling centre that accepts tumble dryers or to arrange a bulky electrical waste collection. Check booking requirements and any charges before travelling.

If you are buying a replacement, ask the retailer whether it offers collection and recycling of your old appliance. Do not attempt to lift or move the dryer alone.
