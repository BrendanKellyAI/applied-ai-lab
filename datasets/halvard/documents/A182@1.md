Resetting your Brio kettle

A factory reset returns your Brio kettle’s saved settings to their original values. Use it if you want to clear your preferences and start again.

Before you begin, make sure the kettle has finished boiling and cooled down. Do not attempt a reset while it is heating.

1. Place the kettle securely on its base.
2. Plug the base into a wall socket and switch the socket on.
3. Press and hold the power button for 10 seconds.
4. Release the button when the indicator light flashes twice. This confirms that the reset is complete.

You can now choose your preferred settings again. If the light does not flash, check that the kettle is seated correctly, then repeat the steps.
