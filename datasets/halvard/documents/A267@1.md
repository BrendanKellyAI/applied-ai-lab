Saving energy with your Brio kettle

Use your Brio kettle’s temperature setting to heat water only as much as you need. For green tea, select 80°C rather than boiling the water to 100°C. Follow the drink manufacturer’s brewing advice if it recommends a different temperature.

Switch off the keep-warm setting when you do not need hot water straight away. This stops the kettle using extra energy to maintain the selected temperature after heating.

Before filling, measure the water you need using your cup, then check that the level is between the kettle’s minimum and maximum marks. Never heat less than the minimum amount.

Descale regularly, especially in hard-water areas, to help the kettle heat efficiently.
