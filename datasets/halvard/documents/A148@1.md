Recycling your old Orrin heat pump

Your old Orrin heat pump must be collected separately from household rubbish. Do not put it in your household recycling bin.

Arrange for a qualified heat pump installer to disconnect the unit and safely recover its refrigerant before recycling. Do not cut pipes, open the refrigerant circuit or dismantle the unit yourself.

Ask your replacement installer whether they offer removal and recycling. Alternatively, contact your local council to find a recycling service that accepts heat pumps. Check acceptance and collection arrangements before moving the unit, as not every recycling centre can take it.

Keep the disconnected unit upright and away from children while awaiting collection. Use a licensed waste carrier if you arrange transport separately.
