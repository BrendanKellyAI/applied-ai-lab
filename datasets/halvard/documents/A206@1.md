Tessa dishwasher display stays blank

If your Tessa dishwasher’s display stays blank, start by checking its power supply.

Make sure your hands are dry, then check that the plug is fully inserted and the wall socket is switched on. If the plug is behind the dishwasher, do not pull the appliance out to reach it.

Switch the dishwasher off at an accessible wall socket for 60 seconds, then switch it back on and press the power button.

If the display is still blank, check whether other appliances have also lost power. A tripped circuit breaker can interrupt the dishwasher’s supply.

If power is available but the display remains blank, contact Halvard Home support. Do not remove any panels or attempt internal electrical repairs.
