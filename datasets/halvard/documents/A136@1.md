Ordering a spare accessory

To order a spare accessory for your Halvard Home Corra induction hob, first find the full model name on your purchase paperwork or appliance label. Use this information when contacting Halvard Home customer support so they can help check compatibility.

Take one clear photo of the accessory you want to replace, if you still have it. Include the photo, model details and a short description of what you need in your enquiry. If the accessory is missing, describe its purpose instead.

Before confirming your order, ask about the price, delivery charge and expected delivery time. Check that the accessory is suitable for your exact hob model rather than relying on its appearance alone.
