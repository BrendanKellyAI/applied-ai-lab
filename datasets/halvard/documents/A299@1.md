Water leaking under your Tessa dishwasher

If water is leaking underneath your Tessa dishwasher, stop the programme and turn off the water supply using the dishwasher’s isolation valve. Switch off the electricity only if you can reach the switch safely without standing in water or touching wet surfaces.

Once the area is safe, wipe up the water to prevent slips. Check the door seal for trapped food and clean it with a damp cloth. Look for visible drips from accessible hose connections, but do not pull out the dishwasher or remove panels.

Use only detergent made for dishwashers. Washing-up liquid can create excessive foam and cause leaks.

Do not run another cycle while the leak remains unexplained. Contact Halvard Home support to arrange an inspection if the source is unclear.
