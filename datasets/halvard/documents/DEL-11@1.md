Mira wall bracket safety notice

Stop using wall brackets from batch 9503 with the Mira robot vacuum, and return them for a refund.

Halvard Home is recalling these brackets because they may not hold the vacuum securely. A falling vacuum could cause injury or damage.

Check the batch marking on your bracket or its packaging. If the marking matches, remove the vacuum carefully and keep it on the floor, away from walkways. Do not refit the bracket.

Contact Halvard Home customer support for return instructions. If you cannot find the batch marking, please contact us before using the bracket again.
