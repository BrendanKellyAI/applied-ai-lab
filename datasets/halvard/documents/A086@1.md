Recycling your Brio kettle

On the Halvard Home Brio kettle, E-6770 means the internal electrical insulation has failed, making the kettle unsafe to use. Stop using it immediately and unplug it from the wall. Do not switch it on again or try to open the casing.

If you are retiring the kettle, let it cool completely, then empty out any water. Take both the kettle and its power base to a recycling centre that accepts small electrical appliances. Keep the cable attached.

Do not put the kettle in your household rubbish or mixed recycling bin. Your local council can tell you where to find an electrical recycling collection point. Check opening times and whether you need to book before travelling.
