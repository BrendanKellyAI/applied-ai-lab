Tessa dishwasher: power cut during use

A power cut stops the wash cycle because your Tessa dishwasher needs electricity to run its pump. Leave the door closed during the cut, as the water and dishes may still be hot.

When power returns:

- Check that the dishwasher’s wall socket is switched on.
- Look at the controls to see whether the programme has resumed or needs restarting.
- If washing resumes, leave the door closed and let the programme finish.
- If the programme has ended before the dishes are clean, select a new wash programme.

If the dishwasher remains unresponsive, switch it off at the wall and contact Halvard Home support. If you notice leaking water or a burning smell, stop using it.
