Burning smell from your Vale fridge freezer

KX-2645 is the compressor start relay in your Vale fridge freezer. Overheated contacts inside this part can cause a burning smell near the back of the appliance.

Stop using the fridge freezer immediately. Switch it off at the wall and unplug it only if the plug and socket are safe to touch. Do not restart it to check whether the smell has gone.

Contact Halvard Home support to arrange an inspection by a qualified technician. The relay may need replacing; do not remove covers or attempt the repair yourself.

Keep the doors closed while arranging alternative cold storage for your food. If you see smoke or flames, move away, leave the property and call 999.
