Pairing your Corra hob with a voice assistant

Pair your Corra induction hob in the Halvard Home app before linking it to your voice assistant.

1. Connect your phone to your home Wi-Fi and open the Halvard Home app.
2. Select **Add appliance**, choose your hob and follow the pairing instructions.
3. Open your voice assistant’s app and find its device or service linking menu.
4. Search for **Halvard Home**, then sign in with the same account you used to register the hob.
5. Allow the assistant to discover devices, then give the hob a simple name, such as “Kitchen hob”.

If the hob does not appear, check that it is online in the Halvard Home app, then repeat device discovery.
