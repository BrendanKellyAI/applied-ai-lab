Corra travel case notice

Halvard Home is asking customers to check the batch details on their Corra travel case.

Stop using travel cases from batch 4055 with the Corra induction hob, and return them for a refund.

If you have an affected case, keep it separate from your hob and contact Halvard Home customer support for help arranging the return. Please have your purchase details ready if available. If the batch label is missing or unclear, stop using the case until customer support can advise you.

This notice concerns the travel case, not the hob itself. We are sorry for the inconvenience and appreciate your help.
