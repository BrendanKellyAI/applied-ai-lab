Ashby tumble dryer: child lock

On the Ashby tumble dryer, E-3685 means the child lock cannot be released because the dryer has detected a control button being held down. This can happen if an object presses against the control panel or a button sticks.

To clear the fault:

1. Switch the dryer off and unplug it. Wait two minutes.
2. Remove anything touching the controls. Wipe the panel with a soft, dry cloth and check that no button is stuck. Do not force any button.
3. Reconnect the power and switch the dryer on.
4. Press and hold the child lock button for three seconds to unlock the controls.

If the message returns, leave the dryer switched off and contact Halvard Home support.
