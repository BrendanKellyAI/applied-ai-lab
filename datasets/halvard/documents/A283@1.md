# Mira robot vacuum display stays blank

If your Mira robot vacuum’s display stays blank, first check that its charging dock is plugged into a working wall socket and switched on.

Place the vacuum on the dock, making sure its charging contacts line up with those on the dock. Leave it to charge for 30 minutes, then press the vacuum’s power button.

If the display still does not light up, unplug the dock and wipe the charging contacts on both the vacuum and dock with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use water or cleaning spray. Reconnect the dock and try charging again.

If there is still no response, contact Halvard Home support. Do not open the vacuum or attempt electrical repairs.
