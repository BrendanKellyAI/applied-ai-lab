Strange noises from your Vale fridge freezer

Some sounds are normal during use. A gentle hum comes from the compressor, while gurgling can occur as refrigerant moves through the cooling system. An occasional click can happen when cooling starts or stops.

If you hear rattling, check that bottles and containers are not touching each other. Move them apart, then check that shelves and drawers are seated properly.

Vibration may mean the appliance is touching nearby furniture or standing unevenly. Check that it is stable before adjusting the feet as described in your user manual.

If the noise is new, persistent or sounds like loud grinding, switch the appliance off and contact Halvard Home support. Do not remove any covers.
