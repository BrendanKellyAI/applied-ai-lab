Recycling your Fenwick cooker hood

E-6292 means your Fenwick cooker hood has detected an electrical insulation fault in its fan motor. Stop using the hood and switch off its electricity supply. Have a qualified electrician assess the fault before deciding whether to repair or recycle the unit.

If you choose recycling, ask the electrician to disconnect any fixed wiring and make the supply safe before removal. Do not cut cables or dismantle the motor yourself.

Take the old hood to a household waste recycling centre that accepts electrical appliances, or ask your replacement appliance retailer about collection. Check acceptance and booking requirements before travelling.

Do not put the hood in your general rubbish bin. Keep it dry and securely stored until collection or transport.
