Ordering a spare Mira side brush

If your Mira robot vacuum leaves crumbs along skirting boards, check the side brush. Bent or broken bristles can stop it sweeping dirt into the suction path.

For the Mira robot vacuum, KX-3542 is the part number for the replacement side brush. To order one, contact Halvard Home customer support and quote this part number. Say that you need a side brush for a Mira robot vacuum, and provide your delivery address. Ask support to confirm the price and availability before paying.

Switch the vacuum off before inspecting the brush. Remove any tangled hair first: if the bristles are intact and the brush turns freely afterwards, you may not need a replacement.
