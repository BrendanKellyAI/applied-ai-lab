Connecting your Corra hob to Wi-Fi

Connect your Corra induction hob to a 2.4 GHz home Wi-Fi network. A 5 GHz-only network will not work.

Before starting, make sure the hob is switched on, all cooking zones are off and your phone is connected to the home network.

Open the Halvard Home app, choose **Add appliance**, then select **Corra induction hob**. Follow the on-screen instructions to put the hob into pairing mode. Select your home network and enter its Wi-Fi password exactly as shown on your router or network details.

Keep your phone near the hob until the app confirms the connection.

If pairing fails, check the password and Wi-Fi signal, then restart the app and try again.
