Corra induction hob: display stays blank

If your Corra induction hob’s display stays blank, first check that its wall isolator switch is on. If the household circuit breaker has tripped, leave the hob switched off and arrange an electrical check.

With the power supply working normally, switch the hob off at the wall isolator for 60 seconds, then switch it back on.

KX-1377 is the Corra’s display control module. A failed power regulator inside this module can stop electricity reaching the display, leaving it completely blank.

If the display remains blank after one reset, stop using the hob and contact Halvard Home support to arrange inspection and replacement of the module if needed. Do not lift the hob or attempt internal repairs yourself.
