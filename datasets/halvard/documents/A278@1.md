Cleaning your Orrin heat pump filter

A clean filter helps your Orrin heat pump maintain airflow and work efficiently. Check the filter once a month, or more often if your home is dusty or you have pets.

Before cleaning, switch off the heat pump and isolate its power supply. Wait until the fan has stopped.

Open the user-accessible filter panel and gently remove the filter. Do not remove fixed covers. Use a vacuum cleaner with a soft brush attachment to lift dust from both sides without pressing hard.

Do not wash the filter unless it is marked as washable. Replace it if it is torn or damaged.

Refit the filter securely, close the panel, then restore power. Never run the heat pump without its filter.
