Energy-saving settings

On the Fenwick cooker hood, E-9590 means the energy-saving auto-off timer is disabled, so the fan will not stop automatically.

To restore it, stop the fan, open Settings and select Energy saving. Switch Auto-off to On and choose 10 minutes. The hood will then stop the fan 10 minutes after you activate the run-on function.

For everyday cooking, use the lowest fan speed that clears steam and smells. Only select Boost for heavy steam or smoke, then return to a lower speed once the air clears. Switch the hood light off when you finish cooking.

If the warning remains after enabling Auto-off, stop the fan manually after use and contact Halvard Home support.
