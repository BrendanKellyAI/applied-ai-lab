Updating your Corra induction hob

Keep your Corra induction hob up to date using the Halvard Home app. Before starting, switch off all cooking zones, remove any pans and let the surface cool. Leave the hob connected to its electrical supply.

1. Open the Halvard Home app and select your Corra hob.
2. Go to **Settings**, then **Firmware update**.
3. If an update is available, tap **Install** and follow the instructions shown.

Do not use the hob or switch off its power while the update is running. Wait until the app confirms that installation is complete before cooking again.

If the update does not start, check that your phone and hob are connected to Wi-Fi, then try again. Contact Halvard Home support if the problem continues.
