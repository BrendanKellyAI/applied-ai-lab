Keel washing machine: fault on the display

If your Halvard Home Keel washing machine shows a fault message, stop the programme and make a note of exactly what appears on the display.

Switch the machine off and unplug it for five minutes before reconnecting it. Only handle the plug with dry hands. If the socket is wet or unsafe to reach, do not touch it.

Check that the door is fully closed and that no clothing is trapped in the seal. Do not force the door open if water remains inside.

If the fault returns, stop using the machine and contact Halvard Home support. Share the display message and explain when it appeared, such as during filling, washing or draining.
