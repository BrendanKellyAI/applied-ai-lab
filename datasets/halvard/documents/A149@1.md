Ashby tumble dryer not switching on

If your Ashby tumble dryer will not switch on, start with the power supply.

Check that the plug is fully inserted and the wall socket is switched on. With dry hands, unplug the dryer and test the socket with a lamp you know works. If the lamp stays off, the socket or household power supply may need attention.

If the socket works, leave the dryer unplugged for 60 seconds before reconnecting it. Close the door firmly, then try switching it on again.

Stop using the dryer if the plug or cable looks damaged, smells burnt or feels unusually hot. Do not remove any panels.

If it still will not switch on, contact Halvard Home support.
