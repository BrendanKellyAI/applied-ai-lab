Outdoor fan grille notice

Stop using outdoor fan grilles from batch 7264 with the Ashby tumble dryer, and return them for a refund.

Halvard Home is asking customers to check outdoor fan grilles supplied for use with the Ashby tumble dryer. Please look for the batch label on your grille or its packaging to confirm whether it is affected. If the label is missing, damaged or difficult to read, contact Halvard Home customer support for advice. Do not fit or reuse an affected grille. Customer support can explain how to return the grille and arrange your refund. Please keep the packaging if you still have it. We apologise for the inconvenience and appreciate your cooperation.
