Burning smell from your Lumen smart thermostat

A burning smell is not normal. Stop using your Lumen smart thermostat and switch off the power to your heating system at its mains isolator, but only if you can reach it safely. Turning the temperature down does not disconnect the electrical supply.

Do not touch the thermostat if it feels hot, looks scorched or is making crackling sounds. Do not remove its cover, attempt repairs or turn the power back on to test it.

If you see smoke or flames, leave the property and call 999. Do not use water on electrical equipment.

Keep the system switched off until a qualified electrician has checked it. Contact Halvard Home support for advice about the thermostat once any immediate danger has been dealt with.
