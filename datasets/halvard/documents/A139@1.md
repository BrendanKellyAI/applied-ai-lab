Brio kettle warranty period

Your Halvard Home Brio kettle comes with a two-year warranty from the date of purchase. For example, if you bought your kettle on 10 June 2026, the warranty period ends on 10 June 2028.

Keep your receipt or order confirmation, as you will need proof of the purchase date when making a warranty claim.

If your kettle develops a fault during this period, contact Halvard Home customer support. Describe the problem and have your proof of purchase ready so the team can check your claim and explain the next steps.

The warranty period alone does not confirm whether a particular fault is covered. Your statutory rights are not affected by the warranty.
