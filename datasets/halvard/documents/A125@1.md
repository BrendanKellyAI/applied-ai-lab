Ashby tumble dryer: fault code on the display

If your Ashby tumble dryer shows a fault code, stop the programme and note exactly what appears on the display.

Switch the dryer off at the wall and unplug it. Wait 5 minutes before reconnecting it. While it is unplugged and cool, remove fluff from the lint filter and check that any accessible air vents are clear. If your dryer has a water container, empty it and refit it securely.

Reconnect the dryer and try starting a programme once. If the code returns, stop using the appliance and contact Halvard Home support with the displayed code and your model details.

Do not remove panels or attempt internal repairs. If you notice smoke or a burning smell, do not restart the dryer.
