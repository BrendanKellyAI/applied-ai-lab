Vale fridge freezer door not closing

If your Vale fridge freezer door will not close fully, check for anything blocking it before trying again. Do not force the door shut.

- Move food, bottles and packaging away from the front edge of each shelf.
- Push drawers fully into place and check that shelves sit correctly in their supports.
- Wipe the rubber door seal and the surface it touches with a soft cloth and warm water, then dry both.
- Look for ice build-up stopping the freezer door from closing. Do not chip ice away with a sharp tool.

If the door still hangs unevenly or the seal is split, contact Halvard Home support. Keep the door closed as much as possible while arranging help.
