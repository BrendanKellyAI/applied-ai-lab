Recycling your old Tessa dishwasher

Recycle your old Tessa dishwasher through an electrical appliance recycling service, rather than putting it in your household rubbish.

Before disconnecting it, switch it off at the mains, unplug it and close the water supply valve. If it is wired directly into the electrical supply, ask a qualified electrician to disconnect it.

Empty the dishwasher and drain any remaining water from the hoses into a shallow container. Keep the appliance upright when moving it, and get help: dishwashers are heavy.

Ask your local council where you can take large electrical appliances or whether it offers a collection service. If you are buying a replacement, ask the retailer about taking away the old unit. Confirm any charges and disconnection requirements before booking.
