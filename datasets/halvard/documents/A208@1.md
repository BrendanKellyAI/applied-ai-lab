# Frost or condensation on your Mira

Frost or condensation can form when your Mira robot vacuum moves from a cold space into a warm, humid room.

If you notice moisture, switch the vacuum off and unplug its charging dock, provided the plug and socket are dry. Do not charge or run the vacuum while it is damp.

Wipe the outside with a soft, dry cloth. Leave the vacuum and dock in a dry room for at least 24 hours, and longer if any moisture remains. Do not use a hairdryer, radiator or direct heat to speed up drying.

To help prevent build-up, store Mira indoors away from cold draughts. If moisture appears inside the vacuum or keeps returning, contact Halvard Home support before using it again.
