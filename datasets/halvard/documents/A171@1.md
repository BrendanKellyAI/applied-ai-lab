Strange noises from your Ashby tumble dryer

A new rattling, thumping or scraping noise can come from loose objects, an uneven load or the dryer touching nearby furniture.

Stop the programme, switch off the dryer and unplug it before checking. Let the drum cool, then remove the laundry and check for coins, keys or other loose items. Metal zips and buttons can also rattle against the drum.

Redistribute bulky items evenly before restarting. Check that the dryer stands firmly on a level floor and is not touching a wall or cupboard. Do not reach underneath or remove any panels.

If the noise continues, or you hear loud grinding, stop using the dryer and contact Halvard Home support. Describe the sound and when it happens during the programme.
