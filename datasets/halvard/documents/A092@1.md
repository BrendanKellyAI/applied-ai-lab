Burning smell from your Tessa dishwasher

On the Halvard Home Tessa dishwasher, E-9474 means the heater’s electrical connection has overheated. This can produce a burning plastic smell, and the dishwasher must not be used until it has been checked.

Stop the programme and switch the dishwasher off. If it is safe to do so, disconnect the power at the wall socket or the household circuit breaker. Do not touch a plug or socket that feels hot, looks damaged or is wet.

Do not restart the dishwasher, remove panels or attempt to repair the wiring. Contact Halvard Home support to arrange a qualified service visit, and describe the smell.

If you see smoke or flames, leave the area and call the fire service.
