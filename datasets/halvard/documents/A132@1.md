Updating your Ashby tumble dryer

Firmware updates help keep your Halvard Home Ashby tumble dryer running smoothly.

Before you begin, finish any drying programme and empty the drum. Leave the dryer switched on and connected to your home Wi-Fi.

To check for an update:

1. Open **Settings** on the dryer’s display.
2. Select **Software update**, then **Check for updates**.
3. If an update is available, select **Install** and follow the instructions on screen.

Do not switch off or unplug the dryer while installation is in progress. You cannot start a drying programme during the update.

Wait until the display confirms that installation is complete before using the dryer again. If the update fails, check your Wi-Fi connection and try again.
